# Unit 48: Financial mathematics and quantitative decisions

Rates, cash flows, amortization, and risk models make income, spending, borrowing, saving, and insurance decisions mathematically explicit.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct and compare compensation and budget models with deductions and consistent time units.
- Compare banking options using a complete fee model and specified transaction patterns.
- Calculate and compare savings growth under simple, periodic, and continuous compounding with consistent rates and time units.
- Use formulas and technology to construct and validate amortization tables and fixed-payment loan models.
- Analyze insurance coverage and rates through scenario costs and probability-weighted comparisons.

## Concepts and topics

Compensation and budgets; Taxes and piecewise rates; Bank-account cost models; Retail credit and delayed payment; Simple, compound, and effective rates; Annuities and investment options; Amortization tables; Housing and vehicle finance; Insurance costs and coverage; Weighted averages, ratings, and indices.
