## Unit safeguards

Every amount, tax bracket, rate, fee and contract is hypothetical or supplied explicitly for educational modeling. Do not present these scenarios as current laws, market returns, actual offers or personal recommendations. Preserve the payment timeline, valuation horizon, zero-rate cases, final rounding adjustments and terminal assets/debts. Compare risk and liquidity only using stated assumptions.
