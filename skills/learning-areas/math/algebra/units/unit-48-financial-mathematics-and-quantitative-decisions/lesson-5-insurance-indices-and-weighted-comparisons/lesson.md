# Lesson 48.5: Insurance, indices, and weighted comparisons

Weighted summaries and risk models support comparisons while making the weighting assumptions visible.

## Learning objectives

- Analyze insurance coverage and rates through scenario costs and probability-weighted comparisons.
- Calculate and critique weighted averages, published ratings, and indices for quantitative decisions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Apply deductibles and limits correctly, identify excluded outcomes, and distinguish expected cost from the possible size of an uninsured loss.
- Recover or state weights and scales, use a common baseline, and test sensitivity before treating rankings as intrinsic properties of the alternatives.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Insurance costs and coverage** | Premiums buy specified coverage subject to deductibles, limits, exclusions, and sometimes coinsurance. Total cost by loss scenario includes premium plus uncovered loss.
