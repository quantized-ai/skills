# Lesson 48.2: Banking, credit, and purchasing comparisons

Fees, balance rules, and payment timing determine the full cost of transaction and credit options.

## Learning objectives

- Compare banking options using a complete fee model and specified transaction patterns.
- Model and compare retail credit options by total cost, cash-flow timing, and outstanding balances.

## Proficiency criteria

- Include applicable waivers and recurring or per-event charges, identify break-even conditions, and state which usage assumptions support a preferred option.
- State interest convention and payment timing, include fees and rate changes, and distinguish the smallest immediate payment from the smallest total cost.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Bank-account cost models** | Account costs can include maintenance fees, balance-dependent waivers, overdraft protection, processing fees, and debit or ATM charges. Online access can change available fee schedules. Cost comparison requires a declared pattern of balances and transactions. | Compare banking options using a complete fee model and specified transaction patterns. | Include applicable waivers and recurring or per-event charges, identify break-even conditions, and state which usage assumptions support a preferred option. |
| **Retail credit and delayed payment** | Cash purchases, installment plans, and revolving credit differ in initial payment, finance charges, fees, and balance recurrence. Promotional rates may change after a stated date; minimum payments need not retire debt quickly. | Model and compare retail credit options by total cost, cash-flow timing, and outstanding balances. | State interest convention and payment timing, include fees and rate changes, and distinguish the smallest immediate payment from the smallest total cost. |
