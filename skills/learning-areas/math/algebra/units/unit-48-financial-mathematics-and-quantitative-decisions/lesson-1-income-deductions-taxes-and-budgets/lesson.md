# Lesson 48.1: Income, deductions, taxes, and budgets

Piecewise and linear models translate compensation rules and expenses into comparable cash flows.

## Learning objectives

- Construct and compare compensation and budget models with deductions and consistent time units.
- Calculate tax liabilities and effective rates from supplied rules and represent bracketed taxes as piecewise functions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Apply all thresholds and deduction rules, distinguish marginal from average rates, and reconcile totals over the same period before selecting among options.
- Distinguish the taxable base, marginal rates, total liability, and effective rate; maintain continuity where the stated brackets require it and apply credits and deductions in the correct order.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Compensation and budgets** | Hourly pay, salary, overtime, commission, and bonuses use rates with specified time units and thresholds. Gross income differs from net income after deductions; a budget reconciles receipts, fixed and variable expenses, savings, and a reporting period. | Construct and compare compensation and budget models with deductions and consistent time units. | Apply all thresholds and deduction rules, distinguish marginal from average rates, and reconcile totals over the same period before selecting among options. |
| **Taxes and piecewise rates** | A supplied marginal tax schedule applies each rate only to the income in its bracket; deductions change the taxable base and credits reduce tax according to their stated rules. Sales and property taxes use different bases. These are mathematical models using specified rules, not claims about current tax law. | Calculate tax liabilities and effective rates from supplied rules and represent bracketed taxes as piecewise functions. | Distinguish the taxable base, marginal rates, total liability, and effective rate; maintain continuity where the stated brackets require it and apply credits and deductions in the correct order. |
