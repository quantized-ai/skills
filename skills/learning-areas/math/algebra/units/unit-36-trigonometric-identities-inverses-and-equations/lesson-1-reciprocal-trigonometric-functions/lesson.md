# Lesson 36.1: Reciprocal trigonometric functions

Secant, cosecant, and cotangent complete the trigonometric function family through reciprocal and quotient relationships, with exclusions and graph behavior inherited from their denominators.

## Learning objectives

- Define secant, cosecant, and cotangent and analyze their exact values, identities, and graphs.
- Graph simple transformations with correct domains, periods, ranges, and vertical asymptotes.

## Proficiency criteria

- Preserve denominator restrictions in every identity and exact evaluation.
- Locate branches and asymptotes from sine, cosine, or tangent, and transform both coordinates and excluded inputs.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Reciprocal and quotient definitions** | $\sec x=1/\cos x$, $\csc x=1/\sin x$, and $\cot x=\cos x/\sin x$ for real radian inputs where their denominators are nonzero. Cotangent equals $1/\tan x$ only on the common domain, which excludes additional inputs where tangent is undefined. Exact values and signs follow from sine and cosine. | Define and evaluate the reciprocal trigonometric functions, distinguishing each natural domain from the domain of an alternative expression. | Record every exclusion, determine signs and exact values, and do not discard cotangent values merely because tangent is undefined. |
| **Reciprocal trigonometric graphs** | Secant has period $2\pi$, domain $x\ne\pi/2+k\pi$, and range $(-\infty,-1]\cup[1,\infty)$. Cosecant has period $2\pi$, domain $x\ne k\pi$, and the same range. Cotangent has period $\pi$, domain $x\ne k\pi$, and range $\mathbb R$. Here $k\in\mathbb Z$. Zeros of the defining denominator determine vertical asymptotes; reciprocal signs and magnitudes determine branches. | Construct secant, cosecant, and cotangent graphs from sine and cosine and justify their periods, ranges, zeros, and asymptotes. | Place branches on the correct sign intervals, locate cotangent zeros, identify the absence of secant and cosecant zeros, and justify each domain exclusion. |
| **Transformed reciprocal graphs** | For $y=A g(B(x-h))+D$, where $g$ is secant, cosecant, or cotangent and $A,B\ne0$, input values transform by $x=h+u/B$ and outputs by $y=Av+D$. Period is the parent period divided by $\lvert B\rvert$; asymptotes and branch endpoints transform with the same rule. Cotangent has no amplitude; secant and cosecant branch boundaries are not a sinusoidal amplitude. | Graph and analyze transformed secant, cosecant, and cotangent functions using coordinate and domain transformations. | Transform asymptotes, periods, ranges, intercepts, and representative branch points consistently, retaining exclusions under horizontal reflection. |
