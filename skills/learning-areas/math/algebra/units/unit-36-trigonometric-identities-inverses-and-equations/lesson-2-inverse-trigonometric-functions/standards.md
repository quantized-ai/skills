# Standards: Lesson 36.2 — Inverse trigonometric functions

Mappings for [Lesson 36.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement.
