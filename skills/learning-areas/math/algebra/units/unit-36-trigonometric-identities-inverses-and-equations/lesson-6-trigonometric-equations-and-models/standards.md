# Standards: Lesson 36.6 — Trigonometric equations and models

Mappings for [Lesson 36.6](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic periodic solution families** | Solve sine, cosine, and tangent equations with affine arguments as complete periodic families and as solution sets on specified intervals. | CCSS HSF-TF.B.7 (+) (Supporting knowledge: complete equation solutions); TEKS §111.42(c)(5)(N) (Direct: trigonometric equations). |
| **Algebraic and identity-based equation methods** | Solve trigonometric equations by factoring, substitution, and identities while identifying lost solutions, extraneous candidates, and special all-or-none cases. | TEKS §111.42(c)(5)(N) (Direct: solving trigonometric equations); CCSS HSF-TF.C.9 (+) (Direct when addition or subtraction formulas are used). |
| **Equations from periodic models** | Generate and solve trigonometric equations from periodic models, evaluate solutions with technology, and interpret all admissible events in context. | CCSS HSF-TF.B.7 (+) (Direct); TEKS §111.42(c)(5)(N) (Direct: mathematical and real-world equations); TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(O) (Direct: use of sinusoidal models). |
