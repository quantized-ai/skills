# Unit 53: Game theory and theory of moves

Strategic models compare simultaneous choices with outcome-dependent moves and countermoves.

## Main learning objectives

- Recognize strategic situations and represent zero-sum and partial-conflict games with payoff matrices.
- Determine pure and mixed optimal strategies and game values, including payoff-envelope methods when one player has two strategies.
- Identify and model prisoners’ dilemma structures in social situations and analyze their equilibria and inefficiency.
- Explain theory-of-moves rules and compare its predictions with Nash analysis in game trees and strict ordinal models of conflicts.

## Concepts and topics

Games, payoffs, and strategy sets; Best responses and equilibrium; Saddle points and pure-strategy value; Two-strategy mixing; Payoff envelopes with two strategies; Prisoners’ dilemma; Chicken and coordination over conflict; Rules of the theory of moves; Backward reasoning and comparison of predictions; Strict ordinal conflict models; Cyclic games and stopping assumptions.
