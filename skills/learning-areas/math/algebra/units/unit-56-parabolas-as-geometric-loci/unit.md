# Unit 56: Parabolas as geometric loci

The focus–directrix definition yields vertical and horizontal parabola equations and connects geometric parameters with quadratic relations.

## Main learning objectives

- Derive a vertical parabola from a focus and horizontal directrix. Recover focal attributes from a quadratic function.
- Derive and analyze horizontal parabola equations. Complete the square to recover focal form and verify equidistance.

## Concepts and topics

A parabola as an equidistance locus; Converting between vertex form and focal attributes; Horizontal focus/directrix equations; Recovering parabola attributes by square completion.

## Lessons

- [56.1: Focus, directrix, and vertical parabolas](lesson-1-focus-directrix-and-vertical-parabolas/lesson.md) — Define a parabola by equal distances from a focus and directrix, then derive its vertical form. Connect focal geometry to vertex-form coefficients and opening direction.
- [56.2: Horizontal parabolas and general equations](lesson-2-horizontal-parabolas-and-general-equations/lesson.md) — Extend the focus/directrix definition to horizontal parabolas and recover attributes from general equations. Distinguish these geometric relations from functions of the horizontal input.
