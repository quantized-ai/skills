# Lesson 49.5: Iteration, recursion, and algorithmic models

Discrete updates represent evolving systems and make an algorithm’s stopping and validity conditions explicit.

## Learning objectives

- Select and apply a recursive or iterative algorithm for a contextual model and communicate its results and limitations.
- Explain and evaluate an applied algorithm in mathematical and nontechnical language.

## Proficiency criteria

- State initial values, update order, units, stopping rule, and admissible states; verify early and boundary behavior and separate observed iteration from a general conclusion.
- Trace input-to-output meaning, identify its correctness or approximation claim, justify checks and stopping conditions, and document assumptions needed to reproduce the result.
