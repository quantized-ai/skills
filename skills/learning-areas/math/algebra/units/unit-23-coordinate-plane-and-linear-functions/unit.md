# Unit 23: Coordinate plane and linear functions

This unit connects ordered pairs, equations of lines, and constant rates of change, culminating in linear models and their transformations.

## Main learning objectives

- Represent linear equations as complete sets of coordinate-plane solutions and connect their slope and intercepts across representations.
- Construct equivalent equations of lines from points, rates, tables, graphs, and verbal relationships.
- Determine parallel and perpendicular lines, including horizontal and vertical exceptions.
- Interpret and transform linear functions with explicit continuous or discrete domains and contextual parameter meanings.

## Concepts and topics

Cartesian coordinates; graph membership; slope triangles; constant rate; intercepts; slope-intercept, standard, and point-slope forms; horizontal and vertical lines; parallel and perpendicular conditions; linear domains and ranges; transformations.
