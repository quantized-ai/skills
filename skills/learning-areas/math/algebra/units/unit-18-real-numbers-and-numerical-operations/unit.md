# Unit 18: Real numbers and numerical operations

This unit establishes the numerical language and arithmetic used throughout the catalog, from whole-number factors and signed fractions to powers of ten and justified approximations.

## Main learning objectives

- Classify, order, and represent real numbers and distinguish exact values from approximations.
- Compute with signed integers, fractions, decimals, and integer powers using valid operations and order conventions.
- Use factors, multiples, scientific notation, and estimation to express and check numerical results.
- Prove rational closure and justify the rational or irrational nature of mixed sums and products with their exceptions.

## Concepts and topics

Number sets; number lines; absolute value; signed arithmetic; divisibility; prime factorization; fractions; decimal representations; numerical exponents; operation order; scientific notation; precision. Rational closure and mixed rational–irrational arithmetic.
