# Lesson 18.3: Factors, multiples, and fraction arithmetic

Whole-number structure supports equivalent fractions and exact rational arithmetic.

## Learning objectives

- Use divisibility, prime factorizations, common factors, and common multiples.
- Represent equivalent fractions and compute exact fraction sums, differences, products, and quotients.

## Proficiency criteria

- Preserve nonzero denominators, use common denominators for addition, and cancel only common factors.
- Explain reciprocal division and verify results through equivalent forms or inverse operations.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Divisibility and prime factorization** | A whole number \(d\ne0\) divides \(n\) when \(n=dk\) for an integer \(k\). A prime integer greater than one has exactly two positive divisors; one is neither prime nor composite. Prime factorizations determine the greatest common factor and least common multiple of positive integers through shared and required prime powers. | Determine prime factorizations, greatest common factors, and least common multiples of positive integers. | Distinguish factors from multiples, retain repeated prime factors, and verify divisibility and minimality or maximality as appropriate. |
| **Equivalent fractions and fraction forms** | For \(b\ne0\) and \(k\ne0\), \(a/b=(ka)/(kb)\). A fraction can be reduced by dividing numerator and denominator by a common nonzero factor. Mixed numbers denote sums of an integer and a proper fraction; a leading negative sign applies to the whole mixed number. Denominators may be normalized to be positive. | Construct equivalent fractions, reduce rational numbers, and convert between mixed-number and improper-fraction forms. | Preserve the represented value and sign, distinguish common-factor reduction from canceling terms, and state why a zero denominator is excluded. |
| **Exact fraction operations** | Addition and subtraction require equivalent fractions with a common denominator. Products multiply numerators and denominators; division multiplies by the reciprocal of the divisor, which must be nonzero. For \(a/b\div c/d\), require \(b,c,d\ne0\). Rational arithmetic includes signed fractions and complex numerical fractions. | Compute and interpret the four operations on signed fractions, including mixed numbers and complex numerical fractions. | Use a valid common denominator for sums, preserve signs and divisor restrictions, simplify exactly, and verify division through the multiplication relationship. |
