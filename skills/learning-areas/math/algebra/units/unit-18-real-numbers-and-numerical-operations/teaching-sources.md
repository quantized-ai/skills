## Specific applications in the revised teaching plans

- [IES/WWC, Developing Effective Fractions Instruction overview](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/15) — consulted recommendations on fractions as numbers and explaining procedures (moderate evidence); overview only, not the complete guide.

The fractions overview motivates explaining numbers and operations before shortcuts. Here that becomes number-line ordering, common-unit fraction addition, reciprocal verification and explicit exact-versus-rounded notation. These original examples extend those general ideas to this unit’s rational/irrational and power scope; the source is not claimed to cover every extension.
