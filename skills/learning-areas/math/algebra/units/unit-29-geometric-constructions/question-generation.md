# Fresh-question generation: Unit 29: Geometric constructions

Read the selected `lesson.md` and `tutor.md` together with [agent-guide.md](agent-guide.md). Select the concept, curriculum proficiency component and intended evidence before choosing values.

## Generate, verify, vary

1. Choose a task family from the lesson-specific guidance below. Build the target case deliberately; do not hope random coefficients produce it.
2. Write complete givens, domains, units, requested representation, permitted tools and precision. Distinguish exact data from noisy or rounded measurements.
3. Solve privately, then verify independently by substitution, reconstruction, exact calculation or a second valid argument. For empirical work specify the design/model and do not invent a tool run. Reject ambiguous or malformed tasks before the student sees them.
4. Compare with the available task history. Change structure, representation, unknown, context or exceptional case as well as values. Match difficulty by reasoning demand rather than number size. Do not recycle worked examples or private-bank prompts as fresh assessment.
5. Present one manageable question and wait. Keep the key hidden until the answer is submitted. Hints convert that attempt to assisted practice; replace it later with independent evidence.

For every concept, sample ordinary cases and its required exceptions/methods. A lesson's reference anchor illustrates only part of its scope. Use each curriculum row's proficiency conditions as the coverage contract, and do not infer missing evidence from another concept's success.

## Lesson task families and constraints

### Lesson 29.1: Copying and bisecting

[Paired tutor](lesson-1-copying-and-bisecting/tutor.md). Require compass/straightedge and a second exact method (constraint-based dynamic geometry or justified folding), label every center/radius/intersection, avoid tangent/nonintersecting arcs and distinguish the intended internal ray.

**Quality check:** Reject or diagnose this error: Calling an approximate ruler/protractor match an exact construction.

### Lesson 29.2: Perpendiculars, parallels, and triangle existence

[Paired tutor](lesson-2-perpendiculars-parallels-and-triangle-existence/tutor.md). Include on/off-line incidence, another exact construction method, circle containment, external/internal tangency and strict triangle bounds; a failed approximate sketch is not evidence of impossibility.

**Quality check:** Reject or diagnose this error: Counting the two reflected vertices as two noncongruent SSS triangles.

### Lesson 29.3: Regular inscribed polygons

[Paired tutor](lesson-3-regular-inscribed-polygons/tutor.md). Vary circle placement/radius and starting point, require exact unmarked-straightedge/compass constraints and proofs of both equal sides and equal angles; do not generalize radius stepping to arbitrary polygons.

**Quality check:** Reject or diagnose this error: Using measured 60° or 90° angles as the sole exact-construction justification.

### Lesson 29.4: Triangle circles and exterior tangents

[Paired tutor](lesson-4-triangle-circles-and-exterior-tangents/tutor.md). Include obtuse triangles with exterior circumcenters, perpendicular inradius feet and both tangent points; require an actual construction trace or artifact before crediting construction execution.

**Quality check:** Reject or diagnose this error: Drawing the incircle with radius to a vertex or treating every line through a contact point as tangent.

## Constructive generation and independent validation

**Entry and routing.** Establish allowed instruments and whether a physical artifact, dynamic constraint trace or another exact realization is available. If no execution can be observed, teach/reason and mark construction execution not assessed.

**Build tasks deliberately.** Choose nondegenerate inputs and radii yielding the intended number of intersections. Specify which intersection or side of a ray to use where the result is not unique. For copied angles transfer both radius and chord. For perpendiculars use equal-distance points; for parallels state the angle or double-perpendicular criterion. For triangles include two-intersection, tangency and no-intersection cases. For exterior tangents deliberately vary exterior/on/interior point position.

**Verification record.** Record givens, instrument actions, dependencies, intersection choices and theorem justification. Inspect equal-radius constraints and the resulting incidence/tangency; dragging should preserve hypotheses, not merely approximate positions. Lessons 29.1/29.2 require a second exact realization: track it separately from compass/straightedge execution. Circumradius goes to a vertex; inradius is perpendicular to a side.

**Completion gate.** Distinguish describing, performing, validating and proving a construction. Measurement alone does not establish exactness, and a student cannot be penalized for unavailable tools by having unobserved execution fabricated.
