# Unit 29: Geometric constructions

Exact constructions use geometric constraints to create and justify figures. Compass-and-straightedge methods are compared with another exact geometric method, and extended to polygons, triangle circles, and tangents.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct and justify congruent segments and angles, bisectors, perpendiculars, and parallels with compass and straightedge and another geometric tool.
- Use constructions to investigate relationships and triangle existence.
- Construct regular inscribed polygons, triangle incircles and circumcircles, and tangents from an exterior point.

## Concepts and topics

Unmarked straightedge and compass, equal-distance arcs, paper-folding or dynamic constructions, segment and angle copying, perpendicular and angle bisectors, parallels, triangle inequalities, equilateral triangles, squares, hexagons, incircles, circumcircles, and exterior tangents.
