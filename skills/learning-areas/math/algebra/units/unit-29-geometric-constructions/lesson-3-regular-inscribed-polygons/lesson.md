# Lesson 29.3: Regular inscribed polygons

Equal central angles and equal chords support exact constructions of three regular polygons.

## Learning objectives

- Construct an equilateral triangle, a square, and a regular hexagon inscribed in a circle.

## Proficiency criteria

- Place every vertex on the given circle and justify both equal side lengths and equal angles.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Equilateral triangle, square, and hexagon** | A radius stepped as a chord partitions a circle into six equal arcs and gives a regular hexagon; alternate vertices give an equilateral triangle. Two perpendicular diameters give an inscribed square. Justification uses equilateral central triangles, congruent chords, equal central angles, and perpendicular diameters. | Construct and justify an equilateral triangle, a square, and a regular hexagon inscribed in a given circle. | Use exact compass-and-straightedge constraints, close the hexagon without measured-angle approximations, and establish regularity of all three figures. |
