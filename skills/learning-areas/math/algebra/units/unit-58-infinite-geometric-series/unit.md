# Unit 58: Infinite geometric series

Limits of geometric partial sums define infinite accumulation and quantify the error of finite approximations.

## Main learning objectives

- Determine convergence and derive and evaluate an infinite geometric sum with all ratio and zero-term exceptions.
- Represent repeating decimals and repeated proportional totals as convergent geometric series.

## Concepts and topics

Partial sums and convergence; Remainders and required term counts; Repeating decimals and accumulation; Finite versus infinite financial models.

## Lessons

- [58.1: Convergence of geometric series](lesson-1-convergence-of-geometric-series/lesson.md) — Infinite sums are limits of partial sums and require conditions absent from finite-sum formulas.
- [58.2: Infinite accumulation models](lesson-2-infinite-accumulation-models/lesson.md) — A convergent idealization can represent repeated proportional contributions while retaining the distinction from a finite process.
