## Constructive generation and independent validation

**Entry and routing.** Check rigid motions, corresponding angles/sides, proportions and positive roots. If correspondence is uncertain, stop numerical cross-multiplication and write the ordered triangle names first.

**Build tasks deliberately.** Generate dilation tasks from a positive scale and explicit center, with k=1 and through-center line cases. Build similar triangles by scaling valid triples, then create a nonexample by changing one required ratio or included angle. For proportionality distinguish whole-side and remainder lengths. Angle-bisector tasks must use adjacent-side ratios. Right-triangle tasks can be constructed from positive hypotenuse segments p,q, then c=p+q,h=√pq and adjacent legs √cp,√cq; verify independently via a²+b²=c².

**Verification record.** Confirm nondegeneracy, correspondence and theorem hypotheses before calculating. Inverse mixed transformations reverse operation order. Derive geometric-mean formulas from ordered similarities; special-triangle ratios require opposite-angle labels. Converse proofs need their own argument rather than repeating the forward theorem.

**Completion gate.** Track transformation properties, similarity criteria, theorem derivations and numerical applications separately. An invariant line under dilation is not pointwise fixed, and one memorized special-triangle ratio does not demonstrate the other family or the Pythagorean converse.
