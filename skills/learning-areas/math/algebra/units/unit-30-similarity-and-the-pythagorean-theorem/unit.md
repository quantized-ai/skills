# Unit 30: Similarity and the Pythagorean theorem

Dilations and rigid motions define similarity and explain proportional relationships. Triangle similarity proves proportionality theorems and establishes right-triangle metric relationships.

## Main learning objectives

- Verify dilation properties, construct similarity transformations, and distinguish uniform from nonuniform scaling.
- Establish triangle similarity criteria and prove and apply proportionality theorems.
- Prove the Pythagorean theorem using similarity and apply its converse, right-triangle altitude relationships, and special-triangle ratios.

## Concepts and topics

Dilations, scale factors, centers, mixed compositions, corresponding angles and sides, AA/SAS/SSS similarity, triangle proportionality and converse, angle bisectors, geometric means, Pythagorean theorem and converse, triples, and special right triangles.
