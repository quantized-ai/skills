# Lesson 37.4: Polar complex multiplication and division

Polar form turns complex multiplication into scaling and rotation, and division into reciprocal scaling and subtraction of angles.

## Learning objectives

- Derive polar multiplication and division rules and use their geometric meaning in computation.
- Explain the effects of conjugation and reciprocation on modulus and argument.

## Proficiency criteria

- Require nonzero divisors, combine moduli and arguments correctly, and normalize angles only after preserving their represented points.
- Check results using rectangular form or geometric invariants.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplication as scaling and rotation** | Writing $\operatorname{cis}\theta=\cos\theta+i\sin\theta$, the addition formulas give $(r\operatorname{cis}\alpha)(s\operatorname{cis}\beta)=rs\operatorname{cis}(\alpha+\beta)$. Multiplication by a fixed nonzero complex number scales all distances from the origin by its modulus and rotates by its argument. Multiplication by zero collapses every point to zero. | Derive the polar product formula and use scaling and rotation to represent and calculate complex products. | Connect the expansion to sine and cosine addition, multiply moduli and add angles, and distinguish a nonzero rotation-scaling from multiplication by zero. |
| **Division, reciprocation, and conjugation** | For $s>0$, $(r\operatorname{cis}\alpha)/(s\operatorname{cis}\beta)=(r/s)\operatorname{cis}(\alpha-\beta)$. A nonzero reciprocal has modulus $1/r$ and argument $-\alpha$; conjugation preserves modulus and negates the argument modulo $2\pi$. These rules agree with division by multiplying numerator and denominator by the conjugate. | Compute complex quotients and reciprocals in polar form and explain their agreement with conjugate-based rectangular division. | Exclude zero divisors, divide moduli and subtract arguments in the correct order, and verify the result by multiplication or rectangular conversion. |
