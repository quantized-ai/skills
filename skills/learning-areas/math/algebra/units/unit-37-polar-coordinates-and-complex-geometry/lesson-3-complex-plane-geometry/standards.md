# Standards: Lesson 37.3 — Complex-plane geometry

Mappings for [Lesson 37.3](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Geometric arithmetic and conjugation** | Represent addition, subtraction, conjugation, and multiplication by the imaginary unit geometrically and use those interpretations to compute complex numbers. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.5 (+) (Direct: geometric operations). |
| **Distance and midpoint in the complex plane** | Calculate distances and midpoints of complex points and interpret fixed-distance loci geometrically. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.6 (+) (Direct: distance and midpoint; Extension: fixed-distance loci). |
| **Polar form and argument** | Convert complex numbers between rectangular and polar forms and explain their equivalence, including real, imaginary, and zero cases. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.4 (+) (Direct). |
