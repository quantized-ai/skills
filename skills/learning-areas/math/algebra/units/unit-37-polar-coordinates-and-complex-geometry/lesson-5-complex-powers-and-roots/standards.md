# Standards: Lesson 37.5 — Complex powers and roots

Mappings for [Lesson 37.5](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **De Moivre powers** | Derive De Moivre’s formula from polar multiplication and apply it to exact positive, zero, and negative integer powers on their valid domains. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.5 (+) (Direct: powers as geometric multiplication; Extension: general integer-power formula). |
| **Complete sets of complex roots** | Determine and verify every complex nth root, including roots of unity, and explain their spacing and multiplicity distinctions. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.5 (+) (Extension: roots reverse geometric multiplication); CCSS HSN-CN.C.9 (+) (Supporting knowledge: root count with multiplicity). |
