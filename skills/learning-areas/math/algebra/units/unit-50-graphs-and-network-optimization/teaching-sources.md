## Application and limits

The 4 curriculum/tutor pairs in [SKILL.md](SKILL.md#lessons) retain the existing curriculum as the scope. Each tutor concept has a separate locally authored diagnostic and worked model, a first hint, a misconception to investigate, a task family and an assessment coverage checklist. Examples and wording are original; no source exercise bank, card set, diagram or extended passage is reproduced.

Worked-example discussion, explanation of assumptions, revision after feedback and transfer across representations are instructional choices. Individual AI interaction is an adaptation, not a replication of classroom research. The evidence labels, two-independent-task convention and fresh-question policy are local design choices; they are not validated thresholds from these sources.

The source consultation and static mathematical/link checks do not establish live tutor reliability, learning gains, retention or successful host-environment activation. Actual technology, data collection and communication performance must be observed when required. Existing standards mappings are retained; these textbooks and teaching guides are not official standards and no new standards alignment is claimed.

## Specific applications in these lessons
