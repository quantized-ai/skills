# Unit 50: Graphs and network optimization

Vertex-edge models distinguish route inspection, tours, connecting networks, and precedence relationships.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct and interpret graph and directed-graph models and determine vertex degrees.
- Determine and construct Euler trails or circuits and justify existence from degree and connectivity conditions.
- Distinguish Hamiltonian tour and Euler inspection models and formulate an appropriate network objective.
- Use Kruskal’s algorithm to find and justify a minimum-cost spanning tree.

## Concepts and topics

Vertices, edges, degrees, and direction; Walks, paths, circuits, and connected components; Euler trails and circuits; Eulerization and inspection cost; Hamiltonian circuits and tour models; Nearest-neighbor and cheapest-link heuristics; Minimum-cost spanning trees; Critical path in a precedence network.
