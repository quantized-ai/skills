# Standards: Lesson 41.3 — Parametric motion models

Mappings for [Lesson 41.3](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Constant-velocity motion** | Construct constant-velocity parametric models and solve position, meeting-time, and collision conditions with a common time parameter. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(C) (Direct: parametric models); CCSS HSN-VM.A.3 (+) (Direct: velocity models). |
| **Uniform circular motion** | Build circular-motion parametrizations and relate angular displacement, angular velocity, linear speed, direction, and period. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(C) (Direct: parametric models); TEKS §111.42(c)(4)(D) (Direct: linear and angular velocity). |
| **Idealized projectile motion** | Construct and analyze idealized projectile parametrizations to determine admissible flight times, heights, ranges, and trajectories. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(C) (Direct: mathematical and real-world parametric models); TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(B) (Direct when eliminating the time parameter). |
