# Unit 41: Parametric relations and motion

Parametric equations describe coordinated quantities through a shared input and retain timing, orientation, and repeated traversal that a rectangular relation can conceal. Linear, circular, and projectile models connect the representation to motion.

## Main learning objectives

- Graph parametric relations with a specified parameter interval, orientation, and point set.
- Convert between parametric and rectangular descriptions while preserving restrictions.
- Construct and analyze linear, circular, and projectile motion models with consistent units and contextual domains.

## Concepts and topics

Parameter notation; ordered-pair graphs; orientation and repeated tracing; elimination and parametrization; line segments and circles; constant-velocity motion; angular and linear velocity; idealized projectile motion.
