# Lesson 14.6: Finite geometric models

Finite geometric sums describe repeated proportional contributions and deposit accumulation. The modeled total depends on the included contributions, their timing, and the common valuation time.

## Learning objectives

- Translate finite proportional-change situations into sums with explicit contributions and endpoints.
- Evaluate and interpret totals with appropriate units and precision.
- Build deposit accumulation models, separate contributions from growth, and compare payment timing at a shared valuation time.

## Learning outcome

Construct, evaluate, and verify a finite accumulation model from its contributions, timing, and stated assumptions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Identify the first included contribution, multiplier, term count, and any repeated contribution.
- Distinguish individual contributions from the total and retain precision until the final reported value.
- Assign growth periods consistently with deposit and valuation times.
- Separate deposited principal from modeled growth and compare beginning- and end-of-period deposits.
- Handle zero growth without invalid division and interpret the result in context.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Totals from repeated proportional change | A proportional-change term describes one contribution; a finite sum describes a total over explicitly included contributions. Specify the first included term, ratio, endpoint indices, and term count. Include any initial amount or repeated physical contribution exactly as the modeled quantity requires. Retain calculation precision until reporting the total in the appropriate units. | Construct a finite geometric sum from proportional contributions, specifying inclusion boundaries and any repeated contribution. Evaluate and interpret an accumulated total with correct endpoints, units, and final rounding. | Identify and count the actual included contributions; distinguish one contribution from the accumulated quantity; include initial or repeated contributions according to the stated model; evaluate the resulting finite sum with sufficient internal precision and appropriate final units and rounding. |
| Repeated deposits and accumulation timing | With zero initial balance, equal deposits $D$, a fixed period rate $i$ satisfying $1+i>0$, and no fees or withdrawals, $N\ge1$ end-of-period deposits valued just after deposit $N$ have $V_{\mathrm{end}}=D\sum_{k=0}^{N-1}(1+i)^k$. Beginning-of-period deposits valued at the same end-of-period-$N$ time each receive one extra growth period, so $V_{\mathrm{begin}}=(1+i)V_{\mathrm{end}}$. Deposited principal is $ND$; modeled growth is $V-ND$. At $i=0$, both totals equal $ND$. | Build and evaluate a finite deposit accumulation model from a stated timeline and distinguish deposited principal from modeled growth. Compare beginning- and end-of-period accumulation at a common valuation time, including zero growth. | Describe the payment and valuation timeline and assign each deposit its growth periods; construct and evaluate the accumulation sum under the stated assumptions; separate principal from growth; compare timing conventions at the same valuation time and handle zero growth without division by zero. |
