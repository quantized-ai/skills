# Standards: Lesson 40.2 — Ellipses from focal definitions

Mappings for [Lesson 40.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deriving the ellipse equation** | Derive the ellipse equation from a constant sum of focal distances and justify the relationship among a, b, and c. | CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Direct: ellipse derivation); TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(G) (Direct: locus and equation). |
| **Ellipse features and translated equations** | Graph translated axis-aligned ellipses and determine centers, axes, vertices, covertices, and foci from their equations. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(H) (Direct: ellipse characteristics); CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Supporting knowledge: interpreting derived equations). |
| **Constructing ellipse equations** | Construct equations of axis-aligned ellipses from centers, vertices, foci, axis lengths, or equivalent sufficient geometric constraints. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(H) (Direct); CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Direct when focal constraints determine the equation). |
