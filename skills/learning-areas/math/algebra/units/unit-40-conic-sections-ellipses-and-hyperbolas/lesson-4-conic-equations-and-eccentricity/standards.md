# Standards: Lesson 40.4 — Conic equations and eccentricity

Mappings for [Lesson 40.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Expanded conic equations** | Convert expanded axis-aligned quadratic relations to standard form and classify their real loci, including degenerate and empty cases. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(G) (Direct: locus and rectangular equation); TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(H) (Direct: ellipse equation); TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(I) (Direct: hyperbola equation). |
| **Eccentricity and focus-directrix form** | Calculate and interpret eccentricity and connect ellipse, parabola, and hyperbola classifications to the focus-directrix distance ratio. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(G) (Extension: eccentricity unifies the stated conic loci); CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Extension: focus-directrix interpretation). |
