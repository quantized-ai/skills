# Standards: Lesson 40.3 — Hyperbolas from focal definitions

Mappings for [Lesson 40.3](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deriving the hyperbola equation** | Derive the hyperbola equation from an absolute difference of focal distances and justify the relationship among a, b, and c. | CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Direct: hyperbola derivation); TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(G) (Direct: locus and equation). |
| **Hyperbola features and asymptotes** | Graph translated axis-aligned hyperbolas and determine their centers, vertices, foci, orientations, and asymptotes. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(I) (Direct: hyperbola characteristics); CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Supporting knowledge: interpreting derived equations). |
| **Constructing hyperbola equations** | Construct axis-aligned hyperbola equations from sufficient center, vertex, focus, asymptote, or point information and identify insufficient data. | TEKS §111.42(c)(3)(I) (Direct); CCSS HSG-GPE.A.3 (+) (Direct when focal constraints determine the equation). |
