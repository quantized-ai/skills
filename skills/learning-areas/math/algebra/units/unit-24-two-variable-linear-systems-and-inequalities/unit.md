# Unit 24: Two-variable linear systems and inequalities

This unit interprets shared linear constraints as simultaneous solution sets, develops exact and graphical solution methods, and extends intersections to inequality regions.

## Main learning objectives

- Solve two-variable linear systems graphically, by substitution, and by elimination while justifying equivalence.
- Classify one-solution, inconsistent, and dependent systems and express complete solution sets.
- Formulate linear systems and inequalities from quantitative relationships and interpret feasible choices with contextual restrictions.

## Concepts and topics

Graph intersections; exact and approximate solutions; substitution; elimination and equivalence; parallel and coincident lines; contextual systems; boundary lines; half-planes; feasible regions; strictness and integer restrictions.

## Lessons

- [24.1: Systems and graphical solutions](lesson-1-systems-and-graphical-solutions/lesson.md) — The solution of a system must satisfy every equation at once, making line intersections the graphical representation of simultaneous solutions.
- [24.2: Solving systems by substitution](lesson-2-solving-systems-by-substitution/lesson.md) — Substitution replaces a quantity by an equal expression to reduce simultaneous linear equations to one variable.
- [24.3: Solving systems by elimination](lesson-3-solving-systems-by-elimination/lesson.md) — Equivalent equation combinations remove one variable while retaining exactly the original simultaneous solutions.
- [24.4: Classification and choice of system method](lesson-4-classification-and-choice-of-system-method/lesson.md) — Coefficient relationships and solution structure connect the graphical and algebraic methods for linear systems.
- [24.5: Constructing and interpreting linear systems](lesson-5-constructing-and-interpreting-linear-systems/lesson.md) — Related quantities and multiple conditions lead to simultaneous models whose solutions must remain meaningful in context.
- [24.6: Linear inequalities and feasible regions](lesson-6-linear-inequalities-and-feasible-regions/lesson.md) — Linear inequalities define half-planes, and simultaneous inequalities define their common solution set. Contextual restrictions determine which points in that real-valued region are feasible choices.
