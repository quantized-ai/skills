# Unit 33: Circle geometry

Similarity, congruent triangles, and angle relationships explain circle geometry. Theorems about chords, arcs, tangents, and secants connect to lengths, radians, and sectors.

## Main learning objectives

- Prove similarity of circles and justify relationships among radii, chords, and tangents.
- Prove and apply inscribed-angle and cyclic-quadrilateral relationships and analyze interior and exterior circle angles.
- Prove and apply chord, secant, and tangent segment-product relationships.
- Derive arc-length and sector-area relationships and connect arc length with radian measure.

## Concepts and topics

Circle similarity, arcs, chords, radius-tangent perpendicularity, central and inscribed angles, circumscribed angles, cyclic quadrilaterals, chord and secant products, tangent lengths, radians, arc length, sectors, and segments.
