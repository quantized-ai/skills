# Unit 31: Triangle trigonometry

Triangle similarity defines acute-angle ratios; angle extensions support laws for every nondegenerate triangle. Proofs, inverse ratios, and admissible-data analysis connect exact relationships with measurement models.

## Main learning objectives

- Define sine, cosine, and tangent from similarity and use complementary-angle identities.
- Solve right triangles and applied measurement problems with ratios, inverse ratios, and the Pythagorean theorem.
- Derive triangle area, sine-law, and cosine-law formulas and use them for right and oblique triangles.
- Determine whether specified data produce zero, one, two, or infinitely many triangles.

## Concepts and topics

Acute-angle ratios, opposite and adjacent sides, complementary angles, inverse ratios, elevation and depression, obtuse-angle extensions, triangle area, Laws of Sines and Cosines, SSA ambiguity, and admissibility of triangle data.
