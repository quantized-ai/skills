# Lesson 31.4: Triangle data and ambiguity

Different combinations of triangle measurements carry different existence and uniqueness information.

## Learning objectives

- Determine the number of noncongruent triangles compatible with side and angle data, including SSA ambiguity.
- Solve and validate general-triangle models while retaining every admissible solution.

## Proficiency criteria

- Distinguish reflected placements of one triangle from genuinely different triangles and check strict angle and side conditions.
- Investigate the supplementary inverse-sine candidate and explain any rejected solution.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **SSA ambiguity** | Given \(A\), opposite side \(a\), and another side \(b\), the Law of Sines gives \(\sin B=b\sin A/a\). Both \(B\) and \(180^\circ-B\) must be checked against \(A+B<180^\circ\). For acute \(A\), writing \(h=b\sin A\): \(a<h\) gives none, \(a=h\) gives one right triangle, \(h<a<b\) gives two, and \(a\ge b\) gives one. For right or obtuse \(A\), exactly one exists if \(a>b\), and none if \(a\le b\). | Determine and solve all admissible triangles for SSA data using inverse-sine candidates and geometric height conditions. | Include zero, one, and two-triangle cases; reject sine values above one and nonpositive remaining angles; do not discard a valid supplementary candidate. |
| **Existence, uniqueness, and triangle models** | Valid SSS, SAS, ASA, and AAS data determine one triangle up to congruence; invalid side inequalities or angle totals give none. AAA with positive angles summing to a straight angle gives infinitely many sizes. SSA may give two noncongruent triangles. Surveying, navigation, design, and force-resultant geometry require converting contextual angles and distances into labeled triangle data before using these criteria. | Classify triangle data as producing zero, one, two, or infinitely many triangles and solve general-triangle models with all valid solutions. | State what is determined up to congruence, distinguish a drawing’s reflected placement from another shape, and validate side lengths, angle conventions, units, and contextual feasibility. |
