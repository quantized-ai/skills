## Additional private transfer checks

These original keyed probes combine or stress concepts. They are calibration references, not a default quiz; generate unseen variants for students.

### Transfer check 1

**Prompt:** Find the point three quarters of the way from (6,-2) to (-2,10); then find the perpendicular through it to the segment.

**Key and required reasoning:** Displacement (-8,12) gives P=(0,7). Segment slope -3/2; perpendicular slope 2/3, so $y=(2/3)x+7$.

### Transfer check 2

**Prompt:** Intersect x²+y²=20 and (x-2)²+y²=20.

**Key and required reasoning:** Subtract to get x=1, then y²=19. Points $(1,\sqrt{19})$ and $(1,-\sqrt{19})$ satisfy both.
