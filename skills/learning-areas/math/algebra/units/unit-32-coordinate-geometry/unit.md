# Unit 32: Coordinate geometry

Coordinates translate distance, direction, partition, and incidence into algebra. These tools support general geometric proofs, polygon measurement, and the equation of a circle.

## Main learning objectives

- Derive and use distance, midpoint, and internal-division formulas.
- Prove parallel and perpendicular slope criteria, including vertical-line cases, and construct related line equations.
- Use coordinates to prove geometric relationships and compute polygon measures.
- Derive circle equations, recover centers and radii by completing the square, and analyze intersections.

## Concepts and topics

Cartesian distances, section ratios, midpoint, slope, parallel and perpendicular equations, coordinate proofs, perimeter and area, circles in standard and expanded form, and line-circle and circle-circle intersections.
