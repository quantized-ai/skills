# Lesson 43.4: Addition and multiplication of probabilities

Compound-event rules combine overlapping possibilities and dependent stages without double counting.

## Learning objectives

- Calculate and interpret probabilities of unions and complements in contextual probability models.
- Apply the general multiplication rule and probability trees to multistage events, with and without independence.

## Proficiency criteria

- Subtract overlap exactly once, distinguish inclusive or from exclusive or, and verify results lie between zero and one.
- Update conditional probabilities at each stage, justify any independence simplification, and connect each path or sum to its event.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Addition and complement rules** | $P(A\cup B)=P(A)+P(B)-P(A\cap B)$ and $P(A^c)=1-P(A)$. Neither the addition rule nor the complement rule requires independence. | Calculate and interpret probabilities of unions and complements in contextual probability models. | Subtract overlap exactly once, distinguish inclusive or from exclusive or, and verify results lie between zero and one. |
| **Multiplication along dependent stages** | $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B\mid A)$ for $P(A)>0$; a corresponding reversed expression applies when $P(B)>0$. A probability tree multiplies along paths and adds disjoint paths; replacement rules control branch probabilities. | Apply the general multiplication rule and probability trees to multistage events, with and without independence. | Update conditional probabilities at each stage, justify any independence simplification, and connect each path or sum to its event. |
