# Unit 43: Probability and counting

Finite and geometric sample spaces, counting, conditional probability, and random allocation provide the probability foundations used in geometry and statistics.

## Main learning objectives

- Represent sample spaces and compound events and calculate probabilities using valid counts or assigned weights.
- Construct area-based probability models with explicit uniformity assumptions.
- Construct organized lists, trees, and products or sums to count restricted finite outcomes.
- Construct and interpret conditional probabilities in two-way tables, trees, Venn diagrams, and area models.
- Calculate and interpret probabilities of unions and complements in contextual probability models.
- Design and justify a random allocation mechanism meeting a stated fairness criterion.

## Concepts and topics

Sample spaces and events; Geometric probability from area; Theoretical and empirical probability; Addition and multiplication principles; Permutations and combinations; Conditional probability in tables and diagrams; Independence and disjointness; Addition and complement rules; Multiplication along dependent stages; Fair random selection; Base rates and decision consequences.
