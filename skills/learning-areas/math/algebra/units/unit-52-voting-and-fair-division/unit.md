# Unit 52: Voting and fair division

Collective choices and resource allocations depend on explicit rules, preference information, and definitions of fairness.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct preference schedules and determine outcomes under ranked and approval voting rules.
- Explain Arrow’s impossibility theorem and its implications for evaluating voting schemes.
- Evaluate allocations using proportionality, envy-freeness, equitability, and Pareto efficiency.
- Use adjusted winner to allocate property and analyze a two-party dispute.
- Apply the Knaster inheritance procedure to allocations with three or more participants.

## Concepts and topics

Ranked and approval voting; Strategic and agenda effects; Arrow’s impossibility theorem; Weighted coalitions and Banzhaf power; Proportionality, envy, equity, and efficiency; Divider-chooser and last diminisher; Adjusted winner procedure; Divisibility and trimming limitations; Sealed bids and fair-share accounts; Surplus and guarantees.
