# Lesson 52.5: Knaster inheritance and compensation

Sealed monetary valuations allocate indivisible goods while a cash settlement balances each participant’s own fair share.

## Learning objectives

- Apply the Knaster inheritance procedure to allocations with three or more participants.
- Complete and evaluate a Knaster settlement including surplus distribution and feasibility conditions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Use separate valuation totals, allocate every good once, calculate each fair share and initial settlement, and maintain consistent payment signs.
- Balance all transfers to zero after distributing the surplus, verify individual fair shares, and identify liquidity and envy limitations.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sealed bids and fair-share accounts** | For $n$ participants with additive monetary valuations, assign each good to its highest bidder using a declared tie rule. Participant $i$ has fair share $s_i=(\text{sum of that participant’s bids})/n$. The value of that participant’s award minus $s_i$ determines an initial payment into or withdrawal from a common account. | Apply the Knaster inheritance procedure to allocations with three or more participants. | Use separate valuation totals, allocate every good once, calculate each fair share and initial settlement, and maintain consistent payment signs. |
| **Surplus and guarantees** | The initial settlement creates a nonnegative surplus under highest-bid allocation; sharing it equally gives each participant their own fair share plus the same cash bonus. The guarantee assumes honest additive monetary valuations and enough liquidity; proportionality does not imply envy-freeness for three or more participants. | Complete and evaluate a Knaster settlement including surplus distribution and feasibility conditions. | Balance all transfers to zero after distributing the surplus, verify individual fair shares, and identify liquidity and envy limitations. |
