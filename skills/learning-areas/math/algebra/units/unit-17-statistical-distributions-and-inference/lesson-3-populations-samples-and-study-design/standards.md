# Standards: Lesson 17.3 — Populations, samples, and study design

Mappings for [Lesson 17.3](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot); section and subsection citations identify the official course. Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Parameters, statistics, and random samples** | Identify the target population, sampling frame, population parameter, and sample statistic, and distinguish a fixed parameter from a statistic’s repeated-sample variation. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.1 (direct); TEKS §111.47(c)(2)(D) (direct). |
| **Parameters, statistics, and random samples** | Explain how random sampling supports population inference and how frame coverage and other design limitations restrict its scope. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.1 (direct). |
| **Surveys, observational studies, and experiments** | Classify surveys, observational studies, and experiments by data collection and exposure assignment, recognizing that a survey may be observational. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.3 (direct); TEKS §111.43(c)(9)(C) (direct). |
| **Surveys, observational studies, and experiments** | Distinguish random sampling from random assignment and state the population and causal conclusions each actual design can support. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSS-ID.C.9 (direct). |
| **Bias, confounding, and design repair** | Identify the mechanism producing selection bias, undercoverage, nonresponse, response bias, or confounding in a study. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSS-IC.B.6 (direct). |
| **Bias, confounding, and design repair** | Propose a targeted design revision, explain why larger sample size alone does not repair bias, and identify limitations that may remain. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSS-IC.B.6 (direct). |
