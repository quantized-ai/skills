# Standards: Lesson 7.1 — Definitions and restrictions

Mappings for [Lesson 7.1](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Rational expressions and allowed inputs** | Classify an expression as rational, rewriting when needed, and identify its polynomial numerator and denominator. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.7 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(F) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Rational expressions and allowed inputs** | Determine all excluded real inputs and evaluate a rational expression only at permitted inputs. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Original domains and equivalent formulas** | Simplify by canceling common nonzero factors and state the domain on which original and reduced formulas agree. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Original domains and equivalent formulas** | Distinguish equivalent formulas on a restricted domain from functions with different natural domains. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
