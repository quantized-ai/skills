# Standards: Lesson 12.5 — Restrictions and inverse verification

Mappings for [Lesson 12.5](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Restricting a quadratic to obtain an inverse** | Restrict a quadratic to an interval on one side of its vertex and justify why it is one-to-one there. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4d (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct). |
| **Restricting a quadratic to obtain an inverse** | Derive the matching inverse branch and exchange domain and range, preserving sign and endpoint conditions. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.2(B) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct). |
| **Verifying both compositions on their domains** | Verify both inverse compositions as identity maps on their respective domains, checking that both compositions are defined. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4b (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct). |
| **Verifying both compositions on their domains** | Identify and explain failed inverse claims caused by missing domain or sign restrictions. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4b (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct). |
