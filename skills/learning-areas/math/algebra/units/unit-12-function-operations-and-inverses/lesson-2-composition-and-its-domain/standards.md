# Standards: Lesson 12.2 — Composition and its domain

Mappings for [Lesson 12.2](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Composition order and evaluation** | Evaluate compositions from formulas or tables in the correct order, checking that both stages are defined. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Composition order and evaluation** | Distinguish composition from multiplication, compare reversed compositions, and check compatible units in context. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS §111.48(c)(3)(E) (supporting knowledge: contextual composition and units). |
| **Algebraic composition with restrictions** | Construct and simplify an algebraic composition by substituting the entire inner expression. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS §111.48(c)(3)(E) (supporting knowledge: composing formulas; complete contextual modeling is not asserted by this objective). |
| **Algebraic composition with restrictions** | Determine the composition domain using the inner domain and outer input conditions, retaining inherited restrictions after simplification. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
