# Standards: Lesson 59.3 — Polynomial quotients and factors across representations

Mappings for [Lesson 59.3](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot); section and subsection citations retain the official course identifiers. Direct addresses the stated standard component; supporting knowledge enables it without completing every required action; extension exceeds its explicit scope. Mapping qualifications preserve partial coverage.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Division identities and tabular quotients** | Determine quotients and remainders for cubic or quartic polynomial functions divided by linear or quadratic functions and connect the symbolic division to tabular values. | TEKS §111.48(c)(4)(C) (Direct); CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (Direct). |
| **Linear factors from zeros and structure** | Determine and verify linear factors of quadratic and cubic polynomial functions from symbolic rules, tables, and graphs where the available information permits. | TEKS §111.48(c)(4)(D) (Direct); CCSS HSA-APR.B.2–3 (Direct). |
| **Cross-representation verification and evidence** | Compare tabular and graphical evidence with symbolic verification when evaluating proposed polynomial factors or quotients. | TEKS §111.48(c)(4)(C), §111.48(c)(4)(D) (Direct: connecting and verifying representations); CCSS HSA-APR.B.2, HSA-APR.D.6 (Supporting knowledge: verification and evidence). |
