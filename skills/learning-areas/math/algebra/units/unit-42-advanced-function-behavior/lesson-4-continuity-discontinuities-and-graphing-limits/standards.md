# Standards: Lesson 42.4 — Continuity, discontinuities, and graphing limits

Mappings for [Lesson 42.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. **TEKS:** Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics (supplied October 2015 snapshot); §111.42 is Precalculus, §111.44 is Advanced Quantitative Reasoning, and §111.48 is Algebraic Reasoning. **Direct:** an explicit component of the cited standard; **Supporting knowledge:** enabling knowledge rather than the complete required action; **Extension:** beyond the standard’s explicit requirement. A **(+)** qualifier is retained from the official standard and does not make content optional. Unmapped objectives are locally authored additions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Continuity and removable discontinuities** | Determine continuity from function values and nearby behavior and identify or repair removable discontinuities without changing the original domain silently. | TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(L) (Direct: discontinuity classification; Extension: explicit continuity condition); TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(M) (Direct: left and right behavior). |
| **Jump, infinite, and oscillatory discontinuities** | Classify nonremovable discontinuities from one-sided behavior, including piecewise jumps, vertical-asymptote behavior, and persistent oscillation. | TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(L) (Direct: types of discontinuity); TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(M) (Direct: one-sided behavior; Extension: oscillatory cases). |
| **Limitations of numerical graphs** | Evaluate graphing-calculator or software displays near discontinuities and corroborate their apparent features with domain, algebraic, and one-sided analysis. | TEKS §111.42(c)(2)(L) (Direct: graphing-calculator limitations). |
