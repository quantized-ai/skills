# Lesson 9.1: Root definitions and principal values

Define real roots, distinguish radical values from equation solutions, and simplify roots of powers with the correct signs.

## Learning objectives

- Evaluate real nth roots and distinguish radical values from complete power-equation solution sets.
- Simplify roots of powers and justify required absolute values and sign conditions.

## Learning outcome

Determine real radical values and simplify root expressions without losing sign conditions or equation solutions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Distinguish even and odd root domains and select the correct single radical value.
- Find all real solutions of a power equation, including zero and no-solution cases.
- Use absolute values for unrestricted even-root extractions; justify any removal using the sign of the extracted quantity.
- Check a proposed simplification using its sign, power, and permitted inputs.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Even and odd nth roots** | For integer $n\ge2$, an nth root of $a$ has nth power $a$. For even $n$, $\sqrt[n]{a}$ exists over the reals only for $a\ge0$ and denotes the nonnegative principal root. For odd $n$, every real $a$ has one real root, with the same sign as $a$. The equation $x^n=a$ has two real solutions for even $n$ and $a>0$, one for $a=0$, none for even $n$ and $a<0$, and one for odd $n$. | Evaluate real nth roots and identify radicands with no real even root. Distinguish a radical value from the complete real solution set of a power equation. | Identify the index and radicand. Determine whether a real root exists and select its principal or unique signed value. List all real solutions of the corresponding power equation without assigning multiple values to the radical symbol. |
| **Absolute value when extracting even powers** | For real $u$ and integer $k\ge1$, $\sqrt[2k]{u^{2k}}=\lvert u\rvert$ and $\sqrt[2k+1]{u^{2k+1}}=u$. An even root must be nonnegative. The absolute value equals $u$ when $u\ge0$ and $-u$ when $u\le0$; it cannot be dropped without establishing the relevant sign. | Simplify roots of powers using absolute values and justified sign conditions. Use sign, power, and input checks to detect an invalid root simplification. | Retain an absolute value for an unrestricted even-root extraction. Establish the sign before replacing it with a signed expression. Verify that the result has the required sign and power, and use permitted negative inputs to detect a missing absolute value. |
