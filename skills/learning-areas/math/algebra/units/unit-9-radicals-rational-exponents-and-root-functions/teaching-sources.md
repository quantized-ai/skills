# Unit 9: teaching sources and local design

Consulted on 26 September 2026. The curriculum is authoritative for this unit’s scope; sources inform instruction and mathematical checks. All embedded questions, numbers, keys, and AI delivery rules are locally authored. No publisher exercise bank, card set, or image is reproduced.

## Teacher guidance consulted

- [IES / What Works Clearinghouse algebra practice guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/20) and its [official summary](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/Docs/PracticeGuide/wwc_algebra_summary_072115.pdf): analyze completed reasoning, attend to algebraic structure, and compare strategies after students can follow a method. The first two recommendations carry minimal evidence ratings; strategy choice carries a moderate rating. Those ratings concern classroom research, not this tutor.
- [Mathematics Assessment Project teacher guide](https://www.map.mathshell.org/docs/map_cc_teacher_guide.pdf), from the Berkeley/Nottingham project: elicit reasoning, use specific feedback, and invite revision. Here these principles are adapted to an individual conversation; the tutor does not simulate classmates or claim reproduced classroom effects.

## Mathematical reference consulted

- [OpenStax Algebra and Trigonometry 2e, 1.3 Radicals and Rational Exponents](https://openstax.org/books/algebra-and-trigonometry-2e/pages/1-3-radicals-and-rational-exponents): a supporting mathematical reference for the corresponding part of this unit. It is not a claim that the section covers every local concept or exception. Definitions and consequential conditions in the curriculum take precedence over a simplified presentation.

Specific qualifications for this unit: Track real domains and principal-root signs, preserve absolute values, and check candidates after nonreversible operations.

## Evidence limits

The evidence labels, two-task minimum, hint levels, exposure record, and fresh-question policy are local operating choices. They are not validated mastery thresholds from these sources. File checks and recomputed examples do not demonstrate learning gains, long-term retention, reliable retrieval, or consistent tutor behavior. Use [agent-evaluation.md](agent-evaluation.md) for actual model sessions and preserve the existing official standards mappings separately.

## Specific application in this revision

The radicals/rational-exponents reference supports root conventions and conditional laws. This revision's counterexamples expose where unrestricted cancellation of root and power fails. Worked and practice sequences distinguish a defined but unequal candidate from an undefined one, and distinguish odd roots from principal even roots throughout.

Every lesson now supplies a local reasoning activity and every concept a concrete teaching sequence, response to observed errors, practice progression and evidence checklist. These are original agent instructions built around the curriculum, not copied classroom scripts. In particular, correct diagnosis requires the student’s actual reasoning; naming a common misconception does not establish that this student has it. A hint or worked repair is followed by revision and then a separate fresh independent attempt.
