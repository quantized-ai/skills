## Additional private transfer checks

These original keyed probes combine or stress concepts. They are calibration references, not a default quiz; generate unseen variants for students.

### Transfer check 1

**Prompt:** An open-top right cylindrical container has radius 2 and height 5. Find capacity and the area of its base and exterior curved surface, neglecting wall thickness.

**Key and required reasoning:** Volume $20\pi$ cubic units; included area $4\pi+20\pi=24\pi$ square units. No top disk is included.

### Transfer check 2

**Prompt:** A similar solid has surface area nine times the original. How does volume change? Contrast tripling only cylinder height.

**Key and required reasoning:** Uniform similarity gives length factor 3 and volume factor 27. Tripling only cylinder height gives volume factor 3 and lateral area factor 3, with base areas unchanged.
