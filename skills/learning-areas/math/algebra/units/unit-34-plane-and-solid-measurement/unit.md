# Unit 34: Plane and solid measurement

Area and volume formulas are explained through dissection, limiting arguments, and cross-sections. Measurement extends from polygons and circles to surfaces, composite solids, and dimensional changes.

## Main learning objectives

- Derive and apply plane area and circumference relationships and measure composite regions.
- Identify cross-sections and solids generated by rotations and use nets to determine surface areas.
- Explain cylinder, pyramid, cone, and sphere volume formulas, including Cavalieri arguments, and apply them to composite solids.
- Distinguish uniform scaling laws from the effects of changing selected dimensions.

## Concepts and topics

Polygon areas, regular-polygon apothems, circle area and circumference, composite regions, cross-sections, solids of revolution, nets, lateral and total surface area, prisms, cylinders, pyramids, cones, spheres, Cavalieri’s principle, volume, and dimensional scaling.
