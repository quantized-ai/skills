GAISE II’s investigation-to-interpretation framework motivates asking for the observational unit and question before choosing a display. Its Level B/C scope supports reasoning about variability, conditional proportions and claim limits. The local examples preserve duplicates, pairing, quartile conventions and practical technology evidence; the framework is not a numerical answer bank.
