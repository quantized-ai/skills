# Lesson 25.8: Time-series, sector, and stem-and-leaf displays

Alternative displays preserve different information and can strengthen or weaken conclusions from the same data.

## Learning objectives

- Construct and interpret line and circle graphs and evaluate the conclusions supported by their scales and denominators.
- Construct and interpret stem-and-leaf displays and compare the information and limitations of multiple displays of the same data.

## Proficiency criteria

- Use chronological spacing and appropriate connections, reconcile sector proportions to a complete whole, and identify the specific display choice that strengthens or distorts a claim.
- Provide a complete key and consistent ordering, preserve all observations including signs and repetitions, and justify which representations support particular conclusions about shape, center, spread, and unusual values.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Time-series and sector displays** | A line graph connects measurements in ordered time and can suggest changes between observations only under a justified time model. A circle graph divides $360^\circ$ in proportion to mutually exclusive category frequencies whose sum is the whole. Unequal time spacing, omitted categories, truncated axes, and three-dimensional styling can distort interpretation. | Construct and interpret line and circle graphs and evaluate the conclusions supported by their scales and denominators. | Use chronological spacing and appropriate connections, reconcile sector proportions to a complete whole, and identify the specific display choice that strengthens or distorts a claim. |
| **Stem-and-leaf displays and comparison of representations** | A stem-and-leaf display splits quantitative observations into leading and trailing digits with a key and preserves values and multiplicities at a stated precision. Displays of the same data reveal different features: grouped bins obscure exact values, box plots emphasize quantiles, and ordered value displays preserve detail. | Construct and interpret stem-and-leaf displays and compare the information and limitations of multiple displays of the same data. | Provide a complete key and consistent ordering, preserve all observations including signs and repetitions, and justify which representations support particular conclusions about shape, center, spread, and unusual values. |
