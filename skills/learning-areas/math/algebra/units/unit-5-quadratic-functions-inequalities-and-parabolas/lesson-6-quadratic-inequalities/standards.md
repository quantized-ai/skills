# Standards: Lesson 5.6 — Quadratic inequalities

Mappings for [Lesson 5.6](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quadratic inequalities with two real zeros | Solve a quadratic inequality with two distinct real boundary roots using a factor sign chart or graph. | TEKS 2A.4(H) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (supporting knowledge for boundary equations). |
| Quadratic inequalities with two real zeros | Justify endpoint inclusion and express the complete solution set in interval notation. | TEKS 2A.4(H) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(I) (supporting knowledge for notation). |
| Repeated-root and no-real-root inequalities | Solve quadratic inequalities when the boundary equation has a repeated root or no real root. | TEKS 2A.4(H) (direct). |
| Repeated-root and no-real-root inequalities | Justify all-real, empty, singleton, or excluded-point solution sets using square form and strict or nonstrict comparisons. | TEKS 2A.4(H) (direct). |
