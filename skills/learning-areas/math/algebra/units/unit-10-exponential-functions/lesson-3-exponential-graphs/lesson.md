# Lesson 10.3: Exponential graphs

Exponential graphs connect multiplicative structure to domain, range, intercepts, and end behavior. Shifts, vertical scales, and reflections determine the corresponding features of transformed graphs.

## Learning objectives

- Sketch exponential parents with bases two, ten, and the natural base, and compare growth with decay.
- Map transformed exponential graphs and determine their domain, range, intercepts, asymptote, monotonicity, and end behavior.

## Learning outcome

Construct and analyze exponential graphs with consistent points, transformations, and key features.

## Proficiency criteria

- Plot consistent parent points and state the parent domain, range, intercepts, and asymptote.
- Give correct growth or decay directions and end behavior.
- Map parent points through the specified transformations.
- Determine transformed intercepts, range, asymptote, and monotonicity using the coefficient’s sign and the base.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Parent graphs for bases 2, 10, and e** | For $b>0$, $b\ne1$, the parent $y=b^x$ has domain $\mathbb R$, range $(0,\infty)$, $y$-intercept $(0,1)$, no $x$-intercept, and horizontal asymptote $y=0$. If $b>1$, it increases, approaching $0$ to the left and growing without bound to the right; if $0<b<1$, these directions reverse. Required growth bases are $2$, $10$, and $e\approx2.71828$; reciprocal bases give corresponding decay graphs. | Sketch exponential parent graphs for bases two, ten, and the natural base and identify their key features. Compare exponential growth and decay and describe their monotonicity and end behavior with correct directions. | Plot points consistent with exponential evaluation and the common intercept. State the domain, positive range, absence of zeros, and horizontal asymptote. Describe change and end behavior correctly for bases above and below one. |
| **Transformed exponential graphs** | For $g(x)=ab^{x-h}+k$, assume $a\ne0$, $b>0$, $b\ne1$, and real $h,k$. Parent points $(u,v)$ map to $(u+h,av+k)$. The domain is $\mathbb R$ and the asymptote is $y=k$; the range is $(k,\infty)$ for $a>0$ and $(-\infty,k)$ for $a<0$. A negative $a$ reverses the parent’s direction of change. The $y$-intercept is $ab^{-h}+k$; an $x$-intercept exists exactly when $-k/a>0$ and satisfies $b^{x-h}=-k/a$. | Graph transformations of exponential functions by mapping parent points. Determine domain, intercepts, range, asymptote, monotonicity, and end behavior after transformation. | Map points with the correct shifts, scale, and reflection. Determine the domain, asymptote, and side of the asymptote occupied by the graph. Locate intercepts or justify their absence. Use the base and coefficient sign to explain monotonicity and both ends of the graph. |
