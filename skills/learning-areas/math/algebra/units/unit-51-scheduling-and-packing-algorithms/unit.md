# Unit 51: Scheduling and packing algorithms

Heuristic schedules and packings are compared against resource and precedence constraints and provable lower bounds.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct list-processing schedules for independent and precedence-constrained tasks on identical processors.
- Apply and compare next-fit, first-fit, best-fit, and their decreasing variants to capacity-constrained packing.

## Concepts and topics

List processing and feasible schedules; Bounds and optimality; Packing heuristics; Packing bounds and scheduling connections.

## Lessons

- [51.1: List scheduling on identical processors](lesson-1-list-scheduling-on-identical-processors/lesson.md) — A priority list assigns ready tasks to available processors while precedence determines which tasks can start.
- [51.2: Bin packing and capacity models](lesson-2-bin-packing-and-capacity-models/lesson.md) — Packing indivisible items into bounded containers reverses the resource question posed by many scheduling problems.
