# Unit 3: Polynomial factorization

Factoring methods expose product structure and connect polynomial equations, zeros, and completeness over a stated coefficient system.

## Main learning objectives

- Distinguish factoring an expression from solving an equation and verify factorizations by multiplication. Find and extract a greatest common monomial factor, preserving every quotient term and sign.
- Factor and verify expressions with repeated polynomial factors. Rewrite opposite binomials while preserving the negative multiplier.
- Factor monic integer-coefficient trinomials using sum-product conditions and sign reasoning. Extract common factors and factor nonmonic trinomials with a valid middle-term split.
- Factor differences of squares, including repeated square structure, and verify the product. Recognize and factor perfect-square trinomials using all three terms.
- Extract common factors and factor differences and sums of cubes. Verify cube identities through cancellation of intermediate terms.
- Identify and factor expressions quadratic in a repeated power or subexpression. Select and sequence factoring methods from the polynomial structure.
- Solve polynomial equations in zero form using the zero-product property and verify solutions. Explain how dividing by a potentially zero expression can discard roots.

## Concepts and topics

Factoring as reversing distribution; Extracting a greatest common monomial factor; Factoring a repeated polynomial expression; Factoring by grouping; Monic quadratic trinomials; Nonmonic quadratic trinomials; Factoring differences of squares; Perfect-square trinomials; Differences of cubes; Sums of cubes; Quadratic structure in higher powers; Selecting a factoring strategy; The zero-product property; Connecting zeros, factors, and polynomial equations.

- [3.4: Square structures](lesson-4-square-structures/lesson.md) — Recognize differences of squares and perfect-square trinomials as reversed product identities. Verify the required structure and continue factoring within the stated coefficient set.

- [3.6: Substitution and a complete strategy](lesson-6-substitution-and-a-complete-strategy/lesson.md) — Expose quadratic structure through substitution and combine common-factor, grouping, and special-product methods. - [3.7: Factored equations and zeros](lesson-7-factored-equations-and-zeros/lesson.md) — Solve polynomial equations by establishing zero form and using a factorization. Preserve every solution and connect real zeros with horizontal intercepts.
