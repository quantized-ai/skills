# Standards: Lesson 3.3 — Factoring quadratic trinomials

Mappings for [Lesson 3.3](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monic quadratic trinomials** | Factor a monic integer-coefficient quadratic by matching both the sum and product of the constant parts of its linear factors, and verify the product. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge); TEKS A.10(E) (direct). |
| **Monic quadratic trinomials** | Use signs and an exhaustive integer factor-pair search to justify whether rational linear factors exist, without inferring that real roots cannot exist. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct). |
| **Nonmonic quadratic trinomials** | Extract a common factor and factor a nonmonic integer-coefficient quadratic by splitting its middle term and grouping. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge); TEKS A.10(E) (direct). |
| **Nonmonic quadratic trinomials** | Verify all coefficients of the selected factors and explain what an exhaustive middle-term-split search establishes about rational factorization. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct). |
