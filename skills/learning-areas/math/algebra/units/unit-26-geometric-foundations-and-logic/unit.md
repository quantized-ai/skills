# Unit 26: Geometric foundations and logic

Precise geometric language supports measurement, classification, conjecture, and proof. Plane geometry is distinguished from spherical geometry so that the role of assumptions remains explicit.

## Main learning objectives

- Define and classify fundamental geometric objects, angle relationships, triangles, and quadrilaterals.
- Distinguish empirical evidence from deduction and analyze conditional statements, converses, counterexamples, and proof structure.
- Compare parallelism and triangle angle sums in Euclidean and spherical geometries.

## Concepts and topics

Points, lines, planes, segments, rays, circles, distance, angles, polygon classifications, postulates and theorems, logical forms, counterexamples, proof formats, and great-circle geometry.

- [26.3: Conjecture and deductive proof](lesson-3-conjecture-and-deductive-proof/lesson.md) — Conjecture formation and deductive arguments play complementary roles in geometric reasoning.
