# Unit 44: Random variables and probability distributions

Discrete and continuous probability models connect counts, expected value, spread, and decision analysis.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct and graph discrete random-variable distributions from theoretical, empirical, and simulated outcomes.
- Construct and use binomial distributions and compare their theoretical probabilities with simulated frequencies.
- Interpret continuous densities and calculate uniform interval probabilities.
- Construct payoff distributions and determine expected net gain and fair entry prices.

## Concepts and topics

Constructing probability distributions; Expected value and variability; Binomial counts; Geometric waiting times; Density and uniform models; Normal probabilities and inverse percentiles; Net payoff and fair price; Comparing strategies under uncertainty.
