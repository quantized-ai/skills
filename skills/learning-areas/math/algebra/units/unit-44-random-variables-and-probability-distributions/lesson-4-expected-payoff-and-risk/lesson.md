# Lesson 44.4: Expected payoff and risk

Expected payoff allows comparable decisions while variability and assumptions remain visible.

## Learning objectives

- Construct payoff distributions and determine expected net gain and fair entry prices.
- Compare strategies using expected outcomes, risk, and sensitivity to assumed probabilities and costs.

## Proficiency criteria

- Distinguish gross from net payoff, include costs and all outcome probabilities, and interpret fairness as a long-run model statement.
- Use the same outcome model across choices, apply costs and coverage consistently, and distinguish expected-value preference from a universal recommendation.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Net payoff and fair price** | Net payoff subtracts costs from returns.
