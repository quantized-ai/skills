# Unit 54: Mathematical reasoning and independent investigation

Logical analysis, conjecture testing, argument, and communication support independent mathematical inquiry across the collection.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct truth tables for compound conditional statements and determine argument validity.
- Develop and carry out a bounded independent mathematical investigation with justified methods and conclusions.

## Concepts and topics

Connectives and truth tables; Quantifiers and counterexamples; Investigating a mathematical question; Representations, tools, and audience.

## Lessons

- [54.1: Compound statements and logical validity](lesson-1-compound-statements-and-logical-validity/lesson.md) — Truth conditions distinguish valid inference from agreement with a particular example.
- [54.2: Independent investigation and mathematical communication](lesson-2-independent-investigation-and-mathematical-communication/lesson.md) — Independent inquiry defines a question, selects methods and representations, and makes results open to scrutiny.
