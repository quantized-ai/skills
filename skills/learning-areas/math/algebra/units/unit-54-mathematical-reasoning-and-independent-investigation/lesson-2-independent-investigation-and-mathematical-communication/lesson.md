# Lesson 54.2: Independent investigation and mathematical communication

Independent inquiry defines a question, selects methods and representations, and makes results open to scrutiny.

## Learning objectives

- Develop and carry out a bounded independent mathematical investigation with justified methods and conclusions.
- Select tools and representations and communicate and critique mathematical reasoning in written and oral forms.

## Proficiency criteria

- Distinguish established results, conjectures, computations, and original arguments; revise unsupported claims, check special cases, and explain remaining uncertainty.
- Explain why tools are appropriate, reconcile representations, verify numerical or symbolic outputs, acknowledge sources, and make each inferential step and its conditions clear to the intended audience.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Investigating a mathematical question** | An inquiry states a precise mathematical question, chooses assumptions and scope, identifies relevant definitions or results, develops a strategy, and tests or proves claims.
