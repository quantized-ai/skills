## Unit safeguards

Keep domains, quantifier order, assumptions and conclusion distinct. Counterexamples must satisfy the hypothesis. A finite table or computer search supports only what was checked unless a mathematical bound/argument extends it. Do not invent citations, original discoveries or oral-delivery evidence.
