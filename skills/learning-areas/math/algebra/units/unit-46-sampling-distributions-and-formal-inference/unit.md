# Unit 46: Sampling distributions and formal inference

Probability-based sampling distributions support confidence intervals and hypothesis tests beyond the simulation-based inference already in the collection.

## Main learning objectives

- Construct and compare theoretical and simulated sampling distributions of means and proportions.
- Explain construction and interpretation of confidence intervals and distinguish confidence level from parameter probability.
- Calculate and interpret a confidence interval for a normal-population mean with known standard deviation.
- Formulate null and alternative hypotheses and explain how a statistic supplies evidence about a population claim.
- Interpret technology-generated large-sample tests for a proportion, a mean, two proportions, and two independent means.

## Concepts and topics

Means and proportions under repeated sampling; Normal approximation and the central limit effect; Interval estimates and confidence; Factors controlling margin of error; Mean with known population standard deviation; Large-sample population proportion interval; Null and alternative hypotheses; P-values and significance decisions; Large-sample test families; Type I and Type II errors.
