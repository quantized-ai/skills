# Lesson 46.2: Confidence levels and interval precision

Confidence describes repeated-procedure coverage and margin of error quantifies sampling uncertainty under a model.

## Learning objectives

- Explain construction and interpretation of confidence intervals and distinguish confidence level from parameter probability.
- Analyze effects of sample size, confidence level, and variability on interval precision.

## Proficiency criteria

- Identify the estimate, margin, parameter, population, and sampling assumptions; interpret completed intervals in context without claiming coverage of individual observations.
- Hold other factors fixed for each comparison, distinguish statistical precision from accuracy, and explain why a narrow biased interval is not trustworthy.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Interval estimates and confidence** | An interval procedure combines a sample estimate with an uncertainty margin. A confidence level is its long-run coverage of a fixed population parameter under the assumed sampling process, not the probability the fixed parameter changes between samples. | Explain construction and interpretation of confidence intervals and distinguish confidence level from parameter probability. | Identify the estimate, margin, parameter, population, and sampling assumptions; interpret completed intervals in context without claiming coverage of individual observations. |
| **Factors controlling margin of error** | For a fixed interval method, a larger confidence level increases the critical value, greater observation variability increases uncertainty, and larger comparable independent samples reduce standard error proportionally to $1/\sqrt n$. Sampling margins do not account for all bias or measurement error. | Analyze effects of sample size, confidence level, and variability on interval precision. | Hold other factors fixed for each comparison, distinguish statistical precision from accuracy, and explain why a narrow biased interval is not trustworthy. |
