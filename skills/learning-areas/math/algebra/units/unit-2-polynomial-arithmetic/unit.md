# Unit 2: Polynomial arithmetic

Polynomial structure supports exact arithmetic, identities, and interpretation of quantities and coefficients.

## Main learning objectives

- Classify polynomial formulas while retaining any restrictions from their original expressions. Distinguish terms, coefficients, constants, and factors.
- Classify polynomials by nonzero term count after expansion and collection of like terms. Determine total degree and identify one-variable leading terms and coefficients.
- Translate between standard form and complete coefficient lists. Evaluate polynomials with correct signs and operation order, preserving equivalent representations.
- Compute polynomial sums and differences, including degrees greater than two. Explain closure under addition and subtraction and degree changes caused by cancellation.
- Multiply monomials with correct coefficient and exponent rules. Expand polynomial products through complete distribution.
- Expand binomial squares and justify doubled cross terms. Expand conjugate products and justify cross-term cancellation.
- Prove polynomial identities by symbolic transformation and coefficient comparison. Disprove false identities and explain the limits of numerical evidence.

## Concepts and topics

What is a polynomial?; Terms, coefficients, constants, and factors; Interpreting quantities from polynomial structure; Monomials, binomials, and trinomials; Degree, leading term, and leading coefficient; Writing a polynomial in standard form; Evaluating polynomial expressions; Adding polynomials and closure; Subtracting polynomials and additive inverses; Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial; Multiplying binomials and general polynomials; Squares of binomials; Products of conjugate binomials; Proving and disproving polynomial identities; Identities and numerical relationships.

- [2.6: Special products](lesson-6-special-products/lesson.md) — Recognize binomial squares and conjugate products as applications of distribution. Connect their cross-term behavior to exact symbolic and numerical calculations.
- [2.7: Polynomial identities and equivalence](lesson-7-polynomial-identities-and-equivalence/lesson.md) — Distinguish identities from equations that hold only at particular inputs and establish polynomial equivalence symbolically. Use a proven identity to generate numerical relationships and state their conditions.
