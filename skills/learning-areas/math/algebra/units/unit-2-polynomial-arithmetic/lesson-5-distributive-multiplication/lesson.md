# Lesson 2.5: Distributive multiplication

Build polynomial multiplication from monomial products and the distributive property. Extend the same reasoning from a single term to factors with any finite number of terms.

## Learning objectives

- Multiply monomials with correct coefficient and exponent rules.
- Expand polynomial products through complete distribution.
- Explain multiplication closure and use degree and leading coefficient to detect possible errors.

## Learning outcome

Expand polynomial products completely and justify the result through distribution and structural checks.

## Proficiency criteria

- Distinguish exponent addition in multiplication from coefficient addition for like terms.
- Account for every term pairing, including negative and constant terms.
- Collect partial products without omission or duplication.
- Explain closure and verify nonzero-product degree and leading coefficient while recognizing the limits of those checks.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial** | Monomial multiplication multiplies coefficients and adds exponents of each matching variable: $x^mx^n=x^{m+n}$ for nonnegative integers $m,n$. Distribution applies the monomial factor to every term, including constants. In one variable, nonzero factors have product degree equal to the sum of their degrees and leading coefficient equal to the product of their leading coefficients; a zero factor gives the zero polynomial. | Multiply monomials using nonnegative integer exponent laws, keeping coefficient and exponent operations distinct. Distribute a monomial across a polynomial, collect the product in standard form, and check its degree and leading coefficient when nonzero. | Multiply coefficients and add exponents only for matching variables. Apply the factor to every term with correct signs. Present the collected product in standard form and apply degree and leading-coefficient checks only when both factors are nonzero. |
| **Multiplying binomials and general polynomials** | Every term of one polynomial multiplies every term of the other. Factors with $m$ and $n$ nonzero terms give $mn$ partial products before collection. Like partial products combine by coefficient addition. Finite distribution and nonnegative exponent sums establish closure. Nonzero one-variable product degrees add and leading coefficients multiply; these checks can reveal errors but do not verify every coefficient. | Multiply binomials and polynomial factors with three or more terms through complete distribution. Justify multiplication and closure through distribution, and use degree and leading-coefficient checks with their stated limitations. | Generate every term pairing once and collect only like partial products. Explain closure from finite distribution and permitted exponents. Check degree and leading coefficient for nonzero factors without treating agreement as complete verification. |
