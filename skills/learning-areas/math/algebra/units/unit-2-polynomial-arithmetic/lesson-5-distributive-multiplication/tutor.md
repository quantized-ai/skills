## Reasoning activity

Use after the relevant basic procedure is accessible. Reveal the prompt first, let the student identify and repair the reasoning, and close with the correct explanation. In assess mode generate a new task instead of administering this exposed example.

**Prompt:** A learner expands (x+2)(x²+1) as x³+2. Which products are absent?

**Private reasoning key:** The four products are x³, x, 2x² and 2. The correct collected result is x³+2x²+x+2; the two interior contributions were omitted.

Ask what changed between the original claim and the repaired explanation. If the response is only a guessed answer, use the corresponding concept’s targeted cue below; after feedback, collect a new independent attempt.

## Concept guidance

### Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial

Curriculum reference: **Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial** in the [curriculum concept table](lesson.md#concepts). Read that row’s Content, Learning Objectives, and Proficiency criteria before using these activities.

#### Calibration and worked reasoning

**Diagnostic prompt:** Multiply $-3x^2y(2xy^2-4)$.

**Agent key:** $-6x^3y^3+12x^2y$; multiply coefficients and add exponents for each matching variable.

**Worked example:** Predict and then check degree and leading coefficient of $2x^3(-4x^2+x-1)$.

**Worked reasoning:** Product $-8x^5+2x^4-2x^3$ has degree 5 and leading coefficient $-8$, as predicted for nonzero factors.


#### Teaching sequence

Multiply numerical coefficients separately from variable powers. For each variable, count or add exponents across multiplication only. Distribute the whole monomial to every signed term and collect the final result. Predict degree and leading coefficient for nonzero factors, then check against the product; introduce a zero factor as the case where that prediction has no degree meaning.

#### Respond to student reasoning

**First hint:** Has the outside factor reached every term?

If a factor reaches only the first term, use two additive boxes and draw one multiplication to each. If unlike variables' exponents are combined, keep separate x and y columns. If degree addition is applied to zero, ask for its leading term.

Give only the cue relevant to the observed error; wait for a revision before showing a worked step. Record any mathematical help as assistance.

#### Practice progression

Use monomial products, signed distribution and multiple variables before reverse missing-factor tasks. Include zero and unit coefficients deliberately.

#### Assessment evidence

Require all distributed terms, correct matching-variable exponents, standard form, and justified degree/leading checks with their nonzero hypotheses.

Use the [fresh-question policy](../question-generation.md); the two calibration examples above are exposed learning material, not the default quiz. Collect the curriculum's full proficiency evidence, retain demonstrated components, and reassess unresolved cases with new tasks.

### Multiplying binomials and general polynomials

Curriculum reference: **Multiplying binomials and general polynomials** in the [curriculum concept table](lesson.md#concepts). Read that row’s Content, Learning Objectives, and Proficiency criteria before using these activities.

#### Calibration and worked reasoning

**Diagnostic prompt:** Expand $(x-2)(x^2+3x+4)$.

**Agent key:** Six partial products collect to $x^3+x^2-2x-8$.

**Worked example:** A proposed product for $(2x+1)(x-3)$ is $2x^2-6$. Can its degree and leading term certify it?

**Worked reasoning:** No. Correct distribution gives $2x^2-5x-3$; matching degree and leading term misses other coefficients.


#### Teaching sequence

Arrange one factor's terms across columns and the other's down rows. Fill every cell once, then collect diagonals or matching powers; this explains distribution beyond two binomials. The worked false product has the expected degree but misses its cross-term coefficient. Compare every coefficient and explain closure through finitely many products of permitted powers.

#### Respond to student reasoning

**First hint:** How many term pairs must be multiplied?

If four-product FOIL is used for a trinomial, count the required row-column pairs before expanding. If leading-term agreement is treated as proof, inspect the linear and constant coefficients separately.

Give only the cue relevant to the observed error; wait for a revision before showing a worked step. Record any mathematical help as assistance.

#### Practice progression

Progress from two binomials to a binomial times a trinomial, then general short polynomials with cancellation. Ask the student to locate a missing cell in an incomplete multiplication grid.

#### Assessment evidence

Assess complete pairing, signed collection, polynomial closure and a full reconstruction check. Degree and leading coefficient are useful error detectors but never the sole verification.
