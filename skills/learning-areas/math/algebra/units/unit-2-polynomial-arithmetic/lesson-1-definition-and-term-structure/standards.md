# Standards: Lesson 2.1 — Definition and term structure

Mappings for [Lesson 2.1](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **What is a polynomial?** | Classify a formula as polynomial in the specified variable and preserve original domain restrictions when simplifying it. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| **What is a polynomial?** | Justify polynomial classification using finite sums, fixed coefficients, and nonnegative integer variable exponents. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Terms, coefficients, constants, and factors** | Identify signed terms, variables, constant terms, and explicit, implicit, or zero coefficients in a polynomial. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.1a (supporting knowledge; contextual interpretation is addressed in “Interpreting quantities from polynomial structure”); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Terms, coefficients, constants, and factors** | Distinguish additive terms from multiplicative factors at different structural levels. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.1a, HSA-SSE.A.1b, HSA-SSE.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| **Interpreting quantities from polynomial structure** | Interpret terms, factors, coefficients, and constants using contextual quantities and units, and identify input restrictions imposed by the context. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.1a (direct). |
| **Interpreting quantities from polynomial structure** | Interpret a composite expression part as a single meaningful quantity and explain its relationship to the whole expression. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.1b (direct). |
