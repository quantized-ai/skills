# Lesson 45.1: Statistical questions and study design

A statistical investigation connects a variable question to a population, data-collection design, and defensible analysis.

## Learning objectives

- Formulate a statistical question and design and carry out a coherent data-collection and analysis plan.
- Compare and implement sampling methods and explain how selection and measurement limitations affect generalization.

## Proficiency criteria

- Connect the question, population, variables, collection method, actual data, and analysis; distinguish association and population estimation from causal aims.
- Identify the actual selection mechanism and sampling frame, preserve randomization where claimed, and explain each proposed repair in relation to a specific bias.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Questions, variables, and a collection plan** | A statistical question anticipates variation among cases. A study specifies its target population, observational units, variables and measurement definitions, required data, collection mechanism, and intended comparison. A survey may be observational; an experiment assigns treatments. | Formulate a statistical question and design and carry out a coherent data-collection and analysis plan. | Connect the question, population, variables, collection method, actual data, and analysis; distinguish association and population estimation from causal aims. |
| **Sampling techniques and selection bias** | Simple random, stratified, cluster, systematic, and convenience samples differ in selection mechanisms and feasible inference. Random sampling differs from voluntary participation; undercoverage, nonresponse, and wording effects are not remedied solely by increasing sample size. | Compare and implement sampling methods and explain how selection and measurement limitations affect generalization. | Identify the actual selection mechanism and sampling frame, preserve randomization where claimed, and explain each proposed repair in relation to a specific bias. |
