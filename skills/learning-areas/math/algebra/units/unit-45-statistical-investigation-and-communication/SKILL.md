## Unit safeguards

Do not fabricate data collection, random assignment, a spoken presentation, or observations. Distinguish proposed design, supplied/simulated data and actually executed investigation. Random assignment supports causality under assumptions; random sampling supports generalization under assumptions. Neither implies the other.
