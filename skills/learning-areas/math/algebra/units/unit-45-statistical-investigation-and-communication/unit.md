# Unit 45: Statistical investigation and communication

Study design, variability, and evidence-based communication define the complete statistical investigation process.

## Main learning objectives

- Formulate a statistical question and design and carry out a coherent data-collection and analysis plan.
- Distinguish deterministic and statistical models and construct a model that represents sources of variability around a systematic relationship.
- Communicate an investigation in written and oral forms with justified methods, clear displays, and contextual conclusions.

## Concepts and topics

Questions, variables, and a collection plan; Sampling techniques and selection bias; Deterministic structure and random variation; Random assignment, replication, and blocking; Statistical reporting; Critical evaluation of published findings.

## Lessons

- [45.1: Statistical questions and study design](lesson-1-statistical-questions-and-study-design/lesson.md) — A statistical investigation connects a variable question to a population, data-collection design, and defensible analysis.
- [45.2: Variability and experimental controls](lesson-2-variability-and-experimental-controls/lesson.md) — Statistical models combine systematic relationships with variation whose sources determine what can be controlled.
- [45.3: Reports and defensible conclusions](lesson-3-reports-and-defensible-conclusions/lesson.md) — A statistical report communicates evidence, uncertainty, and limitations in language suited to its audience.
