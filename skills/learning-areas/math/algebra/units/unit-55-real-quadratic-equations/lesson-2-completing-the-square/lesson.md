# Lesson 55.2: Completing the square

A controlled change to both sides rewrites a quadratic equation as a square and exposes its real solutions.

## Learning objectives

- Complete the square in a monic quadratic equation and solve for all real roots.
- Complete the square with any nonzero real leading coefficient and justify the resulting equivalent equation.

## Proficiency criteria

- Halve the signed linear coefficient, square it, maintain equality, and classify positive, zero, and negative isolated-square cases.
- Normalize coefficients consistently, retain rational values exactly, account for outside scaling, and recover and verify the full real solution set.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Monic square completion** | $x^2+bx=(x+b/2)^2-(b/2)^2$ for real $b$. Adding $(b/2)^2$ to both sides preserves an equation; inserting a term into an expression requires compensating for it. | Complete the square in a monic quadratic equation and solve for all real roots. | Halve the signed linear coefficient, square it, maintain equality, and classify positive, zero, and negative isolated-square cases. |
| **Nonmonic square completion** | For $a\ne0$, divide $ax^2+bx+c=0$ by $a$ or factor $a$ from the quadratic and linear terms before completing the square. An adjustment inside a factored bracket contributes $a$ times that adjustment to the original expression. | Complete the square with any nonzero real leading coefficient and justify the resulting equivalent equation. | Normalize coefficients consistently, retain rational values exactly, account for outside scaling, and recover and verify the full real solution set. |
