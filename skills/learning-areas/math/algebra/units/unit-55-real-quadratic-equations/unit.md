# Unit 55: Real quadratic equations

Equivalent transformations produce complete real solution sets through factoring, square roots, square completion, and the quadratic formula.

## Main learning objectives

- Solve real quadratic equations by factoring and verify all distinct solutions.
- Complete the square in a monic quadratic equation and solve for all real roots.
- Derive the quadratic formula by completing the square and apply it to obtain exact real solutions.
- Select and compare factoring, square-root, square-completion, formula, and graphical methods for real quadratic equations.

## Concepts and topics

Quadratic equations and the zero-product property; Square-root property; Monic square completion; Nonmonic square completion; Derivation and use of the quadratic formula; Discriminant and solution classification; Comparing exact and graphical solutions; Quadratic constraints in context.
