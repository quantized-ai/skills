# Lesson 6.6: Conjugate roots and polynomial construction

Use conjugation to identify required partner roots and construct real polynomial factors. Build least-degree polynomials from zeros, multiplicities, and enough information to determine scale.

## Learning objectives

- Justify conjugate roots and equal multiplicities for real-coefficient polynomials.
- Form real quadratic factors from conjugate pairs.
- Construct, normalize, and verify least-degree polynomials, recognizing insufficient or incompatible data.

## Learning outcome

The student can construct a uniquely specified least-degree polynomial and justify every required factor.

## Proficiency criteria

- Invoke conjugate pairing only with the required real-coefficient condition.
- Include all given multiplicities and any forced conjugate partners.
- Use minimal degree to determine the factor structure and independent data to determine scale.
- Expand or evaluate to verify the construction and explain why insufficient data leave multiple choices.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Conjugate roots of real-coefficient polynomials** | For real-coefficient polynomials, conjugation preserves evaluation: $p(\overline z)=\overline{p(z)}$. Nonreal roots therefore occur in conjugate pairs with equal multiplicity. For real $a,b$ with $b\ne0$, $(x-a-bi)(x-a+bi)=(x-a)^2+b^2$ is a real quadratic. Pairing is not guaranteed for polynomials with nonreal coefficients. | Justify conjugate partner roots and equal multiplicities for real-coefficient polynomials, and explain why the coefficient condition matters. Form and verify a real quadratic factor from a nonreal conjugate pair. | Use conjugation of sums, products, and factors to justify pairing and equal multiplicity. State the real-coefficient condition. Multiply conjugate linear factors into the correct real quadratic. |
| **Constructing a polynomial from zeros and scale** | Prescribed zeros and multiplicities determine a least-degree product of linear factors up to a nonzero scale. Real coefficients require conjugate partners with matching multiplicities. A specified nonzero leading coefficient, or a nonzero value at a nonroot input, fixes the scale. A zero value at a prescribed root supplies no scale information; a nonzero value there, or a zero value at a nonroot of the least-degree product, is incompatible. Without least degree or a specified degree, extra factors may remain possible. | Construct and verify a least-degree polynomial with specified zeros and multiplicities, including conjugate partners when real coefficients are required. Determine the scale from a leading coefficient or suitable supplied value and explain when the conditions do or do not determine a unique polynomial. | Include every prescribed multiplicity and any forced conjugate partner. Establish the least-degree factor structure without adding factors. Determine a nonzero scale from independent data and verify all supplied conditions. Explain insufficient or incompatible normalization data. |
