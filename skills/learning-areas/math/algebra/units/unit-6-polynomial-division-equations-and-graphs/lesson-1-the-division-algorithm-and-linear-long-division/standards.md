# Standards: Lesson 6.1 — The division algorithm and linear long division

Mappings for [Lesson 6.1](lesson.md).

CCSS denotes the Common Core State Standards for Mathematics. TEKS denotes the Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School (October 2015 snapshot): A is §111.39(c), 2A is §111.40(c), and AR is §111.48(c). Direct addresses an explicit component; supporting knowledge enables the standard without completing its full action; extension exceeds its stated scope. Partial qualifications and official (+) advanced-content markers are retained.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quotient, remainder, and division identity** | Express polynomial division as $p(x)=d(x)q(x)+r(x)$ with the required divisor and remainder conditions, including exact division and lower-degree dividends. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
| **Quotient, remainder, and division identity** | Convert the division identity to $p/d=q+r/d$ and distinguish its domain from the identity’s domain. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Long division by a linear polynomial** | Divide linear, quadratic, cubic, and quartic polynomials by linear polynomials using long division. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct); TEKS A.10(C) (direct: linear and quadratic dividends). |
| **Long division by a linear polynomial** | Verify the quotient and remainder by reconstructing the dividend. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
