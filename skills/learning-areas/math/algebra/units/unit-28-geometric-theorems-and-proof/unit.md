# Unit 28: Geometric theorems and proof

Deductive arguments establish angle, triangle, and quadrilateral relationships and their useful converses. Polygon relationships and triangle centers extend those arguments to connected families of figures.

## Main learning objectives

- Prove and apply theorems about intersecting and parallel lines, triangle angles, midsegments, and centers.
- Prove quadrilateral properties and use sufficient conditions to classify parallelograms and their special cases.
- Derive polygon angle relationships and relate triangle sides to angle sizes and existence.

## Concepts and topics

Vertical and transversal angles, perpendicular-bisector locus, triangle angle sum, isosceles triangles, medians and centroid, circumcenter and incenter, parallelograms, rectangles, rhombi, squares, polygon angle sums, and triangle inequalities.
