# Lesson 19.1: Ratios and unit rates

Ratios compare quantities multiplicatively, and unit rates express the comparison for one unit of a selected quantity.

## Learning objectives

- Represent and interpret part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios.
- Compute and compare rates with compatible units.

## Proficiency criteria

- Preserve the order and meaning of the compared quantities.
- Divide by the selected nonzero reference quantity and report the resulting compound units.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Ratios and equivalent comparisons** | A ratio \(a:b\) compares two quantities in a specified order; an equivalent ratio multiplies both quantities by the same nonzero scale factor. Part-to-part and part-to-whole ratios refer to different denominators. Equivalent-ratio tables preserve multiplicative relationships, including with fractional quantities. | Construct and interpret ratios and equivalent-ratio tables while distinguishing part-to-part from part-to-whole comparisons. | Label both quantities, preserve their order, use one common scale factor, and distinguish an additive change from a ratio-preserving change. |
| **Unit rates and rate comparisons** | A rate \(a/b\), with \(b\ne0\), describes the first quantity per unit of the second. Rates may compare like or unlike units and include ratios of fractions. Reciprocal rates change both the value and the units. Meaningful comparisons require compatible reference units and consistent orientation. | Compute unit rates from rational quantities and compare rates in contexts such as price, speed, and productivity. | Identify which quantity is per one unit, simplify the numerical ratio and units together, and convert incompatible units before comparing. |
