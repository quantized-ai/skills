# Unit 19: Ratios, proportional reasoning, and measurement

This unit links ratios and rates to proportional equations, percent change, and the units that give quantitative results meaning.

## Main learning objectives

- Represent ratios, unit rates, and direct proportional relationships using equivalent ratios, tables, graphs, and equations.
- Solve multistep proportional and percent relationships, including reverse-percent and simple-interest situations.
- Convert and compare measurements with compatible units and correct scale factors.

## Concepts and topics

Ratios; unit rates; equivalent ratios; direct variation; proportions; percent; simple interest; percent change and error; unit conversions; length, area, and volume scale.

## Lessons

- [19.1: Ratios and unit rates](lesson-1-ratios-and-unit-rates/lesson.md) — Ratios compare quantities multiplicatively, and unit rates express the comparison for one unit of a selected quantity.
- [19.2: Proportions and direct variation](lesson-2-proportions-and-direct-variation/lesson.md) — Equivalent ratios produce proportional equations, while direct variation connects constant ratios to tables and origin-passing lines.
- [19.3: Percent relationships and change](lesson-3-percent-relationships-and-change/lesson.md) — Percent expresses a ratio per hundred and supports comparisons, multiplicative changes, and reverse calculations.
- [19.4: Measurement units and scale](lesson-4-measurement-units-and-scale/lesson.md) — Measurement conversions and dimensional relationships preserve the meaning of quantities as their numerical representations change.
