## Teacher resources actually consulted

- [Mathematics Assessment Project, A Brief Guide to the Classroom Challenges](https://www.map.mathshell.org/docs/map_cc_teacher_guide.pdf), University of Nottingham and UC Berkeley/MARS. Its formative-assessment approach informed the probe–feedback–revision cycle and the request for explanations before correction. The tutor uses original tasks; no lesson sheets, card sets, diagrams or sample student work were copied or adapted.
- [IES What Works Clearinghouse, Teaching Strategies for Improving Algebra Knowledge in Middle and High School Students](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/20). The official overview recommends analyzing solved reasoning, attending to algebraic structure and choosing strategies intentionally. The overview rates the first two recommendations minimal evidence and the third moderate evidence. We read the overview, not the full guide in this authoring pass. Applying those broad ideas to this unit and an AI conversation is a local design decision, not a demonstrated effectiveness claim.

## Mathematical cross-checks

- [OpenStax Precalculus 2e §9.5, Matrices and Matrix Operations](https://openstax.org/books/precalculus-2e/pages/9-5-matrices-and-matrix-operations). Dimensions, row-column products and ordered matrix arithmetic; transformation and inverse examples are original extensions within the curriculum.

## What is original and what remains untested

All diagnostic questions, numerical choices, worked keys, variation constraints and reviewer scenarios here are original implementations of the existing curriculum. Links to published resources acknowledge consultation; source prose, copyrighted exercises and images are not redistributed. Where a published overview omits a condition, the precise curriculum condition controls and the worked mathematics is checked independently.

Paired curriculum/tutor loading, fresh-question history, hint levels and the two-independent-task minimum are local agent-design choices. They have not been validated as a student-mastery measure. Static mathematical and navigation checks cannot demonstrate successful skill activation, reliable live question generation, long-term learning or retention. Required graphing, construction, simulation or technology evidence must be observed during use rather than inferred from these files. Existing standards mappings are retained; these teaching references are not standards and do not recertify official alignment.

## Concrete application in this unit

The MAP teacher guide’s formative approach is implemented in each lesson’s original error-analysis activity: elicit a reason, test a specific claim, provide misconception-focused feedback and invite revision. These are individual-tutoring adaptations; the guide’s classroom group arrangements are not claimed to have been reproduced. The IES overview informs worked-reasoning analysis and later method comparison after a first method is accessible; it does not establish the effectiveness of these AI instructions.

The concept diagnostics now isolate a different small decision from each worked example. Teaching sequences state the actual derivation or representation steps; practice changes the operation direction, assumptions or cases; assessment lists the curriculum’s explicit proficiency conditions. The chosen source role is therefore specific to an instructional decision rather than a decorative list of links.
