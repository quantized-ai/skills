## Reasoning activity

Use after the relevant basic procedure is accessible. Reveal the prompt first, let the student identify and repair the reasoning, and close with the correct explanation. In assess mode generate a new task instead of administering this exposed example.

**Prompt:** Is cos(5π/6)=√3/2 because its reference angle is π/6?

**Private reasoning key:** The magnitude is √3/2, but quadrant II has a negative x-coordinate. The correct cosine is −√3/2.

Ask what changed between the original claim and the repaired explanation. If the response is only a guessed answer, use the corresponding concept’s targeted cue below; after feedback, collect a new independent attempt.

## Concept guidance

### Special triangles

Curriculum reference: **Special triangles** in the [curriculum concept table](lesson.md#concepts). Read that row’s Content, Learning Objectives, and Proficiency criteria before using these activities.

#### Calibration and worked reasoning

**Diagnostic prompt:** Derive exact sine and cosine at π/4 from a right triangle.

**Agent key:** Equal legs 1 give hypotenuse √2; both ratios are $1/\sqrt2=\sqrt2/2$.

**Worked example:** Give exact sine, cosine, and tangent at π/6.

**Worked reasoning:** The 1:√3:2 triangle gives $1/2,\sqrt3/2,\sqrt3/3$, respectively.


#### Teaching sequence

Derive 45° values from an isosceles right triangle and Pythagoras, then 30°/60° values by bisecting an equilateral triangle. Normalize by the hypotenuse to put the ratios on the unit circle. Retain exact radicals and identify which side is opposite the selected angle before calculating tangent. The two derivations supply values rather than merely a recall table.

#### Respond to student reasoning

**First hint:** Which side lies opposite the chosen angle?

If 30° and 60° ratios are exchanged, point to the shorter opposite leg. If tangent uses hypotenuse, label the ratio's two legs. If a decimal replaces an exact value prematurely, retain the radical relationship.

Give only the cue relevant to the observed error; wait for a revision before showing a worked step. Record any mathematical help as assistance.

#### Practice progression

Derive each triangle, evaluate its acute angles, then explain complementary swaps and exact rationalized equivalents.

#### Assessment evidence

Require both geometric derivations, consistent side roles and all three ratios at the special acute angles. Accept equivalent exact radical forms.

Use the [fresh-question policy](../question-generation.md); the two calibration examples above are exposed learning material, not the default quiz. Collect the curriculum's full proficiency evidence, retain demonstrated components, and reassess unresolved cases with new tasks.

### Reference angles and reflected coordinates

Curriculum reference: **Reference angles and reflected coordinates** in the [curriculum concept table](lesson.md#concepts). Read that row’s Content, Learning Objectives, and Proficiency criteria before using these activities.

#### Calibration and worked reasoning

**Diagnostic prompt:** Find sine, cosine, and tangent at $5\pi/6$.

**Agent key:** Reference angle π/6 in quadrant II gives $1/2,-\sqrt3/2,-\sqrt3/3$.

**Worked example:** Find the unit-circle point at $7\pi/4$.

**Worked reasoning:** Reference angle π/4 in quadrant IV gives $(\sqrt2/2,-\sqrt2/2)$.


#### Teaching sequence

First find a coterminal angle in a known turn, then determine its quadrant and acute reference angle. Use special-triangle magnitudes and coordinate signs separately. Handle axis angles directly instead of forcing an acute reference angle. Verify sine/cosine coordinates satisfy the circle equation and tangent matches their quotient where defined.

#### Respond to student reasoning

**First hint:** What supplies magnitudes, and what supplies signs?

If the reference angle itself is assigned the original sign, separate magnitude from quadrant. If a quadrant III cosine is positive, inspect the x-coordinate. If tangent is evaluated at an axis with x=0, keep it undefined.

Give only the cue relevant to the observed error; wait for a revision before showing a worked step. Record any mathematical help as assistance.

#### Practice progression

Use all quadrants, negative/multiple turns and axis cases, then reconstruct an angle family from signed special coordinates within a specified interval.

#### Assessment evidence

Assess reference-angle selection, exact magnitudes, signs, coordinate pairing and tangent domain. Correct memorized magnitude alone does not show full-angle understanding.
