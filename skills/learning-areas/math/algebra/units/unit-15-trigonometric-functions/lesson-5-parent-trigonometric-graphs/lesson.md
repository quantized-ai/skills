# Lesson 15.5: Parent trigonometric graphs

Parent trigonometric graphs track unit-circle values as the radian input changes. Sine and cosine have bounded repeating cycles; tangent has unbounded branches separated by excluded inputs.

## Learning objectives

- Construct sine, cosine, and tangent graphs from exact unit-circle values.
- Identify and compare domains, ranges, zeros, extrema, and fundamental periods.
- Explain tangent asymptotes, one-sided unbounded behavior, and absence of amplitude.

## Learning outcome

Sketch and compare all three parent trigonometric graphs using their definitions and domain restrictions.

## Proficiency criteria

- Produce smooth sine and cosine cycles with correctly placed quarter-cycle anchors, zeros, and extrema.
- State and compare domains, ranges, and fundamental periods.
- Sketch separate tangent branches with correct zeros, asymptotes, and one-sided unbounded behavior.
- Explain why sine and cosine are bounded and tangent has no finite amplitude.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Sine and cosine graphs** | With radian input, sine and cosine both have domain $\mathbb R$, range $[-1,1]$, amplitude $1$, and fundamental period $2\pi$. Unit-circle coordinates at quarter turns provide anchors for smooth cycles. For $k\in\mathbb Z$, sine zeros occur at $k\pi$, maxima at $\pi/2+2k\pi$, and minima at $3\pi/2+2k\pi$; cosine zeros occur at $\pi/2+k\pi$, maxima at $2k\pi$, and minima at $\pi+2k\pi$. The two graphs have different phase positions. | Construct smooth sine and cosine graphs from exact unit-circle coordinates and extend them periodically. Identify and compare the domains, ranges, zeros, extrema, fundamental periods, and phase positions of sine and cosine. | Construct a full cycle of each function using quarter-turn coordinates. Locate and label zeros and extrema, state domain and range, and identify the least positive period. Extend both graphs smoothly and periodically and distinguish their phase positions. |
| **Tangent graph and asymptotes** | For radian input, $\tan x=\sin x/\cos x$ has zeros $k\pi$ and vertical asymptotes $\pi/2+k\pi$, $k\in\mathbb Z$. Its domain excludes those asymptotes, its range is $\mathbb R$, and its fundamental period is $\pi$. Each branch increases from negative unbounded values at its left endpoint to positive unbounded values at its right endpoint. Branches are separate; tangent has no maximum, minimum, or finite amplitude. | Construct tangent branches using exact values, numerator zeros, denominator zeros, and correct one-sided unbounded behavior. Explain tangent’s domain, range, fundamental period, and lack of extrema or finite amplitude. | Derive zeros and asymptotes from the quotient definition. Sketch exact anchors and correctly oriented branches without connecting across excluded inputs. State domain, all-real range, and least positive period; justify the absence of extrema and finite amplitude. |
