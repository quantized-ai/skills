# Unit 35: Geometric modeling and design

Geometric models turn physical descriptions into measurable shapes, density relationships, and design constraints. Model choice, computation, interpretation, and revision remain connected throughout.

## Main learning objectives

- Build and evaluate geometric models of physical objects and measurements, including composites.
- Use area and volume densities with correct units and justified distribution assumptions.
- Formulate and compare feasible designs under geometric constraints and cost or efficiency criteria.

## Concepts and topics

Idealized shapes, scale and units, measurement precision, composite models, density, capacity, proportional layouts, constraints, objective quantities, feasible dimensions, and model revision.
