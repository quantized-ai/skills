# Lesson 20.2: Substitution and evaluation

Substitution connects algebraic expressions and formulas to numerical values within their permitted domains.

## Learning objectives

- Evaluate expressions and formulas using correctly grouped substitutions.
- Determine whether specified inputs make an expression defined.

## Proficiency criteria

- Replace every occurrence consistently and respect the scope of negatives and exponents.
- Check denominator and real-root restrictions before reporting an output.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Numerical substitution** | Evaluation replaces every occurrence of a variable by its assigned value and applies conventional operation order. Parentheses preserve the meaning of negative or fractional substituted values. A formula can contain several independently assigned quantities and units; the formula itself is not a function until input choices and domain are specified. | Evaluate numerical values of algebraic expressions and formulas at specified real inputs with consistent substitution and units. | Substitute into every occurrence, preserve grouping for negative inputs, and distinguish an exact value from a rounded computation. |
| **Defined and undefined substitutions** | A denominator cannot be zero in real arithmetic. An even root requires a nonnegative radicand for a real value. Restrictions belong to the original expression and are checked before evaluating. A simplified expression may be numerically defined at inputs excluded from the original expression. | Identify whether specified substitutions are permitted and explain undefined real-valued evaluations. | Locate every denominator or even-root restriction, avoid substituting through an undefined step, and preserve original exclusions after a rewrite. |
