# Unit 20: Algebraic expressions and structure

This unit develops the language and equivalence of expressions so that substitution and symbolic rewriting have explicit numerical meaning.

## Main learning objectives

- Translate quantities into expressions and identify their operations, terms, factors, and coefficients.
- Evaluate expressions on their permitted domains using correct substitution and grouping.
- Justify equivalent linear expressions through real-number properties, distribution, and collection of like terms.

## Concepts and topics

Variables and constants; expression structure; verbal translation; substitution; domain exclusions; commutativity; associativity; identities and inverses; distributivity; like terms; equivalence.

## Lessons

- [20.1: Variables and expression language](lesson-1-variables-and-expression-language/lesson.md) — Algebraic notation records operations on quantities and makes the role of each component explicit.
- [20.2: Substitution and evaluation](lesson-2-substitution-and-evaluation/lesson.md) — Substitution connects algebraic expressions and formulas to numerical values within their permitted domains.
- [20.3: Properties and distributive structure](lesson-3-properties-and-distributive-structure/lesson.md) — Real-number properties justify rearranging and regrouping expressions without changing their values.
- [20.4: Like terms and equivalent linear expressions](lesson-4-like-terms-and-equivalent-linear-expressions/lesson.md) — Combining like terms exposes linear structure while the definition of equivalence distinguishes a justified identity from agreement at selected inputs.
