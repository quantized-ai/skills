# Unit 4: Complex numbers and complex quadratic equations

Complex arithmetic extends the real number system, gives geometric representations of arithmetic, and supplies the nonreal solutions of real-coefficient quadratic equations.

## Main learning objectives

- Simplify principal square roots of negative real numbers using the imaginary unit and valid radical rules. Distinguish a principal square root from the complete solution set of a square equation.
- Simplify nonnegative integer powers of the imaginary unit and justify their four-term cycle. Add and subtract complex numbers in standard form.
- Multiply complex numbers and explain the real and imaginary contributions. Identify conjugates and justify their real products.
- Divide by a nonzero complex number using its conjugate and justify preservation of the quotient. Compute modulus and relate its square to a conjugate product.
- Solve real-coefficient quadratics with negative discriminant in standard complex form. Explain conjugate pairing, verify complex roots, and distinguish them from real graph intercepts.

## Concepts and topics

The imaginary unit and negative square roots; Real and imaginary parts of a complex number; Nonnegative integer powers of $i$; Adding and subtracting complex numbers; Multiplying complex numbers; Conjugate products and complex factorization; Dividing complex numbers; The rectangular complex plane; Solving quadratics with complex roots; Choosing and comparing quadratic methods.
