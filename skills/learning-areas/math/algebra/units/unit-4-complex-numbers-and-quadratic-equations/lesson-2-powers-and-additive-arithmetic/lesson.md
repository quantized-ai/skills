# Lesson 4.2: Powers and additive arithmetic

Use the repeating powers of the imaginary unit and componentwise addition to simplify complex expressions. Explain the role of additive inverses when subtraction changes both components.

## Learning objectives

- Simplify nonnegative integer powers of the imaginary unit and justify their four-term cycle.
- Add and subtract complex numbers in standard form.
- Explain exponent reduction, componentwise combination, and subtraction signs.

## Learning outcome

Simplify powers, sums, and differences while keeping real and imaginary components distinct.

## Proficiency criteria

- Derive the cycle from $i^2=-1$ and reduce nonnegative integer exponents modulo four.
- Simplify products and sums involving powers, keeping real and imaginary components distinct.
- Apply the complete additive inverse when subtracting and express results in standard form.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Nonnegative integer powers of $i$** | The powers repeat with period four: $i^0=1$, $i^1=i$, $i^2=-1$, $i^3=-i$, and $i^4=1$. For a nonnegative integer $n=4q+r$ with $0\le r<4$, $i^n=i^r$. Exponent laws apply to products; numerical coefficients remain factors. This concept covers nonnegative integer exponents. | Simplify nonnegative integer powers of $i$ using their four-term cycle. Justify exponent reduction from $i^4=1$ and simplify products involving powers and numerical coefficients. | Derive the cycle from $i^2=-1$. Match each exponent remainder to the correct cycle value. Combine exponents and numerical coefficients correctly and give the result in standard form. |
| **Adding and subtracting complex numbers** | For real $a,b,c,d$, $(a+bi)+(c+di)=(a+c)+(b+d)i$ and $(a+bi)-(c+di)=(a-c)+(b-d)i$. The additive inverse of $c+di$ is $-c-di$. Real components combine with real components and imaginary components with imaginary components; the two components remain distinct. | Add complex numbers and write the result in standard form. Subtract complex numbers and explain componentwise combination and the role of the additive inverse. | Combine corresponding components and retain standard form. Negate both components of the subtracted number. Explain each subtraction sign using the additive inverse. |
