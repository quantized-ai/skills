## Constructive generation and independent validation

**Entry and routing.** Check point coordinates and center/axis conventions before interpreting whole-figure motion. If only one vertex matches, continue checking the correspondence rather than declaring congruence.

**Build tasks deliberately.** Choose simple polygons and apply a known motion sequence to generate exact targets. Include nonorigin centers, arbitrary mirror lines, order-sensitive compositions and inverse tasks. Use an asymmetric polygon when testing orientation so hidden symmetry does not conceal a mistake. For triangle criteria choose valid strict side triples and clearly mark included versus nonincluded angles; include AAA/SSA near-misses and HL without its right-angle hypothesis as rejection cases.

**Verification record.** Check all defining vertices and pairwise lengths; prove invariance when asked for a general result. Reflection fixes its mirror, rotation fixes its center, and inverses undo last operation first. Verify complete symmetry lists modulo 360° and distinguish special from general quadrilaterals.

**Completion gate.** Separate calculating images, describing a full mapping, proving congruence and establishing a triangle criterion. Tracing or dynamic-tool execution requires observed work; a remembered coordinate rule alone does not demonstrate geometric meaning.
