# Mathematical practices and recurring processes

[Unit collection](curriculum.md) · [Standards coverage](standards-coverage.md)

Sources: *Common Core State Standards for Mathematics*, Standards for Mathematical Practice; *Texas Essential Knowledge and Skills for Mathematics, High School*, supplied October 2015 snapshot.

The capabilities below are already specified in lesson objectives and proficiency criteria. These references describe connections among existing requirements; they do not introduce activities, instruments, rubrics, or additional completion rules. The linked companion tables show each lesson’s explicit alignments. The broader practice relationships here are not a claim that every narrow objective by itself fulfills an entire mathematical practice.
