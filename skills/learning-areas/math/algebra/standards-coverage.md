# Standards coverage

[Unit collection](curriculum.md) · [Sources and scope](sources-and-scope.md) · [Reference program](<program/1. common_core_appendix.md>)

The reference pages below preserve the supplied official expectations and link to lesson-level objective mappings. Concept objectives remain authoritative in the lessons, and each adjacent standards table retains direct, supporting, partial, or extension qualifications. A link is evidence to inspect, not a claim that one objective covers every part of a standard or that a learner has met it.

All Common Core high-school content identifiers, including (+) content, have lesson-level mapping evidence in this collection. All nonprocess expectations in the supplied Texas high-school snapshot also have mapped evidence; multipart expectations may require several lessons and retain their qualifications. The [mathematical practices reference](mathematical-practices.md) connects recurring reasoning and process capabilities to their curricular homes. Earlier-grade mappings remain alongside foundational lessons; this index does not claim coverage of all K–8 standards.
