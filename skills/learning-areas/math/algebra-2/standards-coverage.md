# Standards coverage

[Curriculum](curriculum.md) · [Official sources and scope](sources-and-scope.md)

Each concept objective has an individual mapping in the standards.md file beside its lesson. The references below combine standard wording with links to those tables of concepts, learning objectives, and standards. Common Core Appendix A determines the traditional Algebra II pathway; Texas standards supply the additional required content and processes.

## How to read the evidence

**Direct** maps an explicit component; **prerequisite review** revisits earlier content; **supporting knowledge** enables the standard; **extension** goes beyond its explicit bounds. These categories describe alignment, not learner attainment. Combined evidence may be needed for all verbs and conditions in a standard. A `+` marker is separate from required or optional course status.

Every listed Common Core Appendix A content identifier and all 48 Texas Algebra II content expectations retain direct mapped evidence. Parent standards may appear through their explicit children. Reference-only parent wording is included to preserve context.
