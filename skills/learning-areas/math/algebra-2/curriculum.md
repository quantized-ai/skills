# Algebra 2

Algebra 2 develops symbolic reasoning, function analysis, mathematical modeling, and statistical inference. The course contains 17 units and 120 topic-based lessons. Each lesson defines its concepts, learning objectives, and proficiency criteria; the unit pages summarize and organize those lessons.

## Main learning objectives

The following outcomes are derived from the unit objectives. Detailed objectives and proficiency criteria appear in the lessons. Standards mappings are recorded in a standards.md file beside each lesson.

- Represent and compare functions using formulas, tables, graphs, domains, and rates of change; explain transformations and inverse relationships. Units 1 and 5–12.

- Rewrite expressions and solve equations through justified operations with real and complex numbers, while preserving restrictions and checking candidates. Units 2–11.

- Formulate and solve simultaneous constraints using algebraic, graphical, matrix, and numerical methods. Unit 13.

- Analyze discrete and periodic relationships through sequences, finite series, and trigonometric functions. Units 14–15.

- Select, fit, and critique models using quantities, units, data, and meaningful domains. Unit 16 and the function units.

- Use distributions and simulations to evaluate estimates, study designs, uncertainty, and decisions. Unit 17.

## Units

- [1. Function language, transformations, and absolute value](units/unit-1-functions-and-absolute-value/unit.md) — Describe, transform, and interpret functions; solve absolute value conditions and read piecewise rules.

- [2. Polynomial arithmetic](units/unit-2-polynomial-arithmetic/unit.md) — Recognize polynomial structure, perform arithmetic, and justify identities.

- [3. Polynomial factorization](units/unit-3-polynomial-factorization/unit.md) — Select and verify factorizations, then connect factors to polynomial equations and zeros.

- [4. Complex numbers and quadratic equations](units/unit-4-complex-numbers-and-quadratic-equations/unit.md) — Extend arithmetic to complex numbers and choose justified methods for quadratic equations.

- [5. Quadratic functions, inequalities, and parabolas](units/unit-5-quadratic-functions-inequalities-and-parabolas/unit.md) — Connect quadratic forms and graph features, solve inequalities, and describe focus–directrix parabolas.

- [6. Polynomial division, equations, and graphs](units/unit-6-polynomial-division-equations-and-graphs/unit.md) — Use division and factor theorems to find zeros, explain polynomial graphs, and expand binomials.

- [7. Rational expressions](units/unit-7-rational-expressions/unit.md) — Simplify and combine rational expressions while preserving original exclusions.

- [8. Rational functions and equations](units/unit-8-rational-functions-and-equations/unit.md) — Relate rational equations and graphs to holes, asymptotes, domains, and inverse variation.

- [9. Radicals, rational exponents, and root functions](units/unit-9-radicals-rational-exponents-and-root-functions/unit.md) — Apply real-root and rational-exponent rules with their conditions; graph root functions and check equations.

- [10. Exponential functions](units/unit-10-exponential-functions/unit.md) — Construct and compare exponential functions, interpret rates, and solve common-base equations.

- [11. Logarithms](units/unit-11-logarithms/unit.md) — Use logarithms as inverse exponentials, apply valid identities, and solve equations and duration models.

- [12. Function operations, composition, and inverses](units/unit-12-function-operations-and-inverses/unit.md) — Combine functions, determine composition domains, and construct and verify restricted inverses.

- [13. Systems of equations, inequalities, and graphical solutions](units/unit-13-systems-and-graphical-solutions/unit.md) — Solve simultaneous equations and inequalities through algebra, matrices, graphs, and numerical refinement.

- [14. Sequences and series](units/unit-14-sequences-and-series/unit.md) — Connect recursive and explicit sequences, distinguish additive from multiplicative change, and derive finite sums.

- [15. Trigonometric functions](units/unit-15-trigonometric-functions/unit.md) — Extend trigonometry through radians and the unit circle, analyze periodic graphs, and construct periodic models.

- [16. Function models and regression](units/unit-16-function-models-and-regression/unit.md) — Define quantities and constraints, compare function families, fit models with technology, and evaluate predictions.

- [17. Statistical distributions and inference](units/unit-17-statistical-distributions-and-inference/unit.md) — Describe distributions, use simulations for inference, distinguish study designs, and evaluate probabilistic decisions.
