# Standards: Lesson 15.1: Radian measure

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Directed angles and arc length | Interpret radian measure as a directed arc-to-radius ratio and explain its invariance under circle scaling. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.1 (direct). |
| Directed angles and arc length | Represent signed rotations and calculate radius, radian measure, and arc length while distinguishing directed displacement from nonnegative length. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSF-TF.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| Degree conversion and coterminal angles | Convert exactly between degree and radian measure. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.1 (supporting knowledge). |
| Degree conversion and coterminal angles | Find a coterminal representative in a specified full-turn interval and locate its quadrant or axis without equating terminal position with total rotation. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.2 (direct). |
