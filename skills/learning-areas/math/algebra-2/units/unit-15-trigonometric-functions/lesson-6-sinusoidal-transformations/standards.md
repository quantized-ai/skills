# Standards: Lesson 15.6: Sinusoidal transformations

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Amplitude, midline, period, and frequency | Identify nonconstant sinusoidal equations and determine amplitude, midline, range, fundamental period, and cycle frequency with units, justifying period from a full radian cycle. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7e (direct); CCSS HSF-TF.B.5 (direct). |
| Amplitude, midline, period, and frequency | Explain coefficient reflections and distinguish angular frequency from cycle frequency. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSF-TF.B.5 (direct). |
| Phase shift and transformed graphs | Determine phase shift by factoring the radian argument and graph combined sinusoidal transformations with correct quarter-cycle anchors and orientation. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.C.7e (direct). |
| Phase shift and transformed graphs | Recover a sinusoidal equation from graph features and a positional or directional anchor, and recognize equivalent phase and sine/cosine representations. | CCSS HSF-TF.B.5 (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
