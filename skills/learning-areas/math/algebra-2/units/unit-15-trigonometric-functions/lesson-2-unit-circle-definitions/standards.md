# Standards: Lesson 15.2: Unit-circle definitions

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sine, cosine, and tangent as coordinates | Determine sine and cosine from unit-circle points, including axis positions, and connect them to acute-angle triangle ratios. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.2 (direct); CCSS HSG-SRT.C.6 (prerequisite review). |
| Sine, cosine, and tangent as coordinates | Compute tangent where defined and express its complete family of excluded inputs. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.2 (direct). |
| Quadrant signs, periodicity, and symmetry | Use quadrant position and unit-circle reflections to determine ratio signs and justify sine/tangent odd symmetry and cosine even symmetry. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.2 (direct); CCSS HSF-TF.A.4 (+, direct). |
| Quadrant signs, periodicity, and symmetry | Explain the fundamental periods of sine, cosine, and tangent using unit-circle rotations and preserve tangent exclusions. | CCSS HSF-TF.A.4 (+, direct). |
