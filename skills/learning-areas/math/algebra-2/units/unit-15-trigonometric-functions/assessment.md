# Unit 15 assessment

[Unit overview](unit.md)

## Unit cumulative assessment

Five independent tasks, 8 points each, 40 total. Use exact values except where the question supplies measured data.

1. An angle is $-390^\circ$. Convert to radians, find a coterminal representative in $[0,2\pi)$, and determine its sine, cosine, and tangent exactly. **Answer:** $-13\pi/6$, representative $11\pi/6$; $-1/2,\sqrt3/2,-\sqrt3/3$. **Scoring:** 2 conversion, 2 representative/quadrant, 4 values and reference-angle reasoning. **Objectives:** U15-L01-C02-O1–O2; U15-L03-C02-O1–O2.
2. Prove the Pythagorean identity. Given $\cos\theta=-12/13$ in quadrant III, find sine and tangent and explain the signs. **Answer:** substitute circle coordinates into $x^2+y^2=1$; sine $=-5/13$, tangent $=5/12$. **Scoring:** 3 proof, 3 calculation, 2 sign reasoning. **Objectives:** U15-L04-C01-O1; U15-L04-C02-O1–O2.
3. Graph $y=-2\sin((\pi/2)(t-1))+3$ over one cycle. Label period, midline, amplitude, and five anchors. **Answer:** period 4, midline 3, amplitude 2; $(1,3),(2,1),(3,3),(4,5),(5,3)$. **Scoring:** 3 features, 3 anchors, 2 smooth graph/reflection. **Objectives:** U15-L06-C01-O1–O2; U15-L06-C02-O1.
4. Explain tangent’s domain and period using the unit circle. Sketch one branch and explain why tangent has no amplitude. **Answer:** domain excludes $\pi/2+k\pi$; opposite points give the same quotient, so period $\pi$; central branch through $(-\pi/4,-1),(0,0),(\pi/4,1)$, with unbounded range. **Scoring:** 2 exclusions, 2 periodicity reasoning, 2 graph, 2 amplitude explanation. **Objectives:** U15-L02-C02-O2; U15-L05-C02-O1–O2.
5. A periodic quantity has maximum 24 and minimum 8, with peaks at time 3 and 15. Construct a model, predict at time 9, and calculate the residual if the observed value is 9. Explain what would justify extrapolation. **Answer:** $q(t)=16+8\cos((\pi/6)(t-3))$; $q(9)=8$; residual 1; stability over more cycles and appropriate context support extrapolation. **Scoring:** 4 model, 2 prediction/residual, 2 justified limitation. **Objectives:** U15-L07-C01-O1–O2; U15-L07-C02-O1–O2.
