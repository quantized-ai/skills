# Standards: Duration and reasonableness

CCSS refers to the Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to the Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Doubling time and half-life | Calculate a positive doubling time or half-life from an exponential model when the model behavior permits it, retaining time units. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct); TEKS 2A.5(D) (direct). |
| Doubling time and half-life | Explain why fixed proportional-change durations are independent of a positive initial amount. | CCSS HSF-LE.B.5 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| Formulating and validating logarithmic solutions | Formulate an exponential target equation with a defined time variable and solve it logarithmically. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSF-LE.A.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct). |
| Formulating and validating logarithmic solutions | Interpret and verify a target solution using units, permitted time domain, threshold direction, and discrete observation rules. | TEKS 2A.5(E) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct); CCSS HSN-Q.A.3 (direct). |
