## Unit 11 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. Evaluate $\log_2(1/16)$ and solve $\ln(x-2)=3$ with its restriction.

   **Answer and scoring:** First value $-4$ (1); $x-2=e^3$ (1); $x=2+e^3$ (1); domain $x>2$ and substitution checks it (1).

2. Graph $\log_2(x-1)+2$: give three points, domain, range, and asymptote.

   **Answer and scoring:** Points $(1.5,1),(2,2),(3,3)$ (1); domain $(1,\infty)$ (1); range $\mathbb R$ (1); vertical asymptote $x=1$ (1).

3. Condense $\ln(x-1)+\ln(x+1)$ and compare original and condensed natural domains.

   **Answer and scoring:** $\ln(x^2-1)$ (1); original domain $x>1$ (1); condensed natural domain $x<-1$ or $x>1$ (1); equality with original asserted only on $x>1$ (1).

4. Solve $3\cdot10^{2t}=21$ exactly and approximately.

   **Answer and scoring:** Isolate $10^{2t}=7$ (1); $2t=\log7$ (1); $t=\log7/2\approx0.42255$ (1); substitution or magnitude check validates it (1).

5. A quantity is $A(t)=500e^{-0.05t}$, with days as the unit. Find its half-life and first whole-day observation at or below 250.

   **Answer and scoring:** Equation $e^{-0.05t}=1/2$ (1); $t=\ln2/0.05\approx13.863$ days (1); day 14 (1); $A(13)\approx261.02>250$, $A(14)\approx248.29<250$ verifies (1).
