# Standards: Change of base and inverse identities

CCSS refers to the Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to the Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Change-of-base formula | Derive the change-of-base formula and justify its argument, base, and nonzero-denominator conditions. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge). |
| Change-of-base formula | Evaluate logarithms with technology using valid bases and arguments, appropriate rounding, and a nearby-power check. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.4 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| Inverse identities and their domains | Simplify compositions of matching exponential and logarithmic functions and justify the inverse operations. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct). |
| Inverse identities and their domains | Determine and preserve the original input domains in both orders of exponential-logarithmic composition. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct). |
