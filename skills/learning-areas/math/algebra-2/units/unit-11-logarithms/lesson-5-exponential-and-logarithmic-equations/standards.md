# Standards: Exponential and logarithmic equations

CCSS refers to the Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to the Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Solving exponential equations with logarithms | Solve exponential equations with linear exponents by expressing the unknown in logarithmic form. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(D) (direct). |
| Solving exponential equations with logarithms | Determine existence, including constant parameter cases, and verify numerical accuracy, contextual domain, and units of exponential-equation solutions. | TEKS 2A.5(D) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSN-Q.A.1 (direct). |
| Solving logarithmic equations and rejecting invalid roots | Solve logarithmic equations by exponential conversion, valid logarithm properties, or equality of positive arguments in a common base. | TEKS 2A.5(D) (direct); TEKS 2A.5(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct). |
| Solving logarithmic equations and rejecting invalid roots | Determine all original argument restrictions and verify algebraic candidates, rejecting those outside the original domain or failing the equation. | TEKS 2A.5(E) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct). |
