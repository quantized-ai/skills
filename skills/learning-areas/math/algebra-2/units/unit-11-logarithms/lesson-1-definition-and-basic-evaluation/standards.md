# Standards: Definition and basic evaluation

CCSS refers to the Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to the Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Logarithms as exponents | Convert in both directions between equivalent logarithmic and exponential statements. | TEKS 2A.5(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct). |
| Logarithms as exponents | Justify valid real logarithm bases and determine positive-argument restrictions. | TEKS 2A.5(C) (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge). |
| Base 2, common logarithms, and natural logarithms | Interpret common, natural, and explicit-base notation and evaluate recognizable logarithms in bases 2, 10, and $e$. | TEKS 2A.5(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct). |
| Base 2, common logarithms, and natural logarithms | Use technology to approximate logarithms and check their signs and magnitudes against nearby powers. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.4 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(E) (supporting knowledge). |
