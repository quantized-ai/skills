# Standards: Logarithm properties with domains

CCSS refers to the Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to the Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product and quotient properties | Derive and use product and quotient logarithm identities for positive arguments in a common valid base. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| Product and quotient properties | Compare domains when expanding or condensing logarithms and state the precise domain of equivalence. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| Power properties and absolute values | Expand and condense powers of positive logarithmic arguments and distinguish valid power identities from invalid sum rules. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| Power properties and absolute values | Use absolute values or explicit restrictions for even powers and compare original and transformed domains. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.5(E) (supporting knowledge). |
