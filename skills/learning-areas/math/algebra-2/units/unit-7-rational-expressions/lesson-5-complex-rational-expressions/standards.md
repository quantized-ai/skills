# Standards: Lesson 7.5: Complex rational expressions

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 7.5](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Complex fractions by division** | Simplify a complex rational expression by identifying the outer quotient, simplifying its two parts, and using reciprocal multiplication. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.7 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (direct). |
| **Complex fractions by division** | Determine and retain restrictions from all inner denominators and the entire original divisor. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Complex fractions by clearing inner denominators** | Simplify a complex rational expression by choosing an inner LCD and distributing it to the entire numerator and denominator. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.7 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (direct). |
| **Complex fractions by clearing inner denominators** | Justify the nonzero-multiplier condition and explain why simplification retains the entire original domain. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (supporting knowledge). |
