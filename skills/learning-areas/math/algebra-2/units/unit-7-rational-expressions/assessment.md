## Unit 7 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. Simplify $(x^2-x-6)/(x^2-4)$ and state the original domain.

   **Answer and scoring:** Factor numerator $(x-3)(x+2)$ (1); denominator $(x-2)(x+2)$ (1); result $(x-3)/(x-2)$ (1); $x\ne\pm2$ (1).

2. Simplify $[(x^2-1)/x]\div[(x+1)/(x-2)]$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Reciprocal multiplication (1); factor $x^2-1$ (1); result $(x-1)(x-2)/x$ (1); exclusions $0,2,-1$ (1).

3. Compute $1/(x-1)+2/(x+1)$.

   **Answer and scoring:** LCD $(x-1)(x+1)$ (1); numerator $(x+1)+2(x-1)$ (1); answer $(3x-1)/(x^2-1)$ (1); exclusions $\pm1$ (1).

4. Simplify $(1+1/x)/(1-1/x^2)$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Require $x\ne0,\pm1$ (1); clear with $x^2$ (1); factor to $x(x+1)/[(x-1)(x+1)]$ (1); answer $x/(x-1)$ with retained exclusions (1).

5. Rewrite $(x^2+2)/(x-1)$ as quotient plus remainder and explain why it remains rational.

   **Answer and scoring:** Quotient $x+1$ (1); remainder 3 (1); restriction $x\ne1$ (1); multiplication verifies $x^2+2$ and the expression is still a polynomial quotient (1).
