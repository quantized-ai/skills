# Standards: Lesson 7.3: Products and quotients

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 7.3](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplication of rational expressions** | Multiply rational expressions, justify cross-cancellation, and reduce the resulting factors. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.7 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (direct). |
| **Multiplication of rational expressions** | Determine and retain the domain inherited from both original factors. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Division and nonzero divisors** | Divide rational expressions by taking the reciprocal of the entire divisor and simplifying the product. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.7 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(F) (direct). |
| **Division and nonzero divisors** | Identify and retain exclusions from undefined original operands and zero divisor values, including restrictions absent from the final formula. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
