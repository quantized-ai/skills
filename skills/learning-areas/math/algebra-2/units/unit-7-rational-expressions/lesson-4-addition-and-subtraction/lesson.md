# Lesson 7.4: Addition and subtraction

Addition and subtraction combine numerators only after a common denominator is established. Factored denominators identify the least common denominator, while original denominator restrictions survive reduction.

## Learning objectives

- Add and subtract rational expressions with common or unlike denominators, distributing subtraction over entire numerators.
- Construct a least common denominator, justify equivalent-fraction steps, and retain original restrictions after simplification.

## Learning outcome

The student can justify each transformation in a rational sum or difference and state its original domain.

## Proficiency criteria

- Keep a common denominator while combining entire signed numerators.
- Build a least common denominator from each distinct factor at its greatest required multiplicity.
- Multiply each numerator and denominator by the same missing factors.
- Factor and reduce the combined result while retaining exclusions from every original term.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Common denominators** | For $q\ne0$, $p/q\pm r/q=(p\pm r)/q$. Subtraction applies to every term in the following numerator. Simplify the combined numerator, then factor to identify any common factors; the original common-denominator exclusions remain after cancellation. | Add or subtract rational expressions with the same denominator, applying subtraction to the entire following numerator. Simplify and reduce the combined result while retaining all original denominator restrictions. | Confirm a shared denominator before combining numerators; distribute subtraction correctly; cancel only factors; retain every original denominator exclusion. |
| **Least common denominators** | After complete factorization, a least common denominator uses each distinct polynomial factor to its greatest multiplicity among the denominators. Normalize factors that differ by a nonzero constant. For integer-coefficient denominators, first extract each coefficient GCF and include the positive least common multiple of those GCFs. Multiply each numerator and denominator by its missing factors, combine numerators, and reduce on the original common domain. | Construct and justify a least common denominator from factored polynomial denominators, including signs, numerical factors, and multiplicities. Add or subtract unlike-denominator rational expressions using equivalent fractions and retain all original restrictions. | Justify each LCD factor and multiplicity; use matching missing factors in numerator and denominator; combine and reduce correctly; state all original exclusions without adding denominators. |
