# Lesson 7.2: Simplification by factoring

Factoring reveals the multiplicative structure that permits cancellation in rational expressions. Opposite factors require sign changes, and every restriction from the original denominator remains in force.

## Learning objectives

- Simplify rational expressions by factoring and canceling common nonzero factors while preserving signs and restrictions.
- Justify valid reductions algebraically and diagnose invalid cancellation.

## Learning outcome

The student can produce and verify reduced rational expressions using factorization, correct signs, and original domains.

## Proficiency criteria

- Factor numerator and denominator sufficiently to expose all common factors.
- Cancel factors rather than terms; track signs from opposite factors, coefficients, and powers.
- Retain every original denominator exclusion and verify valid reductions algebraically.
- Refute invalid cancellation with structural reasoning or a permitted counterexample.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Canceling factors, not terms** | For $a,c\ne0$, $ab/(ac)=b/c$; this removes a common multiplicative factor, not separate terms in a sum. Factoring numerator and denominator exposes valid cancellations. Reductions retain all original exclusions. Algebra establishes equivalence; a permitted counterexample can disprove it, but checking selected inputs cannot prove an identity. | Simplify rational expressions by factoring numerator and denominator; retain original restrictions and verify equivalence algebraically. Diagnose cancellation across sums and justify its correction through structure or a permitted counterexample. | Expose and cancel common nonzero factors; retain the full original domain; verify the reduced form algebraically; explain or disprove invalid cancellation of terms. |
| **Opposite factors and signs** | Opposite factors satisfy $a-b=-(b-a)$. Extract and combine factors of $-1$ before cancellation; powers of opposite factors contribute the corresponding sign power. Coefficients and variable powers reduce as factors. Original denominator zeros remain excluded after their factors disappear. | Rewrite opposite factors and reduce coefficients and powers to simplify a rational expression. Preserve the correct net sign and all original restrictions through simplification. | Rewrite opposite factors explicitly; track signs through coefficient and power reduction; give the reduced formula with every original denominator exclusion. |
