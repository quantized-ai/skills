# Standards: Lesson 10.3: Exponential graphs

CCSS refers to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent graphs for bases 2, 10, and e | Sketch exponential parent graphs for bases two, ten, and the natural base and identify their key features. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7e (direct); TEKS 2A.2(A) (direct). |
| Parent graphs for bases 2, 10, and e | Compare exponential growth and decay and describe their monotonicity and end behavior with correct directions. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7e (direct); TEKS 2A.2(A) (direct). |
| Transformed exponential graphs | Graph transformations of exponential functions by mapping parent points. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(A) (direct). |
| Transformed exponential graphs | Determine domain, intercepts, range, asymptote, monotonicity, and end behavior after transformation. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7e (direct); TEKS 2A.5(A) (direct). |
