## Unit 10 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. A table at $t=0,2,4$ gives amounts 10,90,810. Build an exponential and interpret the per-unit factor.

   **Answer and scoring:** Equal two-step ratios 9 (1); per-unit factor 3 (1); $A(t)=10\cdot3^t$ (1); initial value 10 and 200% growth per unit (1).

2. Analyze $f(x)=-2\cdot2^x+5$: graph points, domain, range, and asymptote.

   **Answer and scoring:** Points $(0,3),(1,1)$ with decreasing shape (1); domain $\mathbb R$ (1); range $(-\infty,5)$ (1); asymptote $y=5$ approached on the left (1).

3. An amount triples every 6 hours. Write a model from initial value 40 and find the amount after 12 hours.

   **Answer and scoring:** $40\cdot3^{t/6}$ (1); hours stated (1); $A(12)=360$ (1); one-hour factor $3^{1/6}$, not 3 (1).

4. Solve $9^{x-1}=27$ and verify the solution.

   **Answer and scoring:** Rewrite as $3^{2x-2}=3^3$ (1); $2x-2=3$ (1); $x=5/2$ (1); $9^{3/2}=27$ verifies (1).

5. For $A(t)=100e^{0.1t}$, interpret the initial amount and effective per-unit growth, then compare with $100(1.1)^t$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Initial value 100 (1); effective factor $e^{0.1}\approx1.10517$ (1); effective growth about 10.517% (1); greater than the 10% model for $t>0$, not equal to it (1).
