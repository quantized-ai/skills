# Standards: Lesson 10.2: Growth, decay, and construction

CCSS refers to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS refers to Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each learning objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Percent rates and parameters | Write and evaluate growth or decay functions from an initial amount and a percent rate. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.1c (direct); CCSS HSF-LE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct). |
| Percent rates and parameters | Interpret the initial value, multiplier, and percent change relative to the chosen time origin and time unit. | CCSS HSF-LE.B.5 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.C.8b (direct). |
| Constructing a model from points and recursion | Find and verify a model from two observations with distinct inputs and positive outputs, distinguishing a nonconstant exponential from the constant case. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct). |
| Constructing a model from points and recursion | Express integer-step exponential data recursively with an initial value and a valid index range. | TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.A.1a (direct). |
