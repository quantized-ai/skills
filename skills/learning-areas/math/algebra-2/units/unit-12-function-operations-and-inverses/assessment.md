## Unit 12 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. Let $f(x)=\sqrt{x-1}$ and $g(x)=x-3$. Find the formulas and domains of $f+g$ and $f/g$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Sum $\sqrt{x-1}+x-3$ (1); sum domain $[1,\infty)$ (1); quotient $\sqrt{x-1}/(x-3)$ (1); quotient domain $[1,3)\cup(3,\infty)$ (1).

2. For $f(u)=1/(u-1)$ and $g(x)=\sqrt x$, find both compositions and their domains.

   **Answer and scoring:** $f(g(x))=1/(\sqrt x-1)$ (1); domain $[0,1)\cup(1,\infty)$ (1); $g(f(x))=\sqrt{1/(x-1)}$ (1); domain $(1,\infty)$ because its radicand must be nonnegative and denominator nonzero (1).

3. Find the inverse of $f(x)=(x+2)/(x-3)$ and exchange the original domain and range.

   **Answer and scoring:** Solve $y(x-3)=x+2$ (1); inverse $(3x+2)/(x-1)$ (1); original domain excludes 3 and range excludes 1 (1); inverse domain excludes 1 and range excludes 3 (1).

4. Invert $f(x)=(x-4)^2$ on $x\le4$, and verify the composition $f^{-1}(f(x))$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Inverse $4-\sqrt x$ (1); inverse domain $[0,\infty)$ and range $(-\infty,4]$ (1); composition $4-|x-4|$ (1); equals $x$ when $x\le4$, with the sign condition explained (1).

5. Find the inverse of $f(x)=e^{x+1}-2$ and verify both compositions on the correct domains.

   **Answer and scoring:** Inverse $\ln(x+2)-1$ (1); inverse domain $(-2,\infty)$, range $\mathbb R$ (1); $f^{-1}(f(x))=x$ for every real input (1); $f(f^{-1}(y))=y$ for $y>-2$ (1).
