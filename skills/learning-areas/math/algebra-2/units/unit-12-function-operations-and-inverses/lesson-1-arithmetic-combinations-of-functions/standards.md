# Standards: Lesson 12.1: Arithmetic combinations of functions

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 12.1](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard. A **(+)** marker is part of the standard designation and does not make this lesson optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sums, differences, and products** | Construct, evaluate, and interpret sums, differences, and products of functions, distinguishing pointwise multiplication from composition. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1b (direct). |
| **Sums, differences, and products** | Determine combination domains by intersecting original domains and preserve them after simplification. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Quotients of functions** | Construct and evaluate the quotient of two functions in the stated order. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1b (direct). |
| **Quotients of functions** | Determine the quotient domain from both original domains and the nonzero-divisor condition, retaining restrictions after simplification. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
