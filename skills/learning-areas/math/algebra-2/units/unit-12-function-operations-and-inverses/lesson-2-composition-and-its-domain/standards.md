# Standards: Lesson 12.2: Composition and its domain

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 12.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard. A **(+)** marker is part of the standard designation and does not make this lesson optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Composition order and evaluation** | Evaluate compositions from formulas or tables in the correct order, checking that both stages are defined. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Composition order and evaluation** | Distinguish composition from multiplication, compare reversed compositions, and check compatible units in context. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct). |
| **Algebraic composition with restrictions** | Construct and simplify an algebraic composition by substituting the entire inner expression. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct). |
| **Algebraic composition with restrictions** | Determine the composition domain using the inner domain and outer input conditions, retaining inherited restrictions after simplification. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.1c (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
