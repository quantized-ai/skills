# Standards: Lesson 12.6: Inverse function families

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 12.6](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard. A **(+)** marker is part of the standard designation and does not make this lesson optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exponential and logarithmic inverses** | Derive inverses of transformed exponential and logarithmic functions with valid bases, nonzero scales, and explicit domains. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4a (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.5 (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct). |
| **Exponential and logarithmic inverses** | Verify both compositions and explain the reflected domains, ranges, asymptotes, and graph points. | TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.4b (+) (direct). |
| **Inverses of square-root and cubic functions** | Derive inverse formulas for simple transformed square-root and cubic functions and verify both compositions. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.2(B) (direct). |
| **Inverses of square-root and cubic functions** | Determine and preserve inverse domains and ranges, distinguishing a restricted quadratic inverse from an all-real cube-root inverse. | TEKS 2A.2(C) (direct); TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct). |
