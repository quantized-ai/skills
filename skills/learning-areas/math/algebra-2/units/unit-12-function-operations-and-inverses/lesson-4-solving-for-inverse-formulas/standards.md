# Standards: Lesson 12.4: Solving for inverse formulas

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 12.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard. A **(+)** marker is part of the standard designation and does not make this lesson optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Linear inverses and reversal of operations** | Derive the inverse of a nonconstant linear function algebraically and state the exchanged domain and range. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.2(B) (direct). |
| **Linear inverses and reversal of operations** | Interpret the reversal of operations and verify algebraically that the inverse recovers the original input. | TEKS 2A.2(C) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.2(D) (direct). |
| **Simple rational inverses** | Derive the inverse of a nonconstant linear-fractional function and distinguish parameter choices that produce a constant quotient. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.2(B) (direct). |
| **Simple rational inverses** | Determine the original and inverse domains and ranges, exchanging excluded inputs and unattainable outputs. | TEKS 2A.2(C) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
