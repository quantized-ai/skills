# Lesson 8.1: Reciprocal functions and transformations

The reciprocal parent function and its transformations connect formulas, mapped points, symmetry, asymptotic behavior, domain, and range.

## Learning objectives

- Graph the reciprocal parent and its transformations using coordinate mappings.
- Explain branch behavior, symmetry, asymptotes, domain, and range from reciprocal structure.

## Learning outcome

Construct and justify a transformed reciprocal graph whose points, branches, asymptotes, and excluded values agree with its formula.

## Proficiency criteria

- Describe both parent branches, odd symmetry, one-sided behavior near zero, and end behavior.
- Map parent points through inside and outside transformations with correct shifts, scales, and reflections.
- Derive transformed asymptotes and excluded inputs and outputs from the formula.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The reciprocal parent function** | The real function $f(x)=1/x$ has domain and range $\mathbb R\setminus\{0\}$. Positive inputs give positive outputs and negative inputs give negative outputs; $f(-x)=-f(x)$ gives origin symmetry. As $x\to0^+$, $f(x)\to+\infty$; as $x\to0^-$, $f(x)\to-\infty$. As $x\to\pm\infty$, $f(x)\to0$. Its asymptotes are $x=0$ and $y=0$, and neither is crossed by this function. Asymptotes describe limiting behavior; horizontal asymptotes of other functions may be crossed. | Sketch the reciprocal function using points, its asymptotes, and one-sided and end behavior. Explain the domain, range, branch signs, and odd symmetry of the reciprocal function. | Place points and branches on both sides of zero with correct signs. Justify both excluded values and origin symmetry. Describe one-sided unbounded behavior and approach to zero at both ends. |
| **Transformations of reciprocal graphs** | For $f(x)=1/x$, real $h,k$, and real $a,b\ne0$, $g(x)=a f(b(x-h))+k=a/[b(x-h)]+k$ maps a parent point $(u,v)$ to $(h+u/b,k+av)$. Negative $a$ or $b$ produces the corresponding vertical or horizontal reflection. The asymptotes are $x=h$ and $y=k$; the domain excludes $h$ and the range excludes $k$. For this parent, $f(bx)=f(x)/b$, so horizontal scaling/reflection can also be represented by vertical scaling/reflection. | Identify and graph reciprocal translations, stretches, and reflections using coordinate mappings, including equivalent horizontal and vertical scalings. Determine and justify transformed reciprocal asymptotes, domain, and range. | Map parent points with the correct inside and outside changes, including reflections. Explain why the alternative horizontal and vertical scalings give the same reciprocal formula. Derive asymptotes and excluded input and output values from the formula. |
