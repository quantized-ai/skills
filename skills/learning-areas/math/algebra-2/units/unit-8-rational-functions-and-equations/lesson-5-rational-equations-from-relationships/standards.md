# Lesson 8.5: Rational equations from relationships — standards

Mappings use the Common Core mathematics standards and Texas standards.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inverse variation | Identify inverse variation using a constant product, distinguish it from direct variation, and formulate an assumed inverse-variation model from a pair of values. | TEKS 2A.6(L) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (direct). |
| Inverse variation | Solve and interpret inverse-variation equations, including reciprocal scaling, units, and relevant restrictions. | TEKS 2A.6(L) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct). |
| Formulating rational equations and testing reasonableness | Translate constant-rate relationships into rational equations using defined quantities, compatible units, and justified additive-rate assumptions. | TEKS 2A.6(H) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct). |
| Formulating rational equations and testing reasonableness | Solve rational rate equations and verify algebraic validity, contextual restrictions, and reasonableness, including combined versus individual completion times. | TEKS 2A.6(I) (direct); TEKS 2A.6(J) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct). |
