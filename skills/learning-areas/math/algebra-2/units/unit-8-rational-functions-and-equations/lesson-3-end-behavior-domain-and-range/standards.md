# Lesson 8.3: End behavior, domain, and range — standards

Mappings use the Common Core mathematics standards and Texas standards.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Horizontal asymptotes and end behavior | Determine horizontal asymptotes or their absence from degrees and leading coefficients. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (direct); TEKS 2A.6(K) (direct). |
| Horizontal asymptotes and end behavior | Explain end behavior and any claimed direction of approach using an equivalent form or dominant terms, distinguishing asymptotic values from range exclusions. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (direct). |
| Domain and range in three notations | Determine rational-function domains and ranges by testing allowed inputs and attainable outputs, and express both sets in interval, inequality, and set notation. | TEKS 2A.6(K) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| Domain and range in three notations | Account for output values lost through holes rather than inferring range only from asymptotes. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.6(K) (direct). |
