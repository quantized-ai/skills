## Unit 8 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. Analyze $f(x)=-2/(x-1)+3$: give asymptotes, domain, range, and one point.

   **Answer and scoring:** Asymptotes $x=1,y=3$ (1); domain excludes 1 (1); range excludes 3 (1); e.g. $(2,1)$ with correct substitution (1).

2. For $(x^2-4)/[(x-2)(x-3)]$, find the hole, vertical asymptote, and intercepts.

   **Answer and scoring:** Hole $(2,-4)$ (1); vertical asymptote $x=3$ (1); $x$-intercept $(-2,0)$ (1); $y$-intercept $(0,-2/3)$ (1).

3. Solve $2/x+1=3/x^2$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Exclude 0 (1); $x^2+2x-3=0$ (1); candidates 1 and $-3$ (1); both verify in the original (1).

4. Formulate and solve inverse variation with $y=9$ at $x=4$, then find $x$ at $y=6$.

   **Answer and scoring:** $k=36$ (1); $y=36/x$ (1); $x=6$ (1); appropriate nonzero or positive context restriction and check (1).

5. Two processes finish alone in 6 and 9 hours. Formulate and solve for combined time, and judge reasonableness.

   **Answer and scoring:** $1/t=1/6+1/9$ (1); $1/t=5/18$ (1); $t=18/5=3.6$ hours (1); less than each solo time and positive (1).
