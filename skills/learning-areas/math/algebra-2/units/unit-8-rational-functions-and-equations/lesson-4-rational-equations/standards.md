# Lesson 8.4: Rational equations — standards

Mappings use the Common Core mathematics standards and Texas standards.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clearing denominators and checking candidates | Solve rational equations by multiplying every term by an LCD, explain equivalence on the restricted domain, and recognize contradictions or identities. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.6(I) (direct). |
| Clearing denominators and checking candidates | Reject excluded candidates and verify remaining solutions in the original equation. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.6(J) (direct). |
| Multiple solutions and graphical confirmation | Solve rational equations producing quadratics, obtain all real candidates, and classify their validity in the original equation. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.6(I) (direct). |
| Multiple solutions and graphical confirmation | Use graph intersections or a table to verify rational-equation solutions and distinguish valid equal outputs from discontinuity artifacts. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.11 (direct); TEKS 2A.6(J) (direct). |
