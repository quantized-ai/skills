# Lesson 8.6: Optional rational-function extensions — standards

Mappings use the Common Core mathematics standards and Texas standards.

This lesson is an optional extension; a direct mapping for one objective does not make the lesson required.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Slant asymptotes — optional extension | Find a slant asymptote by recognizing the degree relationship and performing polynomial division. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (extension). |
| Slant asymptotes — optional extension | Explain why the function-minus-line difference tends to zero at both ends, while retaining domain exclusions and allowing justified crossings. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (extension). |
| Rational inequalities — optional extension | Solve a simple rational inequality by forming one quotient, retaining original exclusions, and constructing a sign chart or recognizing an identically zero expression. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (extension); CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (extension beyond equations). |
| Rational inequalities — optional extension | Justify endpoint inclusion and exclusion and explain why multiplication by an unknown-sign denominator requires sign cases. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7d (+) (extension); TEKS 2A.6(K) (supporting knowledge). |
