# Standards: Lesson 1.4 — Transformations of functions

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 1.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping labels retain direct coverage, prerequisite review, or supporting knowledge as specified.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Horizontal and vertical translations** | Translate a function formula and graph horizontally and vertically using corresponding-point mappings. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
| **Horizontal and vertical translations** | Recover translation parameters and the translated formula from corresponding points or features. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
| **Reflections and scale factors** | Determine coordinate effects of inside and outside multipliers, including reflections, and explain why zero multipliers require separate treatment. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
| **Reflections and scale factors** | Distinguish horizontal and vertical scaling using corresponding points or a nonsymmetric graph, accounting for concealed symmetry effects. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
| **Combined transformations and experimental checks** | Derive combined transformations to map points, domain, and range and recover parameters when the supplied correspondences determine them. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Combined transformations and experimental checks** | Compare predicted and technology-generated graphs, varying one parameter at a time and resolving discrepancies by substitution. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
