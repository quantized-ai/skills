# Standards: Lesson 1.2 — Sets, intervals, domain, and range

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 1.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping labels retain direct coverage, prerequisite review, or supporting knowledge as specified.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Representing sets of real numbers** | Translate real sets among inequality, interval, and set-builder notation, distinguishing discrete sets from continuous intervals. | TEKS 2A.7(I) (supporting knowledge for domain and range). |
| **Representing sets of real numbers** | Form unions and intersections, including empty and singleton results, with correct endpoint inclusion. | TEKS 2A.7(I) (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSA-REI.B.3 (supporting knowledge). |
| **Domain and range from representations** | Determine domain and range from a table, described graph, or simple formula with a stated domain, accounting for extrema and excluded points. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
| **Domain and range from representations** | Determine the contextually meaningful domain and derive the range from those allowed inputs. | CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (direct). |
