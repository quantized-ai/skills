## Unit 1 cumulative assessment

Use a graphing tool only for Question 3. Award the specified points for mathematical evidence, not merely the final answer.

1. **Function and set notation, 4 points.** For $R=\{(-2,1),(0,1),(3,5)\}$, decide whether $R$ is a function and state its domain and range. Is its domain $[-2,3]$? **Key:** Yes; $\{-2,0,3\}$ and $\{1,5\}$; no, an interval adds unlisted inputs. One point each.
2. **Features and average change, 4 points.** A graph joins $(0,0),(2,6),(5,0)$ by line segments. State its maximum, positive interval, and average rate on $[0,5]$; explain the last answer. **Key:** Maximum $6$ at $2$; $(0,5)$; $0$; equal endpoints do not imply a constant function. One point each.
3. **Transformations, 5 points.** For $f(x)=|x|$, graph $g(x)=-2f(x+1)+4$, label vertex and zeros, and state domain and range. **Key:** Vertex $(-1,4)$, zeros $-3,1$, domain $\mathbb R$, range $(-\infty,4]$. Vertex/shape (2), zeros (1), intervals (2).
4. **Absolute value equations and inequalities, 4 points.** Solve $|2x-3|=5$ and $|2x-3|<5$. **Key:** $x=-1,4$; inequality $(-1,4)$. Two points each, including case logic.
5. **Piecewise notation, 3 points.** Let $p(x)=x+1$ for $x<1$ and $p(x)=4$ for $x\ge1$. Find $p(1)$, describe the boundary markers, and give the range. **Key:** $4$; open $(1,2)$ and closed $(1,4)$; $(-\infty,2)\cup\{4\}$. One point each.

**Interpretation.** Total 20 points. Use concept assessments to diagnose errors: set notation and endpoint errors return to Lesson 1.2; inside/outside parameter errors to Lesson 1.4; union/intersection errors to Lesson 1.7. A high total does not replace evidence on a missed objective.
