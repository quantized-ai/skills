# Standards for lesson 14.3: Geometric sequences

CCSS identifiers refer to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS identifiers refer to Texas standards. Mapping classifications preserve the role of each standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constant ratios and explicit formulas | Identify geometric sequences and construct explicit rules with correct indexing and zero-term exceptions. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct); TEKS A.12(D) (prerequisite review). |
| Constant ratios and explicit formulas | Explain term sign and magnitude behavior from the initial value and the ratio, including constant and zero cases. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.3 (direct); CCSS HSF-LE.A.1a (supporting knowledge). |
| Geometric recursion and percent change | Translate and evaluate geometric rules in recursive and explicit forms with complete indexing, and distinguish sequences from real-input exponential extensions. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct). |
| Geometric recursion and percent change | Construct and interpret constant-percentage-change models using a multiplier on the current amount. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.1c (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (direct). |
