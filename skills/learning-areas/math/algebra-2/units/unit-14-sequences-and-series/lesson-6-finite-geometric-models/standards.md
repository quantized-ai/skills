# Standards for lesson 14.6: Finite geometric models

CCSS identifiers refer to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS identifiers refer to Texas standards. Mapping classifications preserve the role of each standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Totals from repeated proportional change | Construct a finite geometric sum from proportional contributions, specifying inclusion boundaries and any repeated contribution. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct). |
| Totals from repeated proportional change | Evaluate and interpret an accumulated total with correct endpoints, units, and final rounding. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct). |
| Repeated deposits and accumulation timing | Build and evaluate a finite deposit accumulation model from a stated timeline and distinguish deposited principal from modeled growth. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.5(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| Repeated deposits and accumulation timing | Compare beginning- and end-of-period accumulation at a common valuation time, including zero growth. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct). |
