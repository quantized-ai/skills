# Standards for lesson 14.2: Arithmetic sequences

CCSS identifiers refer to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS identifiers refer to Texas standards. Mapping classifications preserve the role of each standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constant differences and explicit formulas | Determine a common difference, construct and verify an explicit arithmetic rule, and explain the limits of finite data. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct); TEKS A.12(D) (prerequisite review). |
| Constant differences and explicit formulas | Calculate indexed terms of an arithmetic sequence, including when the given terms are nonconsecutive. | CCSS HSF-LE.A.2 (direct); TEKS A.12(D) (prerequisite review). |
| Arithmetic recursive and explicit representations | Translate arithmetic sequences between recursive and explicit forms with consistent indices and domains. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct). |
| Arithmetic recursive and explicit representations | Build and interpret an arithmetic model, including its initial value and change per index step. | CCSS HSF-BF.A.2 (direct); CCSS HSF-LE.B.5 (direct). |
