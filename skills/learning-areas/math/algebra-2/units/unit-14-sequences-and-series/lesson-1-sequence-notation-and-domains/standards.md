# Standards for lesson 14.1: Sequence notation and domains

CCSS identifiers refer to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS identifiers refer to Texas standards. Mapping classifications preserve the role of each standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Terms and indices | Interpret an indexed term, evaluate an explicit sequence rule, and represent terms as discrete points. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.2 (prerequisite review); CCSS HSF-IF.A.3 (direct). |
| Terms and indices | Specify a sequence’s integer domain and distinguish an index from its term value. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.3 (direct). |
| Recursive definitions and initial values | Generate terms from a recurrence with sufficient initial values and stated recurrence bounds. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.3 (direct); TEKS A.12(C) (prerequisite review). |
| Recursive definitions and initial values | Identify incomplete recursive definitions and explain how initial values affect the resulting sequence. | CCSS HSF-IF.A.3 (direct). |
