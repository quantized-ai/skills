# Standards for lesson 14.5: Finite sums

CCSS identifiers refer to Common Core mathematics standards; TEKS identifiers refer to Texas standards. Mapping classifications preserve the role of each standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sigma notation and arithmetic sums | Interpret, expand, and evaluate finite sums with correct index bounds, distinguishing individual terms from partial sums. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (supporting knowledge). |
| Sigma notation and arithmetic sums | Derive the arithmetic-sum formula by pairing and apply it with the correct endpoints and term count. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (supporting knowledge; arithmetic sums are not the standard’s direct target). |
| Derivation of finite geometric sums | Derive the finite geometric-sum formula by aligned subtraction and explain its ratio restriction. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct). |
| Derivation of finite geometric sums | Apply finite geometric sums with correct first terms, term counts, and separate treatment of a unit ratio. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.4 (direct). |
