# Standards: Lesson 17.5: Sampling distributions of means

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 17.5](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. Mapping labels retain direct, supporting-knowledge, and prerequisite-review distinctions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Repeated-sample mean variability** | Construct a sampling distribution by recording one mean per repeated random sample of a fixed size and design, and distinguish it from individual-value distributions. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSS-IC.B.4 (direct). |
| **Repeated-sample mean variability** | Explain why sample means vary, how larger comparable random samples reduce variability, and why increased concentration does not remove selection bias. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.4 (direct). |
| **Simulation-based margin of error for a mean** | Construct an empirical resampling model and develop an approximate population-mean margin from a specified percentile of absolute simulated mean errors. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.4 (direct). |
| **Simulation-based margin of error for a mean** | Report and interpret a mean estimate and interval in the original units, explaining approximate repeated-procedure coverage and the model’s representativeness and sampling-design limitations. | CCSS HSS-IC.B.4 (direct); CCSS HSS-IC.B.6 (direct). |
