# Standards: Lesson 17.4: Probability simulation and model checking

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 17.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. Mapping labels retain direct, supporting-knowledge, and prerequisite-review distinctions.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Random trials and empirical frequencies** | Construct a reproducible simulation plan whose generator mapping, complete trial, and replacement or dependence rules match the stated chance mechanism. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.2 (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSS-CP.A.1 (prerequisite review); CCSS HSS-CP.A.2 (prerequisite review). |
| **Random trials and empirical frequencies** | Estimate an event probability from repeated trials and explain run-to-run variation and the effect of increasing repetitions. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| **Consistency of a chance model with observations** | Specify a chance model, statistic, and extremeness rule and use comparable simulated datasets to evaluate the observed result. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.2 (direct). |
| **Consistency of a chance model with observations** | Interpret simulated tail frequencies conditionally on the model without treating unusualness as disproof or compatibility as proof. | CCSS HSS-IC.A.2 (direct). |
