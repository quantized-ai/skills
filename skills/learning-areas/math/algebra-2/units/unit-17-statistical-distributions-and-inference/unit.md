# Unit 17: Statistical distributions and inference

[Algebra 2](../../curriculum.md)

Statistical inference connects sample or experimental evidence to conclusions that remain uncertain. This unit reviews distributions, develops normal models, distinguishes study designs, and uses simulation to examine chance variation, estimate population quantities, and compare treatments. It concludes with critical reading of reports and probability-based decisions. The full Algebra II inference strand is required; statistics is not treated as an optional supplement to the function units.

## Prerequisites

Fractions, percentages, arithmetic means, square roots, basic probability, quantitative displays, and the distinction between association and causation. The opening lessons explicitly review center and spread, while the simulation lesson defines trials, random mechanisms, and empirical frequencies. Use anonymous or constructed data; the numerical datasets in these lessons are instructional constructions rather than claims about observed populations.

## Main learning objectives

- Summarize quantitative data and judge and use an appropriate normal distribution model. [Lesson 17.1](lesson-1-data-distributions-and-summaries/lesson.md), [Lesson 17.2](lesson-2-normal-distribution-models/lesson.md).
- Evaluate sampling and experimental designs and state their justified inference scope. [Lesson 17.3](lesson-3-populations-samples-and-study-design/lesson.md).
- Construct probability simulations and compare observations with specified chance models. [Lesson 17.4](lesson-4-probability-simulation-and-model-checking/lesson.md).
- Develop and interpret simulation-based margins for population means and proportions. [Lesson 17.5](lesson-5-sampling-distributions-of-means/lesson.md), [Lesson 17.6](lesson-6-sampling-distributions-of-proportions/lesson.md).
- Evaluate randomized treatment comparisons, statistical reports, and probability-based decisions. [Lesson 17.7](lesson-7-randomized-treatment-comparisons/lesson.md), [Lesson 17.8](lesson-8-evaluation-of-statistical-reports/lesson.md), [Lesson 17.9](lesson-9-probability-based-decisions/lesson.md).

## Concepts and topics

Concepts connect shape, center, and standard deviation with normal areas; parameters, statistics, sampling frames, and design bias; probability simulation and model consistency; sampling distributions and margins of error; no-effect label randomization and practical effect size; and evidence-based reporting, fair selection, conditional probabilities, and error-cost decisions. Mean uncertainty, individual spread, and proportion uncertainty remain distinct.

## Lessons

- [17.1: Data distributions and summaries](lesson-1-data-distributions-and-summaries/lesson.md): Select and calculate distribution summaries with a stated sample or population convention and meaningful units.
- [17.2: Normal distribution models](lesson-2-normal-distribution-models/lesson.md): Assess normal-model appropriateness and translate standardized areas into estimated percentages or expected counts.
- [17.3: Populations, samples, and study design](lesson-3-populations-samples-and-study-design/lesson.md): Identify populations and statistics, classify studies, and connect specific design weaknesses to targeted repairs.
- [17.4: Probability simulation and model checking](lesson-4-probability-simulation-and-model-checking/lesson.md): Design valid random simulations and interpret empirical frequencies and model-consistency evidence conditionally.
- [17.5: Sampling distributions of means](lesson-5-sampling-distributions-of-means/lesson.md): Construct repeated-sample mean distributions and develop an approximate mean margin under stated sampling assumptions.
- [17.6: Sampling distributions of proportions](lesson-6-sampling-distributions-of-proportions/lesson.md): Simulate proportion variation and report a plug-in margin without confusing counts, percentage points, or coverage.
- [17.7: Randomized treatment comparisons](lesson-7-randomized-treatment-comparisons/lesson.md): Preserve an experiment’s assignment design in a no-effect randomization and qualify the resulting treatment evidence.
- [17.8: Evaluation of statistical reports](lesson-8-evaluation-of-statistical-reports/lesson.md): Trace reported conclusions to their data and design, then rewrite claims whose scope exceeds that evidence.
- [17.9: Probability-based decisions](lesson-9-probability-based-decisions/lesson.md): Verify fair random allocation and compare decisions using conditional probabilities, base rates, and explicit consequences.

## Standards and scope

Common Core Appendix A and the Common Core mathematics standards establish the required normal-model and inference sequence. The (+) probability-decision standards remain required here because Appendix A includes them in Algebra II. Texas Algebra II standards do not provide a direct counterpart for the full inference strand. Formal analytic inference formulas and a full probability course remain outside scope. Simulation reference outputs are approximate; independently repeated runs need not agree digit for digit.
