# Standards: Lesson 13.6: Linear inequalities and feasible regions

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Boundary lines and half-planes | Graph the solution half-plane of a linear inequality in two variables using equivalent forms and correct boundary inclusion. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.12 (prerequisite review). |
| Boundary lines and half-planes | Test boundary and nonboundary points in the original inequality. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.12 (prerequisite review); TEKS 2A.3(G) (supporting knowledge). |
| Intersections of half-planes | Solve systems of two or more linear inequalities graphically and describe bounded, unbounded, empty, or lower-dimensional solution sets. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.12 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(F) (direct). |
| Intersections of half-planes | Identify and verify solution points, including interior, edge, and vertex cases. | TEKS 2A.3(G) (direct). |
| Formulating and interpreting feasible sets | Formulate at least two linear inequalities in two variables with defined quantities, compatible units, and contextual domain restrictions. | TEKS 2A.3(E) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct). |
| Formulating and interpreting feasible sets | Determine and interpret feasible choices using all original inequalities and contextual restrictions. | TEKS 2A.3(G) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct). |
