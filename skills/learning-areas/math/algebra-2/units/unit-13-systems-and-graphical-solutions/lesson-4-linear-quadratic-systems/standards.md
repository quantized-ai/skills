# Standards: Lesson 13.4: Linear–quadratic systems

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formulating simultaneous linear and quadratic constraints | Formulate one linear and one quadratic equation from quantitative constraints, defining variables, units, and contextual restrictions. | TEKS 2A.3(A) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (direct). |
| Formulating simultaneous linear and quadratic constraints | Distinguish a quadratic relation from a function graph and evaluate candidate solutions against both equations, contextual restrictions, and units. | TEKS 2A.3(D) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct). |
| Algebraic solutions and intersection counts | Solve a simple linear–quadratic system algebraically and graphically, recovering every corresponding coordinate. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.7 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(C) (direct). |
| Algebraic solutions and intersection counts | Classify the actual reduced equation, justify its real intersection set, and verify all solutions in both original equations. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.7 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(D) (direct). |
