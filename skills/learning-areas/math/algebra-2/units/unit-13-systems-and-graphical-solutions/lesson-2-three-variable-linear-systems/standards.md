# Standards: Lesson 13.2: Three-variable linear systems

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Formulation and substitution | Formulate a three-variable linear system with defined variables, compatible units, and contextual restrictions. | TEKS 2A.3(A) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (direct). |
| Formulation and substitution | Solve a three-variable system by substitution, recover the ordered triple, and verify every original and contextual constraint. | TEKS 2A.3(B) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.C.6 (supporting extension to three variables). |
| Gaussian elimination and back-substitution | Use reversible Gaussian elimination to transform a three-variable linear system into echelon form. | TEKS 2A.3(B) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.C.5 (supporting extension). |
| Gaussian elimination and back-substitution | Back-substitute and verify a unique solution, or identify contradictory equations and undetermined variables when uniqueness fails. | TEKS 2A.3(B) (direct). |
