# Standards: Lesson 13.1: Solution sets and equivalent systems

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Simultaneous solutions and reversible elimination | Verify a proposed solution and classify a linear system as uniquely solvable, inconsistent, or consistent with infinitely many solutions using equations or geometry. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.6 (prerequisite review). |
| Simultaneous solutions and reversible elimination | Justify reversible equation replacement and explain why discarded constraints can change the solution set. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.5 (direct). |
| Substitution and elimination in two variables | Solve two-variable linear systems using both substitution and elimination across appropriate systems. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.6 (prerequisite review). |
| Substitution and elimination in two variables | Choose an efficient method, verify exact solutions in both original equations, and distinguish them from graphical estimates. | CCSS HSA-REI.C.6 (prerequisite review); TEKS 2A.1(B) (direct process). |
