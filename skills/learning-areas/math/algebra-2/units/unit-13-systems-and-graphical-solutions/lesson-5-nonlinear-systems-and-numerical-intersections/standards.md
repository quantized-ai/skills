# Standards: Lesson 13.5: Nonlinear systems and numerical intersections

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equations as intersections and successive approximations | Explain the equivalence of equal outputs, graph intersections, and difference-function zeros on the common domain, and locate candidate inputs with graphs or tables. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.11 (direct). |
| Equations as intersections and successive approximations | Refine and verify numerical solutions to a stated precision, reject domain or continuity artifacts, and explain limits of sign-change searches and viewing windows. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.11 (direct); TEKS 2A.1(C) (direct process). |
| Quadratic–quadratic systems | Solve systems of two quadratic functions by equating outputs, simplifying coefficients, and recovering all intersection coordinates. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.11 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(C) (extension beyond linear–quadratic systems). |
| Quadratic–quadratic systems | Classify the simplified equation and distinguish identical graphs from systems with zero, one, or two real intersections. | CCSS HSA-REI.D.11 (direct). |
