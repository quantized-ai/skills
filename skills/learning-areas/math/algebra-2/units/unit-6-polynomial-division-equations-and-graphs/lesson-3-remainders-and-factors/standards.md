# Standards: Lesson 6.3: Remainders and factors

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.3](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Remainder Theorem** | Prove the Remainder Theorem from the polynomial division identity and state its linear-divisor scope. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct). |
| **The Remainder Theorem** | Calculate a remainder under division by a monic linear polynomial without full division, distinguishing remainder information from quotient information. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (supporting knowledge). |
| **The Factor Theorem** | Use and justify both directions of the Factor Theorem. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct for cubic/quartic factors). |
| **The Factor Theorem** | Test proposed linear factors and solve and verify coefficient conditions that make them factors. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct). |
