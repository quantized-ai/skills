# Standards: Lesson 6.5: Multiplicity and the Fundamental Theorem

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.5](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplicity of zeros** | Determine root multiplicities from a factorization and distinguish distinct-root counts from counts with multiplicity. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct for cubic/quartic factors). |
| **Multiplicity of zeros** | Justify crossing or touching at a real zero from odd or even multiplicity and local signs. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.3 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.C.7c (direct). |
| **The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra** | State and apply the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra, connecting complex linear factorization to the degree count with multiplicity. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.9 (+) (direct; included in Common Core Appendix A Algebra II). |
| **The Fundamental Theorem of Algebra** | Demonstrate the theorem for every real-coefficient quadratic using all three discriminant cases of the quadratic formula. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.9 (+) (direct; included in Common Core Appendix A Algebra II). |
