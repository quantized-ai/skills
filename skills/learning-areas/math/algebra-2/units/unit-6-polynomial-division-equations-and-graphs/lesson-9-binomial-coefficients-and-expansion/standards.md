# Standards: Lesson 6.9: Binomial coefficients and expansion

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.9](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Pascal's triangle and binomial coefficients** | Generate binomial coefficients using Pascal’s triangle and identify their positions and symmetry through the choice interpretation. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.5 (+) (direct; included in Common Core Appendix A Algebra II). |
| **Pascal's triangle and binomial coefficients** | Explain the adjacent-sum pattern from multiplying by a new binomial factor. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.5 (+) (direct); CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (supporting knowledge). |

| **The Binomial Theorem and selected coefficients** | Find a specified coefficient from the general term without full expansion, justifying the variable-exponent pattern and combining contributions if needed. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.5 (+) (direct). |
