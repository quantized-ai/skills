# Standards: Lesson 6.1: The division algorithm and linear long division

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.1](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quotient, remainder, and division identity** | Express polynomial division as $p(x)=d(x)q(x)+r(x)$ with the required divisor and remainder conditions, including exact division and lower-degree dividends. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
| **Quotient, remainder, and division identity** | Convert the division identity to $p/d=q+r/d$ and distinguish its domain from the identity’s domain. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Long division by a linear polynomial** | Divide cubic and quartic polynomials by linear polynomials using long division. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
| **Long division by a linear polynomial** | Verify the quotient and remainder by reconstructing the dividend. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
