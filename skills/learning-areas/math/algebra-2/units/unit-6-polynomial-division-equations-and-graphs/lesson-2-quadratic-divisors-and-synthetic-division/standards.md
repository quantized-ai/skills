# Standards: Lesson 6.2: Quadratic divisors and synthetic division

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Long division by a quadratic polynomial** | Divide cubic and quartic polynomials by monic and nonmonic quadratic divisors using long division. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
| **Long division by a quadratic polynomial** | Check the remainder-degree condition and verify the quotient and remainder by reconstructing the dividend. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct). |
| **Synthetic division by $x-c$** | Use synthetic division for a monic linear divisor, interpret its quotient and remainder, and verify the division identity. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (supporting method); TEKS 2A.7(C) (direct for relevant division). |
| **Synthetic division by $x-c$** | Explain its relationship to long division, recognize the shortcut’s limitations, and handle nonmonic linear divisors by correct normalization or long division. | CCSS HSA-APR.D.6 (supporting knowledge). |
