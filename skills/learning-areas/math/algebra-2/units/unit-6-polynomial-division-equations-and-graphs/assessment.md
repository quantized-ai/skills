# Unit 6 assessment

[Unit overview](unit.md)

## Cumulative unit assessment

Administer after the concept checks, without worked examples visible. Require intermediate reasoning, not only final answers. For sketches, accept hand-drawn or technology-produced graphs only when the requested features are justified. Optional extension points are reported separately from core scores.

1. **Task:** Divide $x^4+3x^2+2x+5$ by $x^2+1$ and verify.
   - **Expected answer:** $q=x^2+2,r=2x+3$; $(x^2+1)(x^2+2)+2x+3$ reconstructs the dividend.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: algorithm 2, remainder/verification 2.
2. **Task:** For $p(x)=x^3-4x^2+x+6$, show $x-2$ is a factor and solve $p(x)=0$.
   - **Expected answer:** $p(2)=0$; quotient $x^2-2x-3=(x-3)(x+1)$; roots $2,3,-1$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: factor theorem 1, division 1, complete roots 2.
3. **Task:** A monic real quartic has zeros $1$ with multiplicity two and $2i$. Construct it and count distinct versus total complex roots.
   - **Expected answer:** $(x-1)^2(x^2+4)=x^4-2x^3+5x^2-8x+4$; add $-2i$, three distinct roots and four counted roots.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: conjugate/multiplicity 2, construction/count 2.
4. **Task:** Sketch $(x+2)^2(x-1)$, giving signs, intercepts, and tails.
   - **Expected answer:** Touches at $-2$, crosses at $1$, vertical intercept $-4$; negative on $(-\infty,-2)\cup(-2,1)$, positive on $(1,\infty)$; left down/right up.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: zero/shape behavior 1, intercept 1, sign 1, tails/coherent sketch 1.
5. **Task:** Explain the Fundamental Theorem of Algebra and establish its degree-two case; do not claim every root is real or distinct.
   - **Expected answer:** A degree-$n$ complex polynomial has $n$ complex roots counting multiplicity. For real-coefficient quadratics, the quadratic formula gives two real roots, a repeated real root, or a nonreal pair according to the discriminant.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: precise theorem 2, quadratic demonstration 2.
6. **Task:** Use the Binomial Theorem to expand $(x-2)^5$ and extract the $x^3$ coefficient.
   - **Expected answer:** $x^5-10x^4+40x^3-80x^2+80x-32$; requested coefficient $40=\binom52(-2)^2$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: expansion 2, justified coefficient 2.

**Instructional response:** A secure response earns at least 80% of core cumulative points and at least 3/4 on every concept check. Re-teach any missed objective using its explanation and a new numerical instance before reassessment; do not allow a high total to conceal an unassessed objective.
