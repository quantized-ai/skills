# Standards: Lesson 6.4: Finding and solving higher-degree factors

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 6.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping classifications retain the scope of each objective; “+” identifies additional mathematics and does not by itself make an objective optional.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The Rational Root Theorem as a search method** | List all possible rational roots of an integer-coefficient polynomial in reduced form, first extracting zero roots when necessary. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (supporting method); TEKS 2A.7(D) (supporting method). |
| **The Rational Root Theorem as a search method** | Test candidates and distinguish failure to find rational roots from absence of irrational or nonreal roots. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct for evaluating factors); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct for cubic/quartic factors). |
| **Solving cubic and quartic equations by degree reduction** | Solve cubic and quartic equations reducible to linear and quadratic factors using known or discovered factors, division, and quadratic structure or methods. | CCSS HSA-APR.B.2 (direct); CCSS HSA-APR.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct). |
| **Solving cubic and quartic equations by degree reduction** | Verify the complete factorization and account for every root in the requested number system, including complex and repeated roots. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.7 (direct for quadratic factors); CCSS HSN-CN.C.9 (+) (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(D) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct). |
