# Lesson 9.5: Square-root and cube-root functions

Graph square-root and cube-root transformations from exact points and explain their domains, ranges, direction, and symmetry.

## Learning objectives

- Graph transformed square-root and cube-root functions using exact parent-point mappings.
- Determine domains, ranges, endpoints or centers, and increasing or decreasing behavior from the transformation parameters.

## Learning outcome

Construct each root graph and justify its features from the root index and transformation parameters.

## Proficiency criteria

- Map parent coordinates through inside and outside shifts, nonzero scales, and reflections.
- Derive a square-root domain from its radicand inequality and its range from the sign of the outside multiplier.
- Identify the square-root endpoint and the cube-root center of symmetry.
- Justify the all-real domain and range of a nondegenerate transformed cube root and determine whether it increases or decreases.

## Concepts

| Concept Title | Content | Learning Objectives | Proficiency criteria |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Square-root graphs and transformations** | For real $h,k$ and nonzero real $a,b$, $f(x)=a\sqrt{b(x-h)}+k$ has endpoint $(h,k)$. Its domain is $b(x-h)\ge0$: $x\ge h$ for $b>0$ and $x\le h$ for $b<0$. Its range is $y\ge k$ for $a>0$ and $y\le k$ for $a<0$. Parent points $(u,v)$ on $v=\sqrt u$ map to $(h+u/b,k+av)$. The function increases when $ab>0$ and decreases when $ab<0$. | Graph a nondegenerate transformed square-root function using its endpoint, exact mapped points, and parameter-controlled direction. Determine its domain and range, including inside and outside reflections. | Locate the endpoint by setting the radicand to zero. Map exact parent points using both inside and outside parameters. Derive domain and range from the radicand condition and principal-root sign. Determine direction from the parameter signs. |
| **Cube-root graphs and transformations** | For real $h,k$ and nonzero real $a,b$, $f(x)=a\sqrt[3]{b(x-h)}+k$ has domain and range $\mathbb R$ and central symmetry about $(h,k)$. Parent points $(u,v)$ on $v=\sqrt[3]u$ map to $(h+u/b,k+av)$. The function increases when $ab>0$ and decreases when $ab<0$. Odd roots permit every real radicand; nonzero scales preserve the full real range. | Graph a nondegenerate transformed cube-root function using exact mapped points. Explain its domain, range, central symmetry, and increasing or decreasing behavior. | Map exact parent points through shifts, scales, and reflections. Justify the all-real domain and range using the odd root and nonzero scales. Identify the center of symmetry and use both multiplier signs to determine direction. |
