# Lesson 9.5 standards: Square-root and cube-root functions

CCSS: Common Core mathematics standards. TEKS: Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square-root graphs and transformations | Graph a nondegenerate transformed square-root function using its endpoint, exact mapped points, and parameter-controlled direction. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7b (direct); CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(C) (direct). |
| Square-root graphs and transformations | Determine its domain and range, including inside and outside reflections. | TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct); TEKS 2A.4(C) (direct). |
| Cube-root graphs and transformations | Graph a nondegenerate transformed cube-root function using exact mapped points. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7b (direct); TEKS 2A.6(A) (direct). |
| Cube-root graphs and transformations | Explain its domain, range, central symmetry, and increasing or decreasing behavior. | TEKS 2A.2(A) (direct); TEKS 2A.7(I) (direct). |
