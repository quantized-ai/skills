## Unit 9 cumulative assessment

Complete independently. Each item is worth four points; award one point for each stated scoring element. Total: 20 points. Report concept-assessment results alongside this sample, since these cumulative items do not replace assessment of every objective.

1. Simplify $\sqrt{32x^2}$ and evaluate your result at $x=-3$.

   **Answer and scoring:** $\sqrt{16\cdot2\cdot x^2}$ (1); $4|x|\sqrt2$ (1); $12\sqrt2$ at $-3$ (1); explanation that a principal root is nonnegative (1).

2. Evaluate $(-8)^{2/3}$ and simplify $x^{5/4}/x^{1/2}$ for $x>0$.

   **Answer and scoring:** Cube root $-2$ (1); square gives 4 (1); subtract exponents to get $x^{3/4}$ (1); positive-base hypothesis justifies the law (1).

3. Rationalize $1/(\sqrt7+2)$ and state the identity used.

   **Answer and scoring:** Multiply by $(\sqrt7-2)/(\sqrt7-2)$ (1); denominator $7-4=3$ (1); result $(\sqrt7-2)/3$ (1); difference of squares named or shown (1).

4. Graph $2-\sqrt{4-x}$ and give its domain and range.

   **Answer and scoring:** Endpoint $(4,2)$ (1); points $(3,1),(0,0)$ (1); domain $(-\infty,4]$ (1); range $(-\infty,2]$ (1).

5. Solve $\sqrt{x+12}=x$ and $x^{2/3}=25$.

   **Answer and scoring:** First equation gives candidates 4 and $-3$ (1); only 4 checks, with rejection explained (1); second uses cube-root values $\pm5$ (1); both $x=\pm125$ verified (1).
