# Lesson 9.7 standards: Rational-power equations and root formulas

CCSS: Common Core mathematics standards. TEKS: Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Equations with rational powers | Solve real rational-power equations, including negative exponents, with their original domain restrictions. | TEKS 2A.7(H) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| Equations with rational powers | Choose root substitutions or sign cases that preserve all allowed solutions and verify them in the original equation. | TEKS 2A.7(H) (direct); CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| Formulating square-root equations from tables | Construct a square-root function in the specified family from an endpoint and a distinct compatible point, verify the remaining data with technology, and explain the limits of a finite-data fit. | TEKS 2A.4(E) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (direct). |
| Formulating square-root equations from tables | Solve target-output equations and verify solutions against the original model, its range and domain, and contextual input restrictions. | TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (direct). |
