# Lesson 9.3 standards: Simplification and radical arithmetic

CCSS: Common Core mathematics standards. TEKS: Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extracting perfect powers | Simplify numerical and variable radicals by extracting perfect powers with correct absolute values. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (direct). |
| Extracting perfect powers | Determine when splitting or combining radical products preserves real values and the full original domain. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (direct). |
| Adding, subtracting, and multiplying radicals | Combine like radical terms after simplification and distinguish valid combination from invalid root-of-a-sum rules. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (direct). |
| Adding, subtracting, and multiplying radicals | Multiply radical expressions by distribution and simplify products under valid real-domain conditions. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (direct). |
