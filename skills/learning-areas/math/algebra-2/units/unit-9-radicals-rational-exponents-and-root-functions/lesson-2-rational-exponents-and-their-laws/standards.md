# Lesson 9.2 standards: Rational exponents and their laws

CCSS: Common Core mathematics standards. TEKS: Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Rational exponents as roots | Convert between radical and rational-exponent notation and evaluate values using the reduced-exponent convention. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct). |
| Rational exponents as roots | Determine real-domain restrictions for positive, zero, and negative bases, and identify invalid radical rewritings. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (supporting knowledge). |
| Exponent laws with stated hypotheses | Simplify rational-exponent expressions using exponent laws with valid base conditions. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct). |
| Exponent laws with stated hypotheses | Identify and correct exponent-law steps that change a real value, sign, or original domain. | CCSS HSN-RN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(G) (direct). |
