# Lesson 9.6 standards: Radical equations

CCSS: Common Core mathematics standards. TEKS: Texas standards. Mapping classifications are retained for each objective.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Square-root equations and extraneous candidates | Solve square-root equations by isolation and squaring with the required real-domain and sign conditions. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| Square-root equations and extraneous candidates | Verify candidates in the original equation and explain why any extraneous candidates are rejected. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(G) (direct). |
| Two radicals and cube-root equations | Solve simple equations containing two square roots using staged isolation, complete expansion, and original-equation checks. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct); TEKS 2A.4(G) (direct). |
| Two radicals and cube-root equations | Solve cube-root equations and explain the equivalence preserved by cubing compared with squaring. | TEKS 2A.6(B) (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.A.2 (direct). |
