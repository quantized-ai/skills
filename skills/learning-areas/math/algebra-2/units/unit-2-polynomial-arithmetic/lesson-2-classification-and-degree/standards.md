# Standards: Lesson 2.2: Classification and degree

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 2.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monomials, binomials, and trinomials** | Classify a simplified polynomial by its number of nonzero terms, using the stated zero-polynomial convention. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Monomials, binomials, and trinomials** | Expand factored expressions and combine like terms before determining term count. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Degree, leading term, and leading coefficient** | Determine term and polynomial degree after simplification, including total degree in several variables and the constant and zero cases. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Degree, leading term, and leading coefficient** | Identify the leading term and signed leading coefficient of a nonzero one-variable polynomial. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSF-IF.C.7c (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.7(B) (supporting knowledge). |
