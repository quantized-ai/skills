[Unit overview](unit.md)

## Cumulative unit assessment

Administer after the concept checks, without worked examples visible. Require intermediate reasoning, not only final answers. For sketches, accept hand-drawn or technology-produced graphs only when the requested features are justified. Optional extension points are reported separately from core scores.

1. **Task:** Classify $2x^4-3x^2+1$, $x^{-1}+4$, and $\sqrt2x$; give degree and leading coefficient where applicable.
   - **Expected answer:** Trinomial, degree $4$, leading coefficient $2$; not polynomial; monomial, degree $1$, leading coefficient $\sqrt2$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: classification 2, degree/coefficient 2.
2. **Task:** Simplify $(3x^3-x+2)-(x^3+4x-5)+(2x-1)$.
   - **Expected answer:** $2x^3-3x+6$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: negative distribution 2, collection 2.
3. **Task:** Expand $(x^2-2x+1)(x+3)$ and check at $x=1$.
   - **Expected answer:** $x^3+x^2-5x+3$; both forms give $0$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: partial products 2, collection 1, check 1.
4. **Task:** Prove $(a+b)^2-(a-b)^2=4ab$ and use it to compute $51^2-49^2$.
   - **Expected answer:** Expansion leaves $4ab$; $a=50,b=1$ gives $200$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: proof 3, numerical use 1.
5. **Task:** Explain why polynomial sums and products are polynomials and why division is different; give a division counterexample.
   - **Expected answer:** Addition collects permitted powers and multiplication adds nonnegative exponents. $1/x$ is not a polynomial, so division is not closed.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: addition 1, multiplication 1, division counterexample and reason 2.

**Instructional response:** A secure response earns at least 80% of core cumulative points and at least 3/4 on every concept check. Re-teach any missed objective using its explanation and a new numerical instance before reassessment; do not allow a high total to conceal an unassessed objective.
