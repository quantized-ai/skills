# Standards: Lesson 2.7: Polynomial identities and equivalence

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 2.7](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Proving and disproving polynomial identities** | Distinguish identities from equations with restricted solution sets and prove polynomial identities by symbolic transformation or coefficient comparison. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct). |
| **Proving and disproving polynomial identities** | Disprove false identities with counterexamples or unequal coefficients and explain why sample agreement alone is insufficient. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (supporting knowledge). |
| **Identities and numerical relationships** | Prove the Pythagorean-triple polynomial identity by expansion and explain its mixed-term combination. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct). |
| **Identities and numerical relationships** | Use the identity with integers $u>v>0$ to generate and verify a positive integer triple and determine whether it is primitive. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct). |
