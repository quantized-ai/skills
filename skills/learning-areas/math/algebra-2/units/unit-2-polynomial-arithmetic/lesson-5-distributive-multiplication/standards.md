# Standards: Lesson 2.5: Distributive multiplication

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 2.5](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial** | Multiply monomials using nonnegative integer exponent laws, keeping coefficient and exponent operations distinct. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Multiplying monomials and distributing a monomial** | Distribute a monomial across a polynomial, collect the product in standard form, and check its degree and leading coefficient when nonzero. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Multiplying binomials and general polynomials** | Multiply binomials and polynomial factors with three or more terms through complete distribution. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Multiplying binomials and general polynomials** | Justify multiplication and closure through distribution, and use degree and leading-coefficient checks with their stated limitations. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
