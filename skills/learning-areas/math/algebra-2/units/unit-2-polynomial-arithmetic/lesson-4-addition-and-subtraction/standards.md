# Standards: Lesson 2.4: Addition and subtraction

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 2.4](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Adding polynomials and closure** | Add polynomials, including degree greater than two, by collecting like terms. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Adding polynomials and closure** | Explain closure under addition and distinguish degree reduction from complete cancellation. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Subtracting polynomials and additive inverses** | Subtract polynomials by negating every term of the subtrahend and collecting like terms. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Subtracting polynomials and additive inverses** | Explain subtraction closure and degree changes from cancellation; use complete additive inverses and reversed differences to diagnose sign errors. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
