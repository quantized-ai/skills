# Standards: Lesson 2.6: Special products

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 2.6](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Squares of binomials** | Expand both binomial-square forms, including polynomial or composite components. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Squares of binomials** | Derive the square identities by distribution and use the cross terms to explain and correct missing or incorrectly signed middle terms. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Products of conjugate binomials** | Expand products of conjugate binomials, including composite terms, and justify cross-term cancellation. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
| **Products of conjugate binomials** | Use the difference-of-squares identity to compute and justify exact numerical products. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(B) (direct). |
