# Standards: Lesson 5.5: Three-point construction

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constructing a quadratic through three specified points | Form coefficient equations from three specified points and solve them by elimination to obtain the interpolating polynomial. | TEKS 2A.4(A) (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| Constructing a quadratic through three specified points | Verify all three points, determine whether $a\ne0$, and distinguish exact interpolation from regression. | TEKS 2A.4(A) (direct). |
| Uniqueness and degenerate three-point data | Determine whether three supplied points specify a unique quadratic, a lower-degree polynomial, insufficient conditions, or no function. | TEKS 2A.4(A) (direct, boundary cases). |
| Uniqueness and degenerate three-point data | Justify uniqueness for distinct inputs and diagnose redundant or inconsistent point conditions. | TEKS 2A.4(A) (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSF-IF.A.1 (prerequisite/review). |
