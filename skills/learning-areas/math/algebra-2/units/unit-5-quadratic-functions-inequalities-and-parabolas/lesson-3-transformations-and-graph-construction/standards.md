# Standards: Lesson 5.3: Transformations and graph construction

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transformations of a quadratic parent function | Describe and apply coordinate transformations from $y=x^2$ to $y=a(x-h)^2+k$. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(D) (supporting knowledge). |
| Transformations of a quadratic parent function | Analyze inside and outside scaling, including when different transformations of the quadratic parent produce the same graph. | CCSS HSF-BF.B.3 (direct). |
| Sketching from verified attributes | Construct an accurate labeled sketch from the vertex, axis, opening, intercepts, and symmetric points. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7a (direct). |
| Sketching from verified attributes | Describe increasing and decreasing intervals from the vertex and opening, and distinguish them from output-sign intervals. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.7a (supporting knowledge); CCSS HSF-IF.B.4 (supporting knowledge; contextual interpretation later). |
