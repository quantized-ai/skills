# Standards: Lesson 5.4: Constructing quadratics from attributes

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A vertex and an additional point | Construct a quadratic function from its vertex and an additional point that determines a nonzero scale. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.4(D) (supporting knowledge for construction). |
| A vertex and an additional point | Verify the supplied attributes and distinguish sufficient, redundant, contradictory, and constant-rule data. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.8a (direct). |
| Real zeros and an additional point | Construct a quadratic from two specified real roots, counting multiplicity, and a nonroot point with nonzero output. | CCSS HSA-SSE.B.3a (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (supporting knowledge). |
| Real zeros and an additional point | Verify a constructed rule and distinguish sufficient, redundant, or contradictory conditions when zeros and an additional point are supplied. | CCSS HSF-IF.C.8a (direct). |
