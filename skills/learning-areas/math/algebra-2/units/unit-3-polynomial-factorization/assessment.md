# Unit 3 assessment

[Unit overview](unit.md)

## Cumulative unit assessment

Administer after the concept checks, without worked examples visible. Require intermediate reasoning, not only final answers. For sketches, accept hand-drawn or technology-produced graphs only when the requested features are justified. Optional extension points are reported separately from core scores.

1. **Task:** Factor $6x^4-24x^2$ completely over the rationals.
   - **Expected answer:** $6x^2(x-2)(x+2)$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: GCF 2, continued factoring 2.
2. **Task:** Factor $2x^3+3x^2-8x-12$.
   - **Expected answer:** $(2x+3)(x-2)(x+2)$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: grouping 2, square difference 2.
3. **Task:** Factor $8x^3+125$ and multiply to verify.
   - **Expected answer:** $(2x+5)(4x^2-10x+25)$; mixed terms cancel.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: factors 2, verification 2.
4. **Task:** Factor $x^4-13x^2+36$ and solve the corresponding zero equation.
   - **Expected answer:** $(x-2)(x+2)(x-3)(x+3)$; zeros $\pm2,\pm3$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: substitution 1, factors 2, zeros 1.
5. **Task:** A student solves $x^3=9x$ by dividing by $x$ and reports $\pm3$. Correct the work and interpret graphically.
   - **Expected answer:** Move left and factor $x(x-3)(x+3)=0$; include $0$ as well as $\pm3$. These are the three horizontal intercept inputs.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: diagnosis 1, complete solving 2, graph connection 1.

**Instructional response:** A secure response earns at least 80% of core cumulative points and at least 3/4 on every concept check. Re-teach any missed objective using its explanation and a new numerical instance before reassessment; do not allow a high total to conceal an unassessed objective.
