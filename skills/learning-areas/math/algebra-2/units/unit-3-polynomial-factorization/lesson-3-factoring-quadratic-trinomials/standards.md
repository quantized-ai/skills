# Standards: Lesson 3.3 — Factoring quadratic trinomials

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [this lesson](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping qualifiers retain the scope of each objective’s alignment.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Monic quadratic trinomials** | Factor a monic integer-coefficient quadratic by matching both the sum and product of the constant parts of its linear factors, and verify the product. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct, prerequisite/review); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Monic quadratic trinomials** | Use signs and an exhaustive integer factor-pair search to justify whether rational linear factors exist, without inferring that real roots cannot exist. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct, prerequisite/review). |
| **Nonmonic quadratic trinomials** | Extract a common factor and factor a nonmonic integer-coefficient quadratic by splitting its middle term and grouping. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct, prerequisite/review); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Nonmonic quadratic trinomials** | Verify all coefficients of the selected factors and explain what an exhaustive middle-term-split search establishes about rational factorization. | CCSS HSA-APR.A.1 (prerequisite/review); CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct). |
