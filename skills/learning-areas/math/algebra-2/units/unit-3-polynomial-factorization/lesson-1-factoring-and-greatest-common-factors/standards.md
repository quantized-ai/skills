# Standards: Lesson 3.1 — Factoring and greatest common factors

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [this lesson](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping qualifiers retain the scope of each objective’s alignment.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Factoring as reversing distribution** | Recognize and verify a factorization as a product equal to the original polynomial, distinguishing expression rewriting from equation solving. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Factoring as reversing distribution** | Judge whether a factorization is complete in the stated coefficient set and distinguish rational from real irreducibility. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Extracting a greatest common monomial factor** | Determine the greatest common monomial factor of several integer-coefficient terms. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Extracting a greatest common monomial factor** | Extract a positive or negative common factor, verify every quotient term by distribution, and inspect the remainder for further factoring. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
