# Standards: Lesson 3.5 — Cube structures

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [this lesson](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping qualifiers retain the scope of each objective’s alignment.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Differences of cubes** | Extract any common factor and factor a difference of cubes using the complete cube-root expressions and correct quadratic coefficients. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Differences of cubes** | Justify the difference-of-cubes identity by multiplication and cancellation, distinguishing its quadratic factor from a binomial square. | CCSS HSA-APR.C.4 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct). |
| **Sums of cubes** | Extract common factors, factor sums of cubes with the correct signs, and verify the complete product. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Sums of cubes** | Explain why the sum-of-cubes identity does not apply to a sum of squares, without claiming every sum of polynomial squares is irreducible. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
