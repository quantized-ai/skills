# Standards: Lesson 3.4 — Square structures

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [this lesson](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping qualifiers retain the scope of each objective’s alignment.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Factoring differences of squares** | Recognize, factor, and verify differences of squares, including quartic expressions, without applying the identity to a sum. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Factoring differences of squares** | Continue factoring resulting factors and justify completeness over the stated coefficient set. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Perfect-square trinomials** | Recognize and factor perfect-square trinomials, verify the product, and inspect the binomial base for further factoring. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree four). |
| **Perfect-square trinomials** | Distinguish a perfect square from a trinomial with square endpoints by checking the signed doubled cross term. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct). |
