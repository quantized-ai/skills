# Standards: Lesson 3.2 — Common binomial factors and grouping

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [this lesson](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. Mapping qualifiers retain the scope of each objective’s alignment.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Factoring a repeated polynomial expression** | Factor an expression containing a repeated polynomial factor and verify the complete product. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Factoring a repeated polynomial expression** | Rewrite opposite binomials using a negative multiplier before factoring. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Factoring by grouping** | Factor suitable cubic and quartic expressions by grouping, complete any further factorization, and verify the product. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
| **Factoring by grouping** | Choose groupings and signs that produce an identical common factor and explain why a failed grouping does not prove irreducibility. | CCSS HSA-SSE.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(E) (direct for degree three or four). |
