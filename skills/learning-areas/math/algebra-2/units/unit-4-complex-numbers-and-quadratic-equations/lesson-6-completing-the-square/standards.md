# Standards for Lesson 4.6: Completing the square

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Completing the square when the leading coefficient is one** | Transform $x^2+bx=c$ into an equivalent perfect-square equation and justify the equal additions. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Completing the square when the leading coefficient is one** | Solve the completed-square equation for all exact real or complex roots, identifying any repeated root. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Completing the square with a nonunit leading coefficient** | Complete the square in a nonmonic quadratic by dividing by the nonzero leading coefficient or factoring it from the quadratic and linear terms, preserving the solution set. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4a (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Completing the square with a nonunit leading coefficient** | Solve the resulting equation exactly and explain the outside scaling of an adjustment made inside a factored bracket. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
