[Unit overview](unit.md)

## Cumulative unit assessment

Administer after the concept checks, without worked examples visible. Require intermediate reasoning, not only final answers. For sketches, accept hand-drawn or technology-produced graphs only when the requested features are justified. Optional extension points are reported separately from core scores.

1. **Task:** Simplify $i^{27}+(2-3i)(1+4i)$.
   - **Expected answer:** $-i+14+5i=14+4i$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: power 1, multiplication 2, standard form 1.
2. **Task:** Factor $x^2+36$ over the complex numbers and multiply the factors.
   - **Expected answer:** $(x-6i)(x+6i)$; product $x^2-36i^2=x^2+36$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: factors 2, verification 2.
3. **Task:** Solve $3x^2-12x+2=0$ by completing the square.
   - **Expected answer:** $(x-2)^2=10/3$, so $x=2\pm\sqrt{30}/3$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: normalization 1, completion 1, exact roots 2.
4. **Task:** Derive the quadratic formula and solve $2x^2+4x+5=0$.
   - **Expected answer:** Square completion yields the formula; $D=-24$, roots $-1\pm i\sqrt6/2$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: derivation 2, roots 2.
5. **Task:** Explain the root counts for $x^2-4x+4$, $x^2-4x+5$, and $x^2-4x+3$.
   - **Expected answer:** Discriminants $0,-4,4$: one repeated real root; two nonreal conjugate roots; two distinct real roots.
   - **Scoring:** 4 points: discriminants 2, precise classifications 2.
6. **Task:** Optional extension: compute $(1+2i)/(3-i)$ and describe conjugation of $1+2i$ on the complex plane.
   - **Expected answer:** Quotient $(1+7i)/10$; conjugation reflects $(1,2)$ to $(1,-2)$.
   - **Scoring:** 4 extension points: quotient 3, geometric description 1; exclude from core mastery denominator.

**Instructional response:** A secure response earns at least 80% of core cumulative points and at least 3/4 on every concept check. Re-teach any missed objective using its explanation and a new numerical instance before reassessment; do not allow a high total to conceal an unassessed objective.
