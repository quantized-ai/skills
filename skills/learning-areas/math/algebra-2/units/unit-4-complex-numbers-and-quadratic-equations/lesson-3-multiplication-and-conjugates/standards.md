# Standards for Lesson 4.3: Multiplication and conjugates

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Multiplying complex numbers** | Multiply complex numbers using distribution and $i^2=-1$, expressing the result in standard form. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct). |
| **Multiplying complex numbers** | Explain the sign change and real contribution from the product of imaginary terms. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct). |
| **Conjugate products and complex factorization** | Identify a conjugate, compute its product with the original number, and explain the cancellation of imaginary terms. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); CCSS HSN-CN.A.3 (+) (partial direct: conjugates); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct for multiplication). |
| **Conjugate products and complex factorization** | Use a difference-of-squares identity to factor a real sum of squares over the complex numbers and distinguish nonreal zeros from real graph intercepts, including the zero-constant case. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.8 (+) (direct; included in Common Core Appendix A Algebra II). |
