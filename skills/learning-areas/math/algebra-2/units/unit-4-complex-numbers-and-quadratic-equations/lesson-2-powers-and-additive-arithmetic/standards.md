# Standards for Lesson 4.2: Powers and additive arithmetic

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nonnegative integer powers of $i$** | Simplify nonnegative integer powers of $i$ using their four-term cycle. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.1 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (supporting knowledge). |
| **Nonnegative integer powers of $i$** | Justify exponent reduction from $i^4=1$ and simplify products involving powers and numerical coefficients. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct). |
| **Adding and subtracting complex numbers** | Add complex numbers and write the result in standard form. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct). |
| **Adding and subtracting complex numbers** | Subtract complex numbers and explain componentwise combination and the role of the additive inverse. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.2 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct). |
