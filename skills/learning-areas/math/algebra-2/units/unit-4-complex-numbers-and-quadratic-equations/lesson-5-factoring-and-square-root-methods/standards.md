# Standards for Lesson 4.5: Factoring and square-root methods

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Selecting and using a factoring method** | Solve a quadratic equation by factoring after moving all terms to one side, identifying every distinct root and any repeated root. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Selecting and using a factoring method** | Justify when factoring is efficient and verify a root in the original equation. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **The square-root property** | Isolate a squared linear expression and solve $(mx+n)^2=k$, with real $m,n,k$ and $m\ne0$, using every square-root branch. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **The square-root property** | Classify roots from the sign of $k$ and express them exactly, including negative, zero, and nonsquare positive cases. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); CCSS HSN-CN.C.7 (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
