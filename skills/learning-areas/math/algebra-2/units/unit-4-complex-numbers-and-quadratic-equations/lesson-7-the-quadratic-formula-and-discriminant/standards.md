# Standards for Lesson 4.7: The quadratic formula and discriminant

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Deriving and applying the quadratic formula** | Derive the quadratic formula by completing the square for any nonzero real leading coefficient. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4a (direct). |
| **Deriving and applying the quadratic formula** | Apply the formula with signed coefficients to obtain all exact roots, including repeated and nonreal cases. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **The discriminant and root classification** | Compute the discriminant and predict the number and type of roots of a real-coefficient quadratic, including multiplicity. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); CCSS HSN-CN.C.7 (supporting knowledge); TEKS 2A.4(F) (supporting knowledge). |
| **The discriminant and root classification** | Distinguish root-count conclusions from rationality conclusions and justify the rational-coefficient and rational-square conditions. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (supporting knowledge). |
