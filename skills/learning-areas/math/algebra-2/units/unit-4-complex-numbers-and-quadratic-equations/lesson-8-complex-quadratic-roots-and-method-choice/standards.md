# Standards for Lesson 4.8: Complex quadratic roots and method choice

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Solving quadratics with complex roots** | Solve real-coefficient quadratics with negative discriminant in simplified standard complex form, distinguishing their zeros from real graph intercepts. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.7 (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Solving quadratics with complex roots** | Check a complex root by substitution and explain why its conjugate also appears for real coefficients. | CCSS HSN-CN.C.7 (direct); TEKS 2A.7(A) (direct for checking arithmetic). |
| **Choosing and comparing quadratic methods** | Select and use an efficient exact method from the equation structure, justify the choice, and preserve all solutions. | CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct); TEKS 2A.4(F) (direct). |
| **Choosing and comparing quadratic methods** | Solve a quadratic by two valid methods and reconcile their equivalent root expressions. | CCSS HSA-REI.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSA-REI.B.4b (direct). |
