# Standards for Lesson 4.4: Optional complex-number extensions

Optional extension mappings; completion is not required for core-unit mastery.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Dividing complex numbers — optional extension** | Divide by a nonzero complex number using its conjugate and justify the equivalent quotient in standard form. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.3 (+) (direct, optional extension beyond Common Core Appendix A Algebra II allocation). |
| **Dividing complex numbers — optional extension** | Compute modulus and use $z\overline z=\lvert z\rvert^2$ to explain the positive denominator for a nonzero divisor. | CCSS HSN-CN.A.3 (+) (direct, optional extension). |
| **The rectangular complex plane — optional extension** | Represent $a+bi$ by rectangular coordinates $(a,b)$ and recover a complex number from a plotted point. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.4 (+) (partial direct: rectangular form only; optional extension). |
| **The rectangular complex plane — optional extension** | Interpret complex addition as coordinate displacement and conjugation as reflection across the real axis. | CCSS HSN-CN.B.5 (+) (partial direct: addition and conjugation; optional extension). |
