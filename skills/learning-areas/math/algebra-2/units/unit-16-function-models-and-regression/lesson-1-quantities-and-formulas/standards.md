# Standards: Lesson 16.1: Quantities and formulas

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 16.1](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| **Variable definitions, units, and scales** | Choose reporting precision consistent with the data and context while retaining calculation precision. | CCSS HSN-Q.A.3 (direct). |
