# Standards: Lesson 16.6: Quadratic and exponential regression

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 16.6](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Quadratic fitting** | Use technology to fit and verify a quadratic-family equation from paired data, distinguishing exact interpolation from approximate least-squares fitting. | TEKS 2A.8(B) (direct); TEKS 2A.4(E) (direct). |
| **Quadratic fitting** | Interpret and predict from fitted quadratic features on a justified domain, recognizing degenerate fits and distinguishing model extrema from observations. | TEKS 2A.8(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.B.4 (direct). |
| **Exponential fitting and fitting scale** | Use technology to fit an exponential model, document its fitting scale, and apply and back-transform log-response regression only when its conditions hold. | TEKS 2A.8(B) (direct); CCSS HSS-ID.B.6a (direct). |
| **Exponential fitting and fitting scale** | Interpret exponential parameters and evaluate original-scale predictions and residuals without confusing original-scale and log-scale error minimization. | TEKS 2A.8(C) (direct); CCSS HSF-LE.B.5 (direct). |
