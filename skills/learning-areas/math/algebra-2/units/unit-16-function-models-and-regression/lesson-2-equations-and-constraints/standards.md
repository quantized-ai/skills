# Standards: Lesson 16.2: Equations and constraints

Mappings for the concepts and learning objectives in [Lesson 16.2](lesson.md).

**CCSS:** Common Core mathematics standards. **TEKS:** Texas standards. **Direct:** the objective addresses the standard. **Supporting knowledge:** the objective provides a foundation for the standard.

| Concept | Learning objectives | Standards |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Equations from quantitative relationships** | Create equations in one or more defined variables from quantitative descriptions and graph two-variable relationships with labeled axes and scales. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSA-CED.A.2 (direct). |
| **Equations from quantitative relationships** | Solve a constructed model, verify candidates, and interpret solutions using consistent units and a meaningful domain. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.1 (direct); CCSS HSF-IF.B.5 (direct). |
| **Feasible sets and inequality constraints** | Represent simultaneous contextual constraints by equations, inequalities, and required nonnegative or integer domains. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(E) (direct). |
| **Feasible sets and inequality constraints** | Test candidate solutions against every constraint and distinguish algebraic solutions from contextually feasible solutions. | CCSS HSA-CED.A.3 (direct); TEKS 2A.3(G) (direct). |
