# Unit 16: Function models and regression

[Algebra 2](../../curriculum.md)

Function models combine algebraic structure with measured quantities and data. This unit synthesizes formulas, constraints, family selection, function combinations, and regression so that a fitted equation remains connected to its units, domain, and intended use. Learners construct exact relationships when the information warrants them and use approximate fits when observations vary. Comparison and revision are integral: a successful calculation must also support an appropriate model interpretation.

## Prerequisites

Function notation and transformations; linear, quadratic, exponential, radical, rational, and periodic families; solving equations and inequalities; logarithms; interval notation and graph interpretation. Learners should recognize a family’s characteristic behavior, evaluate it accurately, and preserve algebraic domain restrictions. Regression requires a calculator or spreadsheet capable of numerical fitting and tables, but no particular software product is assumed.

## Main learning objectives

- Define quantities and construct or rearrange equations with consistent units and valid restrictions. [Lesson 16.1](lesson-1-quantities-and-formulas/lesson.md), [Lesson 16.2](lesson-2-equations-and-constraints/lesson.md).
- Represent feasible choices and compare model families using data, rates, and interval extrema. [Lesson 16.2](lesson-2-equations-and-constraints/lesson.md), [Lesson 16.3](lesson-3-function-family-selection/lesson.md).
- Combine functions arithmetically while preserving quantity meanings and component domains. [Lesson 16.4](lesson-4-arithmetic-combinations-of-models/lesson.md).
- Produce reproducible linear, quadratic, exponential, and square-root fits with correctly interpreted coefficients. [Lesson 16.5](lesson-5-regression-foundations-and-linear-fitting/lesson.md), [Lesson 16.6](lesson-6-quadratic-and-exponential-regression/lesson.md), [Lesson 16.7](lesson-7-square-root-fitting-and-residual-analysis/lesson.md).
- Evaluate residuals and predictions, then document a justified model selection or revision. [Lesson 16.7](lesson-7-square-root-fitting-and-residual-analysis/lesson.md), [Lesson 16.8](lesson-8-prediction-and-model-revision/lesson.md).

## Concepts and topics

The concepts cover quantity definitions, precision, and literal formulas; equations and feasible sets; equal-step patterns, average rates, and attained extrema; sums, differences, products, and quotients; residuals and least squares; family-specific regression and fitting scales; transformed square-root predictors; and interpolation, extrapolation, validation, and communication. Exact construction is distinguished from regression throughout.

## Lessons

- [16.1: Quantities and formulas](lesson-1-quantities-and-formulas/lesson.md): Define quantities and units, choose reporting precision, and rearrange formulas with explicit exceptional cases.
- [16.2: Equations and constraints](lesson-2-equations-and-constraints/lesson.md): Translate relationships and simultaneous constraints into models, then distinguish algebraic answers from feasible solutions.
- [16.3: Function-family selection](lesson-3-function-family-selection/lesson.md): Compare families through patterns, context, and rates, and analyze extrema on valid restricted intervals.
- [16.4: Arithmetic combinations of models](lesson-4-arithmetic-combinations-of-models/lesson.md): Build combined quantities from component functions and retain all mathematical and contextual restrictions.
- [16.5: Regression foundations and linear fitting](lesson-5-regression-foundations-and-linear-fitting/lesson.md): Prepare reproducible fitting data, analyze residuals, and obtain and interpret a verified linear regression.
- [16.6: Quadratic and exponential regression](lesson-6-quadratic-and-exponential-regression/lesson.md): Fit quadratic and exponential models while distinguishing interpolation, noisy fitting, and response-scale choices.
- [16.7: Square-root fitting and residual analysis](lesson-7-square-root-fitting-and-residual-analysis/lesson.md): Fit the stated square-root family and use residual plots to diagnose inadequate shape or changing error behavior.
- [16.8: Prediction and model revision](lesson-8-prediction-and-model-revision/lesson.md): Qualify predictions by their domains and assumptions and compare models using additional evidence and a documented report.

## Standards and scope

Common Core Appendix A supplies the Algebra II function-modeling emphasis, and the Common Core mathematics standards support quantities, equations, representations, combinations, and residual interpretation. Texas standards explicitly require linear, quadratic, and exponential regression and technology-based quadratic and square-root equations. Regression confidence intervals, multivariable regression, and numerical optimization theory are outside this unit’s required scope.
