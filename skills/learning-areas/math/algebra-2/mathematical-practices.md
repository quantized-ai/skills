# Mathematical practice evidence

[Curriculum](curriculum.md) · [Standards coverage](standards-coverage.md)

Source documents: Common Core mathematics standards and Texas standards.

The following are recurring instructional expectations. They identify actual evidence to collect, rather than asserting that every exercise measures every practice.

| Practice | Examples of observable evidence | Related Texas process |
|---|---|---|
| CCSS MP.1: make sense and persevere | Select a factoring or equation strategy, revise after a failed approach, and check the result against the original problem. | 2A.1(B) |
| CCSS MP.2: reason quantitatively | Define units and interpret a root, rate, parameter, or estimate in its quantitative setting. | 2A.1(A), 2A.1(F) |
| CCSS MP.3: construct and critique arguments | Explain why an algebraic move preserves solutions, identify an extraneous candidate, or critique an unsupported causal claim. | 2A.1(G) |
| CCSS MP.4: model with mathematics | Formulate a relationship, choose a function family, evaluate fit, and limit predictions to a justified domain. | 2A.1(A), 2A.1(B) |
| CCSS MP.5: use tools strategically | Compare hand algebra with a graph, use matrix technology, or select a reproducible simulation and explain its limitations. | 2A.1(C) |
| CCSS MP.6: attend to precision | Preserve excluded inputs, label axes/units, distinguish exact from approximate answers, and state uncertainty accurately. | 2A.1(D), 2A.1(G) |
| CCSS MP.7: use structure | Recognize quadratic form within higher powers, a common factor, or inverse relationships across families. | 2A.1(F) |
| CCSS MP.8: express regularity | Derive a finite geometric sum or explain stable behavior across repeated simulations or transformations. | 2A.1(E), 2A.1(F) |

Teachers can collect one annotated example per practice in a portfolio. A brief rubric awards one point for a correct representation, one for a justified method, and one for interpreting the result or its limitations. These portfolio indicators supplement the individually mapped content assessments; they do not replace them.
